---
id: SR-319
title: The study room plays a study's video
statement: The study room plays each committed clip of a study inline, at its place in the study's body,
  with play, pause and seek; a clip that is missing, unreadable or outside the project is replaced by
  a fallback line at its place and the rest of the study stays readable.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a study with a rendered clip between two paragraphs
  when: the owner opens it in the room
  then: the clip shows between those paragraphs with play, pause and seek
- id: EX-2
  given: a study whose clip file is missing or not a valid mp4
  when: it is opened
  then: a fallback line at the clip's place says the video is unavailable and the rest of the study is
    shown
- id: EX-3
  given: a study naming a video path outside the project
  when: it is opened
  then: nothing is loaded from that path; the fallback line is shown
proof:
  plan: 'After landing, in a live intent loop: a video study on ''How the judge cascade runs'' is proposed,
    the owner accepts it at depth video in the band; the owner sees its storyboard, the reviewer''s frame-check
    verdict, and the video playing in the room with subtitles on; every screenshot (PNG) is opened on
    the owner''s screen.'
