---
id: SR-199
title: Records that may silently depend on a changed record are shown as...
feature: FEAT-002
statement: When a confirmed record changes, a bounded scan proposes records with no link to it that may
  depend on what changed, each confirmed by the judge, and shows them to the agent as drift hints; a hint
  never marks a link stale, adds an edge or interrupts the owner. The scan's limits default to 5 candidates
  and 1 second, and the owner may raise them. A link to a whole test file gets the test node that checks
  an example proposed the same way, as a hint.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: confirmed SR-004 changes and SR-012 shares its key terms with no link
  when: the drift scan runs
  then: a hint says SR-012 may depend on the change to SR-004 and no link state changes
- id: EX-2
  given: 50 candidate records overlap the change and the limits are the defaults
  when: the scan runs
  then: at most 5 are judged and the scan ends within 1 second
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner has asked in plain words for a wider scan, and the candidate limit is now 10
  when: the scan runs on a change with 50 overlapping candidates
  then: at most 10 are judged
- id: EX-4
  given: no judge can be reached
  when: the scan runs
  then: iaso says judging is unavailable and shows no hint decided by rule
- id: EX-5
  given: a document under docs/ changes and no record changes
  when: the turn ends
  then: 'no scan runs: only records are the specification'
- id: EX-6
  given: SR-001 is verified_by tests/test_calc.py, a test file with test_add_returns_sum inside and no
    test node named
  when: the drift scan runs
  then: a hint proposes tests/test_calc.py::test_add_returns_sum for SR-001's first example and no link
    changes
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended for the owner to decide: the agent changes one confirmed record in a scratch repository
    where an unlinked record shares its terms; the agent''s reply reports the hint it received naming
    that record, and the iaso line shows no stale link and no question.'
  kind: hook
