id: SR-397
title: An independent evaluator's verified verdict confirms a proof
statement: 'When the owner picked the evaluator, or while the owner''s standing choice is on (DEC-464),
  iaso starts at the end of the build, for a requirement''s proof, a fresh agent session that took no
  part in the build. It is given the requirement, its examples, the stated proof and the built tree, and
  nothing of the builder''s conversation. It writes an evaluation case (the requests to send and the checks
  to run) and runs it with iaso evaluate''s parts: a scratch repository with the branch''s iaso set up,
  headless Claude Code turns, the scripted owner answering the questions raised, and the checks. It ends
  on one verdict: verified, not verified (with what was not shown) or could not check (with why). Verified
  is recorded as the proof''s evidence with a small report the evaluator leaves in the repository (its
  verdict, what it checked and what it saw), saved by its repository path; the run''s folder is not kept.
  Verified confirms the proof: done and the merge accept it as they accept the owner''s yes, and the owner
  is told the verdict and where the evidence is, not asked. Could not check: the owner is asked ''Did
  you see it working?'' for that proof only. The owner overrules a verdict by saying so, which opens the
  proof again. For a proof the evaluator was picked for, done refuses passing evidence that neither the
  evaluator''s verdict nor the owner''s yes confirmed. The verdict is logged by iaso''s evaluator run,
  never by the building agent, and counts only for the requirement''s words and evidence it was given
  on.'
