id: SR-333
title: Every question reaches the owner through the question tool first
statement: iaso puts each question to the owner through the question tool. When the question has more
  to see than fits in it (a diagram, long evidence, whole versions side by side), one of its options is
  to study it in the room; choosing that opens the study room on that question, and the question waits
  there. The owner can also ask for the room at any time. The room never opens by itself, shows one question
  at a time with Back and Next, grouped as waiting, being processed, blocked (with the reason) and done;
  an answered question leaves the waiting list at once, and one whose record changed after it was shown
  is blocked, not applied.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a question whose options fit in a few lines
  when: iaso puts it to the owner
  then: it comes through the question tool, with no option to study it in the room
- id: EX-2
  given: a question that needs a diagram
  when: iaso puts it to the owner
  then: it comes through the question tool with an option 'study it in the room'; the room does not open
    unless that option is chosen
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner chooses 'study it in the room'
  when: the answer is applied
  then: the room opens on that question with its diagram, and the question waits there until answered
- id: EX-4
  given: three questions wait in the room, the second carrying a diagram
  when: the owner opens the room and presses Next
  then: it shows the second question with its diagram above it and '2 of 3'
- id: EX-5
  given: the owner answers a question in the room
  when: the next prompt or turn end lists what waits
  then: that question is not among them
proof:
  plan: 'In a live Claude Code session on this repository, the owner sees: no card in iaso''s band and
    Hide staying hidden; every waiting question arriving through the question tool as soon as it is raised,
    grouped by principle, each offering to study it in the room when there is more to see; no question
    the owner already answered coming back; and no turn-end line counting questions (SR-361, SR-363).'
