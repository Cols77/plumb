---
id: SR-350
title: A reloaded panel takes back a silent slot
statement: When a new panel says hello for a session whose registered panel has sent no heartbeat for
  over a minute, iaso replaces the registration with the new panel's key; a hello while the registered
  panel is live is still refused, and a hello run by the agent is never accepted.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the session's panel last beat two minutes ago and a plugin reload starts a new panel
  when: the new panel says hello
  then: it is registered and the band shows again
- id: EX-2
  given: the session's panel beat ten seconds ago
  when: a second hello arrives
  then: 'it is refused: this session already has its panel'
proof:
  plan: reload plugins mid-session and see the band come back without a restart
  kind: live-session
  post_merge: true
