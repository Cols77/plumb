id: SR-217
title: iaso resolves from the recorded intent what it already answers...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'Before a merge conflict, untraced change, gap or contradiction between two approved records
  reaches the owner, the judge reads it against the approved records and decisions: what they settle is
  applied and logged with the record that settles it, reversible by the owner''s word. A proposed record
  only the agent wrote never settles anything and never answers for the owner; a decision whose choice
  is the owner''s own logged answer settles what it answers (SR-335, DEC-285). Only an intent choice or
  trade-off they leave open reaches the owner, as one question-tool question; with no judge the issue
  reaches the owner with no recommendation. Stale links are P1''s. A frozen part (SR-380) raises nothing.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a merge conflict on a requirement where an approved decision already chose one side's principle
  when: the conflict is found
  then: the side the decision implies is applied and logged with the decision; no question
- id: EX-2
  given: two confirmed records contradict each other and the recorded intent already says which holds
  when: the stale check runs
  then: the record the intent contradicts is reworded to agree with it, the rewording is judge-approved
    and logged with that intent, and the owner is not asked
- id: EX-3
  given: an issue the approved records do not settle, or two approved records that contradict with no
    recorded intent saying which holds
  when: the judge reads them
  then: it reaches the owner as one question-tool question at the level of the principle, saying in plain
    words what the recorded intent says on each side, with no record ids
- id: EX-4
  given: no judge can be reached
  when: an issue is found
  then: 'iaso says so once; the issue is neither resolved by rule nor withheld: it reaches the owner as
    one question-tool question with no recommendation, as a merge conflict does under P11'
- id: EX-5
  given: a resolution the owner disagrees with
  when: the owner says in conversation that it got that one wrong
  then: the resolution is reverted, the issue is back as it was, and the owner's words are captured as
    intent
