id: SR-223
title: The clarifier reads the owner's words in context and asks every...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'Inside the loop the clarifier reads the owner''s words with the agent''s last reply, the owner''s
  recent messages and repository facts, and acts only on a change to the system being built. A gap with
  a better-supported option becomes a labelled, flippable assumption; every gap whose answer changes what
  gets built is asked as soon as it arises, specific, with options and a recommendation, through the question
  tool, those that hinge on one principle asked once as that principle, with no limit per turn (SR-361).
  The question rules are written once, in the iaso-records skill, and iaso-clarify points to it (SR-379);
  the hooks remind the agent to ask, and outside the intent loop only an edit that contradicts or extends
  a confirmed record waits, until the owner answers or a loop reaches clear intent; every other edit goes
  ahead, and a waiting question never blocks an unrelated edit; inside the loop the same holds: only an
  edit that contradicts or extends a confirmed record waits, and an unrelated edit goes ahead while the
  loop asks its questions (owner, 2026-11-06: "Only related edits wait (Recommended)"); the loop itself
  does not build its planned work before its clarify gate passes, which is its own discipline (iaso intent
  gate), not an edit block. The owner never reads the interpretation itself. The owner''s answers are
  logged as their words and never asked again; nothing of the clarifier''s self-questioning is recorded.'
