id: SR-244
title: JEV's question form is a setting, and a calibration counts only...
feature: FEAT-002
statement: 'jev.output sets how JEV is asked the entailment question where it is still used (the probe):
  yes/no by default, or the three-way choice over supported, beyond and contradicts, whose probability
  of supported is used and logged with the form. Record approval asks SR-372''s contradiction question
  instead, and calibration scores that form only (form "contradiction"): a calibration measured with another
  form is not used for approval.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: jev.output is unset
  when: JEV is asked
  then: it is asked yes/no
- id: EX-2
  given: jev.output is choice
  when: JEV is asked
  then: it is asked the three-way choice over supported, beyond and contradicts, the probability of supported
    is used, and the log names the form
- id: EX-3
  given: a calibration measured with yes_no or choice
  when: JEV judges a record for approval
  then: the fast track runs on the 0.9 floor, not on that calibration's threshold, and JEV status says
    a calibration for the approval (contradiction) form is needed
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended: the owner says to switch JEV to the choice form and /iaso jev status then says a
    calibration for the choice form is needed. The owner decides whether this is the proof.'
  kind: command
