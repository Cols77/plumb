id: SR-412
title: The problems class holds the known problems
statement: When known problems are open the band shows a problems chip with how many are open, and nothing
  more of them in the band. The problems class of iaso's one pane lists each known problem, open ones
  first, as a row with its title and state; expanded, the row shows its symptom, cause, fix and case history.
  The class follows the register while the pane is open. When a known problem changes - its fix lands,
  it is proven, or it comes back in another session - the change shows in the pane and the band, and the
  session that owns its case is told at its next prompt, once.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: two known problems are open
  when: the band draws
  then: it shows the chip problems 2 in plum and no line per problem
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner presses the problems chip
  when: the pane opens
  then: 'the switch shows problems bold and each problem is one folded row: glyph, title, open or fixed'
- id: EX-3
  given: a problem's row is expanded
  when: the owner reads it
  then: its symptom, cause, fix and history are dim lines under the bold title; fixed problems are below
    the open ones, dim
- id: EX-4
  given: a problem's fix lands while the pane is open
  when: the register moves
  then: the row reads fixed and moves below the open ones
- id: EX-5
  given: a known problem was proven fixed and session s1 owns its case
  when: s1 sends its next prompt, and again after that
  then: s1 is told once; the next prompt and any other session are told nothing
proof:
  plan: 'After the landing and a plugin reload, with one known problem open: the band shows the problems
    chip; the pane''s problems class shows the row folded and expanded with symptom, cause, fix and history;
    screenshots as evidence'
  kind: live-session
