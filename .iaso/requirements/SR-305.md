---
id: SR-305
title: Pressing the band's step strip opens a pane showing the iaso loop
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'While this session''s intent loop is open, the band keeps the loop''s section: its title,
  the step strip (clarify, plan, build, proven, marking done, current and to come) and the task bar; the
  owner can fold the section to the title alone and unfold it, and it stays present until the loop ends.
  Each step of the strip can be pressed (a click, or the arrow keys and Enter as for Claude Code''s own
  workflow entry): it opens iaso''s one docked pane on the loops class with this session''s loop expanded
  and that step''s details shown: its gate passed, refused or to come, the low findings left, and for
  the current step the tasks done and left. The same step details are reached from any loop''s row in
  the loops class: an expanded loop row lists its steps, and pressing one shows that step''s details.
  The records a loop wrote and each requirement''s proof are read in the loop''s own window (SR-414).
  Esc closes the pane and the band stays; the pane stays open when a loop ends.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a loop whose clarify and plan gates passed with 2 low findings each and whose build has 3 of
    4 tasks done
  when: the owner presses 'build' in the band's step strip
  then: 'the pane opens on loops with this loop expanded at its build step: gate open, 3 of 4 tasks done
    with the task left named; clarify and plan read passed with 2 low findings each, proven to come'
- id: EX-2
  given: the pane is open on loops and another session's loop row is expanded
  when: the owner presses its 'plan' step
  then: that step's gate state and findings left are shown under the row; no records or proofs are listed
    in the pane
- id: EX-3
  given: the loop runs as a Claude Code workflow
  when: the pane is open
  then: it shows no agent names, counts or tokens until iaso knows them (DEC-511)
- id: EX-4
  given: the pane is open
  when: the owner presses Esc
  then: the pane closes and the band stays
- id: EX-5
  given: no intent loop is open in this session
  when: the owner looks at the band
  then: no loop section is drawn; the loops chip still opens the pane on every loop of the project
- id: EX-6
  given: the band's loop section is unfolded
  when: the owner folds it
  then: the band shows the loop's title alone on line two, no strip and no bar, until the owner unfolds
    it or the loop ends
- id: EX-7
  given: the pane is open on loops
  when: this session's loop ends with intent done
  then: the pane stays open; the loop leaves the live rows and the band's loop section is gone
proof:
  plan: 'After the landing and a plugin reload, in a live loop: the band''s loop section folds to the
    title and unfolds; pressing a step opens the one pane on loops with this loop expanded at that step''s
    details (gate, low findings left, tasks done and left); the same step details open from another loop''s
    row; Esc closes the pane and the band stays. The evaluator checks it from the session log and screenshots.'
