---
id: SR-402
title: A new debug work copy is refused when the disk is nearly full
statement: Before iaso makes a new debug work copy it reads the free space of the disk holding the project;
  below 5 GB it makes no copy, leaves the problem open, and prints the free space and the debug work copies
  that could go (those an idle-copy removal would take, largest first) with the command to remove them.
  Reusing an existing copy needs no space and is never refused.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: under 5 GB free and two idle debug work copies
  when: iaso is about to make a work copy for a new problem
  then: no copy is made, the problem stays open, and the line names the free space, the two idle copies
    with their sizes and iaso debug prune
  quote: a new debug worktree is refused below a free-space floor, naming which could go
  prompt: a4218e97aba44b36
- id: EX-2
  given: 5 GB or more free
  when: iaso is about to make a work copy
  then: the copy is made as before
- id: EX-3
  given: under 5 GB free and no idle copy
  when: iaso is about to make a work copy
  then: no copy is made, the problem stays open, and the line says nothing can be freed automatically
    and gives the free space
- id: EX-4
  given: under 5 GB free and a work copy that already exists for the problem
  when: iaso reuses it
  then: it is reused and not refused
proof:
  plan: in a scratch project with the free-space floor raised above the disk's free space, ask iaso to
    make a debug work copy and see it refused with the idle copies named; lower the floor and see it made
  kind: command
  evaluator: true
