---
id: SR-235
title: Capture turns the owner's words into linked records in one call
feature: FEAT-002
statement: One capture call keeps the owner's words with their context as a pre-intent and writes the
  agent's formal intent, requirement and decision records with pinned links, ids allocated without collision,
  all proposed until the judge cascade validates them; the quote must be verbatim in the logged prompt.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a capture document with the owner's quote and context, a formal intent, one requirement with
    two examples and one decision
  when: iaso capture runs
  then: one pre-intent, one INT, one SR with EX-1 and EX-2 and one DEC exist, each proposed, the SR and
    DEC derived from the INT and the INT from the pre-intent, and the output is text for the agent, not
    a card for the owner
- id: EX-2
  given: a capture document whose quote is not a verbatim span of the logged prompt
  when: iaso capture runs
  then: it exits 2 and writes nothing
- id: EX-3
  given: a document updating the examples of an approved SR
  when: iaso capture runs
  then: the SR becomes an unconfirmed edit and goes back through the judge cascade; no question reaches
    the owner while the judge finds the change keeps the older intent
- id: EX-4
  given: any capture document
  when: iaso capture --dry-run runs
  then: it prints what it would write and writes no file and no event
- id: EX-5
  given: SR-004 has EX-1 and EX-2
  when: the agent adds an example to SR-004
  then: SR-004 gains EX-3; an approved SR-004 becomes an unconfirmed edit, a proposed one stays proposed
- id: EX-6
  given: the judge cascade validates a captured interpretation
  when: the records are approved
  then: they are marked judge-approved and the trail from the pre-intent to each formal record is kept
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended for the owner to decide: in a live session the owner states a small new intent in
    plain words; the AI then shows the pre-intent with its context, the formal records derived from it,
    and the judge''s verdict marking them approved, all without the owner reading a card.'
  kind: live-session
