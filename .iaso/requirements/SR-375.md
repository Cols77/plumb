id: SR-375
title: No hook waits on work its reader does not need this turn
statement: 'A hook''s inline work is bounded by what its reader needs before the next step: the read-back
  and the relevant records at a prompt, the stale notice at an edit, the next action at a turn''s end.
  Everything else (test selection beyond the turn''s files, summaries, judge jobs, link states of untouched
  records) runs in the background worker and reaches the agent at the next message. Each hook''s default
  soft budget and hard limit are set from a profile of its steps on this repository (DEC-394): the time
  of each step is measured first, and the limits are the measured time plus headroom; the owner changes
  them in plain words. A hook cut by its limit is a defect that opens a debug case, not a normal turn.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a stop hook on this repository with its logs compacted
  when: it runs with profiling on
  then: it records the time of each of its steps, and its default limits equal the measured time plus
    headroom
- id: EX-2
  given: a hook that would need 20 s
  when: it runs
  then: it prints what it kept within its hard limit, the rest is finished by the background worker, and
    one debug case is opened for the cut
- id: EX-3
  given: three working days of sessions
  when: the hook error log is read
  then: no stop hook was cut at its hard limit
- id: EX-4
  given: the owner says hooks may take longer
  when: the agent sets the limit
  then: hooks.hard_limit_s changes and nothing else
