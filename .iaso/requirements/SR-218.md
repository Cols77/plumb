id: SR-218
title: An open question the approved records already answer, or that...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: In the background, the judge reads each open question against the approved records and decisions
  and withdraws it only when an approved record answers it, quoting that record, or when it duplicates
  another open question, naming it. Unsure keeps it; a proposed record only the agent wrote never settles
  anything, while a decision whose choice is the owner's own logged answer settles what it answers (SR-335,
  DEC-285); withdrawing approves nothing. Each withdrawal is logged.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an open question asking whether a conflict may be answered through the agent in chat, and an
    approved decision saying it may
  when: the background check runs
  then: the question is withdrawn and the withdrawal is logged quoting that decision's words
- id: EX-2
  given: the same question and only a proposed decision
  when: the background check runs
  then: the question stays
- id: EX-3
  given: two open stale-link issues on the same requirement asking the same thing
  when: the background check runs
  then: one is withdrawn as a duplicate, the log naming the other
- id: EX-4
  given: the judge cannot be reached
  when: the background check runs
  then: nothing is withdrawn and no owner line is printed (SR-373); the check runs again when a judge
    is back
- id: EX-5
  given: the judge is unsure whether an approved record answers an open question
  when: the background check runs
  then: the question stays open and nothing is logged as resolved
- id: EX-6
  given: a judge question waits and the owner answered the same choice earlier through the question tool
  when: the background job runs
  then: the question closes, logged with the words quoted from that answer, and the agent is told in one
    line; the owner can reopen it
- id: EX-7
  given: the same question and a quote that is not in the answer
  when: the background job runs
  then: the question stays open
