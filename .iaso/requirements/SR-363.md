id: SR-363
title: The turn-end line counts no waiting questions
statement: 'The owner''s one iaso line at a turn end no longer counts questions waiting for the owner;
  it says only a behaviour that stopped working or a merge that waits (SR-373, DEC-384: no ''answer applied''
  or ''touched, N checks pass'' line). A turn with nothing else for the owner shows nothing.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: three judge questions were raised and asked during the turn
  when: the turn ends
  then: no 'questions for you' text appears
- id: EX-2
  given: a question the owner dismissed still waits
  when: the turn ends with nothing else for the owner
  then: iaso prints no line; the band still counts the waiting question

  plan: In the same live session the turn ends with no 'N questions for you' line.
  kind: live-session
  post_merge: true
