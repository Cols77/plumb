id: SR-234
title: A requirement is one durable behaviour, and a fix goes under its...
feature: FEAT-001
statement: 'Each requirement states one durable observable behaviour with examples and a proof, under
  a feature; a fix of a stated behaviour is claimed under its requirement (or an NC when it was broken),
  never as a new requirement; one intent per principle; a newer decision wins over an older example''s
  test. No iaso check enforces this: the rule lives in iaso''s iaso-records skill, which the agent follows,
  and in commit trailers that claim the requirement fixed.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: iaso's installed iaso-records skill
  when: the agent reads it
  then: it says each requirement states one durable observable behaviour, a fix of a stated behaviour
    is claimed under its requirement or recorded as an NC, never as a new requirement, there is one intent
    per principle, and a record is retired through a superseding record, never deleted
- id: EX-2
  given: a behaviour a requirement states is broken (a background process opens a console window)
  when: it is fixed
  then: the fixing commit's trailer claims that requirement, and no new requirement appears
- id: EX-3
  given: a newer decision changed a behaviour that an older approved example's test still asserts
  when: the agent finds the conflict
  then: the agent's iaso-records skill tells it that the test follows the newer decision and the older
    example is reworded or superseded through the judge cascade, and that the owner is not asked per test
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended for the owner to decide: the AI fixes a broken behaviour in a scratch repository
    and shows the commit''s trailer naming its requirement with no new requirement written, and the skill''s
    text stating the rule.'
