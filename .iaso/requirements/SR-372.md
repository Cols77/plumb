id: SR-372
title: The judge approves what does not contradict the owner
statement: A proposed intent, requirement or decision is judge-approved when the judge finds that it neither
  contradicts the owner's logged words and answers nor adds intent the owner did not give; a benign restatement
  or extension is approved and marked judge-approved with the verdict in its trail. Only a contradiction,
  or new intent the owner did not give, becomes a question through the question tool. The owner reverses
  a judge-approved record by saying so in conversation. Entailment of the record by the owner's words
  is never the test.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a requirement that restates the owner's request in formal words
  when: the judge reads it
  then: it is approved as judge-approved with no question asked
- id: EX-2
  given: a requirement that adds an edge case the owner did not name but would not object to
  when: the judge reads it
  then: it is approved as judge-approved, marked benign extension
- id: EX-3
  given: a requirement that reverses behaviour the owner confirmed
  when: the judge reads it
  then: one question reaches the owner through the question tool, naming the behaviour in plain words
- id: EX-4
  given: a requirement that adds a feature the owner never asked for
  when: the judge reads it
  then: one question asks whether that intent is theirs
- id: EX-5
  given: 415 proposed records at the start
  when: one judge pass runs
  then: those that contradict nothing are judge-approved and only the contradictions wait for the owner
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner says in conversation that an approved record is wrong
  when: the reader reads the message
  then: the record is reversed to proposed with the owner's words as the reason
proof:
  plan: After one judge pass on this repository, iaso status shows most of the 415 proposed records judge-approved
    and only contradictions waiting; a new restating record in a live session is approved without a question,
    and a contradicting one asks one question through the question tool.
  kind: live-session
