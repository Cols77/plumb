---
id: SR-245
title: With no judge reachable, iaso says so once and holds only what...
feature: FEAT-002
statement: When no judge can answer a record (not configured, no key, timeout, service down, an out-of-schema
  answer, a diff too large), iaso says once per session window (from the session's start, or the judge's
  last answer, until the judge answers again) which judge, why and what waits; verdicts already given
  are applied at once, and only the unanswered records and the steps needing them (fast track, triage,
  calibration, the stale check, which never holds the turn for it) hold until a judge answers. Records
  whose judge question a recorded decision already answers wait too, labelled with that decision, until
  the judge rejudges them with it in view. Work needing no judge goes on, the owner's prompt is never
  held, nothing is decided by rule.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: no judge is configured
  when: the prompt hook judges an intent change
  then: iaso says no judgment model is configured, naming the setting, the prompt goes through, and no
    rule-based judge answers
- id: EX-2
  given: a JEV key is configured and JEV times out
  when: a judgment is requested
  then: iaso says JEV timed out; nothing falls back to a heuristic, the step needing it holds, and the
    prompt is not held
- id: EX-3
  given: the judge's key is missing
  when: the fast track would run
  then: one line says the judge is unreachable and the approval holds; nothing is approved by rule
- id: EX-4
  given: the judge's key is missing
  when: the agent edits a file and the turn ends with no stale link
  then: work goes on; the turn is not held
- id: EX-5
  given: the judge is back
  when: the held step runs again
  then: it completes and the line is gone
- id: EX-6
  given: a judge job of five records, JEV clears two, and the judge model is unreachable
  when: the job runs
  then: the two verdicts are applied and logged as JEV's, the three others wait, and one line says they
    wait for the judge model
- id: EX-7
  given: the judge model answers out of schema for a job
  when: the job runs
  then: its records wait with the same waiting line, not an unresolved error
- id: EX-8
  given: the diff of a job is too large for the judge model
  when: the job runs
  then: its records wait with the waiting line that names the size
- id: EX-9
  given: every record of a job is answered
  when: the job runs
  then: nothing waits and no line is printed
- id: EX-10
  given: the judge stays unreachable across two turns of the same session window
  when: each turn ends
  then: the line is printed once, not every turn
- id: EX-11
  given: the judge model answered out of schema for a job, and its records were retried once
  when: iaso cannot settle the job
  then: the records keep waiting with the waiting line, and the iaso line says one case went to debugging
- id: EX-12
  given: a judge job whose only question DEC-170 already answers
  when: the job runs
  then: the question is not asked again; its records wait with a line saying the owner already answered
    it (DEC-170)
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended: in a live session with the JEV key and the judge model both made unreachable, the
    owner states a small intent and the agent edits a file; the owner sees one line naming the judge and
    why, the records of the request waiting, the edit and the turn going on, then the key restored and
    the line gone on the next turn. The owner decides whether this is the proof.'
  kind: hook
