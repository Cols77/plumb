---
id: SR-376
title: The logs stay small and iaso answers fast
statement: 'Judgment entries are telemetry: at every landing the tidy job moves every judgment entry older
  than the landing into the kind''s archive segment, and the live judgment files are not tracked by git
  (the segment is). The offer kind no longer exists; nothing logs a card being drawn. Entries that record
  the owner''s decisions (approval, decision, rejection) stay one file each and tracked, with their outcome
  and the digests of the owner''s words, not the words; the owner''s messages and answers (prompt, pre-intent,
  question-answer, answer-bound, premise and the owner-words entries) stay out of git (DEC-116). This
  replaces SR-287 for judgment entries only; SR-287 still holds for every other kind. iaso brief, status
  and show answer in under 3 seconds on this repository; no command reads an archive segment unless it
  validates the chain or judges a record.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 19,770 live judgment entries
  when: the next landing runs
  then: they move into the judgment archive segment and the live folder holds only entries newer than
    the landing
- id: EX-2
  given: a judge job writes a judgment
  when: git status runs
  then: the new file is ignored
- id: EX-3
  given: a card is drawn anywhere
  when: the logs are read
  then: no offer entry exists
- id: EX-4
  given: this repository after compaction
  when: iaso brief runs
  then: it answers in under 3 seconds
- id: EX-5
  given: iaso validate
  when: it runs after compaction
  then: the hash chain checks through the segments
proof:
  plan: Entry counts per kind before and after the compaction, git ls-files showing no live judgment entries,
    a clean iaso validate, and iaso brief timed under 3 seconds.
  kind: command
