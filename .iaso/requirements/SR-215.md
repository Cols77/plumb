id: SR-215
title: A reversal re-formalizes the record, and counts as a calibration...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: When the reader returns a judge-approved record as reversed by the owner's own logged words,
  the record is re-formalized and judged again. Its old version counts as a calibration 'no' only when
  the words say iaso misread them; a change of mind, or words that do not say, carries no label. Words
  no hook-logged prompt holds reverse nothing.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a judge-approved intent and the owner's words 'that records your solution, not what I asked'
  when: the reader returns reversed, saying it misread the owner
  then: the old version counts as a calibration 'no', and the intent is re-formalized and judged again
- id: EX-2
  given: a judge-approved intent and the owner's words 'no, I don't know what to do with pi-agent-factory
    just yet'
  when: the reader returns reversed as an intent change
  then: the old version gets no calibration label and the intent is re-formalized
- id: EX-3
  given: a reversal whose words do not say whether the record misread them
  when: the reader returns it
  then: it is taken as an intent change, with no label, and the record is re-formalized
- id: EX-4
  given: words reversing a record that no prompt the hook logged holds
  when: the agent passes them on
  then: nothing is reversed
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended for the owner to decide: in a live session the owner says a judge-approved record
    misread them and sees it re-formalized, with the calibration count showing one more ''no''; then says
    they changed their mind about another and sees it re-formalized with no label.'
  kind: live-session
