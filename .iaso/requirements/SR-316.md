id: SR-316
title: A study you accepted can be shown as a video, rendered in the...
statement: When a study accepted at depth 'video' holds a clip, iaso starts a background render of that
  clip's Manim scene once its storyboard is complete (SR-364); the study shows its text and diagrams at
  once with 'video rendering…' at the clip's place, the clip appears there when its render ends and passes
  its check against its storyboard (SR-366), and a failed render or check leaves the rest of the study
  with the reason at the clip's place. No render starts for a depth other than video.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an accepted idea 'How the judge cascade runs' at depth video with one clip in its body
  when: the agent starts the clip's render
  then: the study opens with its text, its diagrams and 'video rendering…' at the clip's place, and the
    turn does not wait for the render
- id: EX-2
  given: a clip render in progress
  when: it ends and the reviewer's check passes
  then: the clip shows in place of 'video rendering…'
- id: EX-3
  given: a clip's scene raises an error while rendering
  when: the render ends
  then: the study keeps the rest of its body and the clip's place says the clip could not be rendered
    and why; no render is retried by itself
- id: EX-4
  given: the owner picks 'Markdown only' or 'with diagrams'
  when: the study is written
  then: no render starts
- id: EX-5
  given: the machine has no LaTeX
  when: a clip is rendered
  then: the scene uses plain text and shapes and the clip still renders

  plan: 'After landing, in a live intent loop: a video study on ''How the judge cascade runs'' is proposed,
    the owner accepts it at depth video in the band; the owner sees its storyboard, the reviewer''s frame-check
    verdict, and the video playing in the room with subtitles on; every screenshot (PNG) is opened on
    the owner''s screen.'
