id: SR-393
title: The band counts the project's loops and opens a pane on them
statement: 'In every session of the project, iaso''s band shows in its one line a short count of the loops
  being worked on (the loops chip), whether or not this session has a loop of its own; the band gains
  no line. Pressing the chip opens iaso''s one docked pane beside the conversation on its loops class,
  as the command lists them. The class shows one type of loop at a time, with a switch always in view
  that names the three types, intent, debug and evaluate, marks the one shown and gives how many loops
  each holds; one key or press moves to another type. Each row gives the loop''s goal and progress, live
  loops first and idle ones apart below. Enter on a row shows that loop''s goal, the steps done, what
  is left and what it waits for. The pane follows the loops while it is open and Esc closes it. A turn''s
  end never waits on reading the loops again: the band reads them just after, its count saying it is updating
  and keeping the last list until the new one is read. The known problems are the problems class of the
  same pane, reached from the band''s problems chip (SR-410, SR-412); no pane of their own stays. The
  pane lists no agents or tokens. It acts on a loop in two ways only: reopening an idle loop in a new
  session (or bringing a live loop''s window to the front, SR-414) and sending a message to a working
  loop, each on the owner''s press; it stops, closes and deletes nothing.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: two intent loops, one problem being fixed and one evaluate run are being worked on in the project
  when: the band draws in any session
  then: its line holds 'loops 4' and there is no line per loop
- id: EX-2
  given: the count shows
  when: the owner presses it
  then: a pane opens beside the conversation on the intent loops, one row per loop with its goal and its
    progress, the idle ones apart below, and a switch reading intent 2, debug 1, evaluate 1 with intent
    marked
- id: EX-3
  given: the loops pane is open
  when: the owner presses Enter on a row
  then: it shows that loop's goal, the steps done, what is left and what it waits for
- id: EX-4
  given: a session with no loop of its own while another session has an intent loop at plan
  when: its band draws
  then: the count shows and its pane lists that loop
- id: EX-5
  given: this session has its own intent loop open
  when: the band draws
  then: it still grows with the step strip and the task bar as before, and the count stays in its first
    line
- id: EX-6
  given: no loop is open in the project and none is idle
  when: the band draws
  then: no count is drawn
- id: EX-7
  given: only idle loops remain
  when: the band draws
  then: the count names them as idle and still opens the pane
- id: EX-8
  given: known problems are open
  when: the owner presses the problems chip of the band
  then: iaso's one pane opens on its problems class (SR-412), each problem a row; no pane of its own opens
- id: EX-9
  given: the loops pane is open and a loop of another session passes a gate
  when: the band next refreshes
  then: the pane shows the new step
- id: EX-10
  given: the loops pane shows the intent loops
  when: the owner moves the switch to debug
  then: the pane lists the problems being fixed, the switch marks debug, and one more move shows the evaluate
    runs
- id: EX-11
  given: no evaluate run is going
  when: the owner moves the switch to evaluate
  then: the switch reads evaluate 0 and the pane says no evaluate run is going
- id: EX-12
  given: an intent loop's state changed during the agent's turn
  when: the turn ends
  then: the turn ends without waiting for the loops to be read again; within seconds the band's count
    says updating, keeps the last list meanwhile, and then shows the new state
proof:
  plan: 'After landing and a plugin reload: two live sessions; a loop opened in one shows in the other''s
    band count and pane with its goal, progress and what is left; a problem being fixed and an evaluate
    run show too; screenshots (PNG) of the band and the pane are opened in a window on the owner''s screen.'
