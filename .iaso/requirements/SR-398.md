id: SR-398
title: A not verified verdict goes back to the build, not to the owner
statement: When the evaluator's verdict is not verified, the loop stays open and the builder is given
  what was not shown, as it is given a failing test; the owner is not asked. The builder fixes and the
  evaluator checks once more. Verified then, the proof is confirmed. Still not verified after that retry,
  the owner is told in one plain line what was not shown, and the loop stays open with nothing merged.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a change deliberately broken so that it does not show what its proof states
  when: the evaluator checks it
  then: the verdict is not verified and says what was missing, the loop stays open and the owner is asked
    nothing
- id: EX-2
  given: a not verified verdict, and the builder fixed what was missing
  when: the evaluator checks again and the case shows the proof
  then: the proof is confirmed
- id: EX-3
  given: a proof still not verified after the retry
  when: the turn ends
  then: the owner is told in one plain line what was not shown, the loop stays open and its branch is
    not merged
