id: SR-355
title: The owner can ask for a study themselves
statement: When the owner asks for a study, in plain words or with /iaso study <topic>, iaso offers that
  study idea on the band with its recommended depth, whether or not an intent loop is open, outside the
  one-per-step limit; the owner picks the depth before anything is written
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: no intent loop is open
  when: the owner writes 'make me a study on how merges are held'
  then: the band offers a study idea on how merges are held with a recommended depth, and nothing is written
    yet
  quote: trigger study rooms on request too
  prompt: 3fb136db34988462
- id: EX-2
  given: the clarify step of a loop already has its proposed study
  when: the owner asks for a study on another topic
  then: the requested idea is offered too; the step's limit holds only iaso's own proposals
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner's picture says they know merges, or they once declined a study of merges
  when: they ask for a study on merges
  then: 'it is offered: iaso''s refusals hold only its own proposals'
- id: EX-4
  given: a requested idea is on the band
  when: the owner picks diagrams
  then: iaso writes it at depth diagrams and the room shows it, as for a proposed study
proof:
  plan: after landing and reloading the plugin, the owner types a plain request for a study in a session;
    the band offers it, they pick a depth, it appears in the room
  kind: live-session
  post_merge: true
