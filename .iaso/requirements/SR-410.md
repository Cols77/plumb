---
id: SR-410
title: One docked pane with a class switch, rows that expand to detail...
statement: 'Pressing a chip of iaso''s band opens iaso''s one docked pane beside the conversation on that
  class, as wide as the host allows, so a title is cut only at the width the pane really has, never at
  a fixed width. Across the top a switch names every class with its count, the shown one bold; the loops
  class keeps its intent, debug and evaluate sub-switch. Arrow keys move across the switch and the rows,
  Enter expands or folds a row, Esc closes the pane and leaves the band unchanged. A folded row is one
  truncated line: glyph, title, short state. Live or open rows come first in normal colour; idle, fixed
  or read rows come below, dim. An expanded row shows its title bold in plum, two to four dim detail lines,
  then its actions on one row; an action, the message field included, is drawn only on the expanded row.
  An expanded loop row also lists the loop''s steps with their marks; pressing a step shows that step''s
  details (SR-305). Everywhere iaso draws in Claude Code: plum for iaso''s own structure, brass for what
  needs the owner''s choice or marks a proof shown, red for broken, dim for idle, fixed, history, detail
  and zero, bold on at most one run per line; glyphs carry the state, and at eighty columns nothing wraps.
  The pane follows iaso''s files while open. It may show the agents and the token spend of a loop''s work
  once iaso knows them (DEC-511); it stops, closes and deletes nothing.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the band shows a studies chip
  when: the owner presses it
  then: the pane opens with the switch loops · studies · problems · judge · build, studies bold, and the
    study rows below
- id: EX-2
  given: the pane is open on loops with a live row and an idle row
  when: the owner moves to the idle row and presses Enter
  then: the row shows its title bold, dim lines for done, left, waits for and branch, then one action
    row with reopen; no other row shows an action
- id: EX-3
  given: a loop row is expanded and the loop is being worked on
  when: the owner looks at its actions
  then: the message field is there and on no folded row
- id: EX-4
  given: the pane is open
  when: the owner presses the right arrow on the switch
  then: the next class is shown, bold, with its rows; the band is unchanged
- id: EX-5
  given: a row's title is longer than the pane
  when: it is drawn folded
  then: it is cut with an ellipsis on one line
- id: EX-6
  given: the pane is open on any class
  when: the owner presses Esc
  then: the pane closes and the band is exactly as before
- id: EX-7
  given: a terminal wide enough to dock the pane, and a loop whose title is longer than 80 characters
    but fits the width the pane has
  when: the owner presses a chip of the band
  then: the pane opens as wide as the host allows and the folded row shows the whole title; only a title
    longer than the pane's real width is cut with an ellipsis
