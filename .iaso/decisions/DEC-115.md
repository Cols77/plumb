title: When no judge can answer, or a judge job is only partly answered,...
question: When no judge can answer, or a judge job is only partly answered, what does iaso do?
chosen: Say so once, apply the verdicts already given, and hold only the unanswered records and the steps
  needing them until a judge answers; work needing no judge goes on; nothing is decided by rule.
alternatives:
- Say so once and skip, never hold
- Fall back to simple rules
- The whole job waits until every record has a verdict
matters: true
recommended: Say so once, apply the verdicts already given, and hold only the unanswered records and the
  steps needing them until a judge answers; work needing no judge goes on; nothing is decided by rule.
