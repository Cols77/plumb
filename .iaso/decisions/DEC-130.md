question: What does the agent get, and what does the owner see?
chosen: The agent gets everything as text (brief, ids, stale links, untraced files, warnings, conflicts,
  answers) whatever the owner sees and on every host; the owner sees the one-line status with progress,
  at most one iaso line at turn end, and questions through the question tool or the study room. Cards
  and the full/compact/off setting are retired. The background checks do the work and the band only draws;
  the session-start hook still records iaso's Python before any slow step so the band always connects,
  and the two old band tests change their expectation to the brief arriving as text.
alternatives:
- The agent is told only what blocks it
- Keep cards on the panel for the owner, with the full/compact/off setting
- No brief text to the agent while the band is up (the old band tests' expectation)
