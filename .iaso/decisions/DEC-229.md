title: Does the owner's answer to a judge question still count once the...
question: Does the owner's answer to a judge question still count once the agent rewords the record to
  agree with it?
chosen: 'yes: the answer follows the record through its later wordings, until a version is confirmed on
  it; an edit of a confirmed record is asked about again (SR-248 EX-9)'
alternatives:
- 'no: the answer applies only to the version asked about, and the reworded record is asked again'
- yes, at any later version, even an edit of a record confirmed on that answer
rationale: 'The judge asked INT-092''s refresh question five times on 2026-10-18: each typed answer led
  to a rewording, and the reworded record came back to the judge without the answer. The owner: a typed
  answer counts; the judge asks again only when it truly leaves the question open. The judge model refused
  the wider rule on the repin of SR-248: it would let an answer carry an edit made after the record was
  confirmed on it.'
matters: false
recommended: 'yes: the answer follows the record through its later wordings, until a version is confirmed
  on it; an edit of a confirmed record is asked about again (SR-248 EX-9)'
