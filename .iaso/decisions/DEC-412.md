title: Where do the words the owner typed live when an entry that records...
question: Where do the words the owner typed live when an entry that records their decision is committed,
  and what happens to the owner's-words entries already tracked?
chosen: A shared entry (approval, revocation, conflict-resolution, waiver) keeps only the outcome and
  the digests of the owner's words; the words are a local owner-words entry readers join by digest, and
  a machine without them takes the outcome as it stands. The owner's yes to a proof is a committed proof-confirmed
  entry that follows the record through a renumber. The prompt, question-answer, pre-intent and premise
  entries already committed stop being tracked (files kept, history not rewritten); the landing restores
  any file a merge deletes from main.
alternatives:
- Keep the words in the shared entries
- Ask for the proof again after a merge
- Rewrite history to remove the tracked entries
rationale: 'Owner, 2026-11-01: "nothing you write goes into git". Their reasoning, verbatim: "Let''s think
  what means an intent that is being merged. Word to intent matching should already have been validated
  locally when merged. So what''s the point of having the words versionized? and then why should it be
  re-checked everytime?" So a record is validated once, locally, before it merges, and a clone without
  the words takes the outcome as it stands; the owner''s yes to a proof is that outcome and follows the
  record through a renumber. Asked whether to stop tracking the owner-word entries already committed (prompt,
  question-answer, pre-intent, premise, answer-bound) with no history rewrite, the owner answered through
  the question tool: "Try to stop tracking them but quickly verify if it leads to any kind of failure".
  DEC-232 holds: the kinds stay shared, with the text split out. Verified: a merge deletes the untracked-by-the-branch
  files from main''s working tree (369 of 533 in a simulated landing), so the landing restores them from
  the merge base; the landing session also resolves the prompt archive modify/delete conflict and untracks
  files main gained since (landing-owner-words note).'
