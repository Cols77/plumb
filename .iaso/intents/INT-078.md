id: INT-078
title: Intent is captured when given, and rationalized into one coherent set
quote: When a user clearly answers to a specific question, that should be logged as an intent without
  having to have a new card created and re-asked the same question. The intent expression should be enough
readback: 'iaso takes your intent only when you give it: when you open /iaso intent, answer an intent
  conflict, answer a question in the question tool or the study room, or reply to what the last message
  showed. It reads your words by what you mean, not by fixed wording, takes the part that is clear without
  asking it again, and asks only about what stays unclear. Each piece it takes is fitted into the intent
  already recorded, so the whole set stays coherent.'
will:
- Keep each answer you give with the question, options and context it answered, so it is judged in context.
- Close a question once you clearly answered it, and raise no new question about your answer.
- 'Read your whole message with a language model independent of the coding agent, item by item: answered,
  change asked, reversed, or open.'
- Take the clear part of a mixed message and ask again, through the question tool, only the unclear part.
- Fit every captured piece into the recorded intent; a change to older intent becomes one question for
  you to arbitrate.
- When you say a judge-approved record misread you, re-formalize it and count the old version as a calibration
  'no'; an intent change gets no label.
wont:
- Capture intent from an ordinary work request outside these moments.
- Understand you with fixed word rules (affirmation or objection words, sentence splitting).
- Ask you the same question again after a clear answer.
- Let the agent's own relay or files stand for your words.
- Ask you to approve, by id or by reading a card, what the AI wrote.
assumptions:
- P4's 'a card press' now means an answer given in the browser study room, since cards the owner presses
  are retired (P12).
- Approval of the records an answer produces is the judge cascade's (P3, P9); this principle only captures
  and reads.
- An ordinary request outside the loop goes through the edit gate of P6, not through capture.
- No reachable reader model means iaso says so and holds (P8), never a fallback to word rules.
- Both requirements sit under FEAT-008 (intent understood by models); the cluster anchors SR-130 and SR-163
  carried no feature.
