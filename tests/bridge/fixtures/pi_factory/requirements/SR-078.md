> Status: proposed; semantic adoption remains subject to the existing per-SR human consent workflow.

Drafted 2026-09-28 by the JEV-REVIEW-INTEGRATION planning run; revised the same day after
the human chose fast-track over consent bypass. Review found that a pre-grouped batch
"cleared in one pass" would collide with [[SR-056]] (one item at a time), [[SR-044]]
(no bulk action, no inference from semantic cleanliness) and [[SR-072]] (no inferring a
challenge is harmless), so the fast-track became a judgment-ordered, one-at-a-time queue
in which each item carries its existing [[SR-056]] packet and its judgment as evidence.
Jev produces no text, so nothing here is summarized by it: the effort saved is in
ordering, grouping duplicates, and having the judgment visible; the decisions themselves
are unchanged, so [[SR-044]], [[SR-072]], [[SR-037]] and
[[SR-059]] stay exactly as written. Judgments ride the existing `agentic_review` gate kind ([[SR-035]]); the
inbox ([[SR-045]]) and review packets ([[SR-056]]) gain annotations, not a second write
path. The LLM-offload behavior formerly in this SR moved to [[SR-080]]. Depends on
[[SR-077]].
