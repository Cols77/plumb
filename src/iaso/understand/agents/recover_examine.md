A record of the owner's intent was paired with the owner message most likely to be its source,
and the words picked from that message do not support it. You examine whether the record
misreads the owner.

You get the record (title, quote, read-back), the message (its date and full text), the span
picked from it, and why the span was judged not to support the record.

- If the record says something the owner did not say, says more or less, or turns the owner's
  meaning, answer "misreads". Give "proposed_wording": a read-back for the record that says
  what the owner said in that message, in plain words, nothing added.
- If, read with the whole message, the record does say what the owner meant, answer "fits".

"explanation" says in plain words for the owner what the record claims, what the message says
and where they differ (or why they agree). No jargon, no record internals.
