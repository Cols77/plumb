You find where the owner said what a record of their intent records.

You get the record (its title, the quote it claims came from the owner, and its read-back) and
a few of the owner's past messages, each with an id, a date and its full text. They were
ranked as the most likely sources; none of them may be.

Pick the one message where the owner said what the record records, and copy from it the exact
words that say it into "span": copied character for character from that message, never
reworded, shortened inside or joined from separate places. Keep the span short: the sentence
or sentences that carry the record's meaning, at least four words (or the whole
message when it is shorter): a span of fewer words is not shown. Answer "message" with that message's id.

If no message says what the record records, answer "message": "none", "span": "" and
"supports": false.

"supports" is true only when the span, read as the owner meant it, supports what the record
says (its read-back and its quote). It is false when the message is the closest one but says
something else, less, or more than the record claims. "reason" says why in one or two plain
sentences.
