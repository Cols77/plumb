title: Drift is caught while the work happens
quote: Plumb should fix those problems during the design/development instead of afterwards. Understand
  what happens and why this happens and how to prevent this. SR overlap, SR that should be modified on
  new intent, SR contradiction,  missing tests,  no earlier version, failing tests should be leveraging
  plumb mechanisms to prevent this type of issiue.
readback: 'plumb exists to keep what you mean and what is being built in step. It catches drift while
  the work happens: in the turn that leaves a link stale, a change unowned or a record silently out of
  date, and at the merge at the latest, never afterwards. What the recorded intent and the judge can settle
  is settled without you; you are asked only when your intent itself would change.'
will:
- Tell the agent right after an edit which links it made stale, and keep the turn open until each is resolved,
  a set number of times at most; a link still open then becomes one question for you (INT-114).
- Repin silently when the covering tests pass and the judge says the meaning is kept; reword a record
  that drifted from its intent; send a change that breaks intent with no request behind it back to the
  agent as development work.
- Ask you, through the question tool, only when a change would change or drop confirmed intent, or two
  confirmed records contradict and no recorded intent settles it.
- Catch the same for agents a workflow runs, and hold a merge carrying new stale links, links to untested
  files or untraced changes.
- Show, as hints only, records with no link that may depend on a changed record.
- Name changed source that no requirement owns, with the judge's proposed owner, in the turn it appears.
wont:
- Ask you about a refactor or any change the judge finds keeps the meaning.
- Write a link, mark a link stale or confirm a record from a hint or a proposal.
- 'Decide a verdict by rule when no judge is reachable: it says so and nothing is decided; the agent
  is never held for it (INT-114).'
- Leave drift to be found afterwards, or run the full suite routinely to find it.
assumptions:
- 'Drift means code, tests or records no longer matching the confirmed intent: stale links, links to untested
  files, pins to uncommitted versions, changes no requirement owns, and unlinked records that may depend
  on a change.'
- '''The judge'' is P8''s: JEV above its calibrated threshold, else the configured judge model; a repin
  or rewording it validates counts as approved (P3, P9).'
- A case plumb cannot settle (a plumb bug, missing information) goes to the debug workflow, not to the
  owner (P5).
- The scan limits (5 candidates, 1 second) are defaults the owner may raise in plain words (P13).
- Only records are the specification (P15), so a changed document no longer triggers the dependency scan.
- 'Superseded in part by INT-114 (DEC-400, the owner''s answer of 2026-10-27, "Newer decisions replace
  them"): the stale hold ends after hooks.stale_holds_max holds, and an unreachable judge never holds
  the agent (SR-378, DEC-391).'
