title: See the intent loop's step and progress, learn what is being...
quote: Help me draft an update of plumb so that the user gets a nice visualisation of the step of plumb
  intent workflow we're at, as well as progress bar for in-workflow task progress. Maybe also during that
  workflow we could have suggestions of studies proposed to the user through the browser, so that when
  the intent is being validated, integrated, developped, the user can expand his knowledge on what has
  been built, or what's being built, suggested by the AI with a nice UI notification with nice colors.
  Make sure also that the colors used by plumb, its visual mod identity is uniquely differenciated and
  not mis-identified as claude internals
readback: While an intent loop runs, plumb's band in Claude Code grows to show which step you are at (clarify,
  plan, build, proven) and a bar for the tasks of that step, then shrinks back to one line when the loop
  ends. Along the way the AI proposes a few on-point studies in the browser room, chosen from a picture
  it keeps of what you already understand of the system, and tells you with a notification in plumb's
  colours. plumb takes its own plum and brass colours so it is never mistaken for Claude Code's own text.
will:
- Give plumb one colour identity, a plum violet with a brass accent, used by the band, the status line,
  the end-of-turn line where the host allows colour, and the browser room, and by no Claude Code element.
- 'Grow the band to at most three lines while an intent loop is open: a step strip, a bar for the current
  step''s tasks, and a line for a new study; back to one line when the loop ends.'
- Keep a picture of what you understand of the system, inferred from your prompts, your answers and the
  studies you open, corrected by 'I know this' and 'Go deeper' on each study, readable and editable in
  the browser room.
- 'Propose few, on-point studies: key architectural choices, areas you likely understand little, what
  you need to know to choose well later, open conflicts waiting for your choice, and features worth considering.'
- Announce a new study in the band where plumb shows its progress, opened by pressing it, which opens
  the room on that study; the room never opens by itself.
- Open a pane of what plumb knows about the loop (gates and findings, tasks, records, proofs) when you
  press the step strip.
- Build all of it in this loop and show it in a live loop.
wont:
- Open the browser room by itself or send operating-system notifications.
- Quiz you or ask you to rate your knowledge.
- Propose a study at every turn, or studies unrelated to this system and your choices.
- Show the grown band outside an intent loop.
- Change the colours of hosts without a band beyond what their status line allows.
assumptions:
- At most one new study per loop step, and none when nothing valuable stands out; the AI decides which.
- 'Plum is about #B05FB0 on dark terminals and #8E3B8E on light ones, brass about #C9A227; exact values
  are chosen so they read in both themes.'
- The step's tasks are the plan's items for plan and build, and the open questions for clarify; when the
  total is unknown the bar shows elapsed time, as SR-272 already says.
- The understanding picture is stored per project in .plumb, as plain text the room shows and lets you
  edit.
- Studies are written by the agent of the session running the loop, from the records, the code, your prompts
  and the open conflicts.
- The notification uses Claude Code's mod notification surface; where none exists the band line alone
  announces the study.
