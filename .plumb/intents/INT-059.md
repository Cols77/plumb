title: 'Only what plumb saw itself counts as your answer: a card press, a...'
quote: Ok for option 1. but then we should have a mechanism that prevents what can go wrong.
readback: 'Only what plumb saw itself counts as your answer: a card press, a typed /plumb command, or
  your own message read by plumb against the question it answers; the agent never carries your answer.
  Mechanisms make that safe: a question whose answer would change a record always has a card by the time
  you answer it, only words you really typed count as yours, and the gate that holds the agent’s edits
  answers to your message alone'
will:
- when the agent’s reply asks you something whose answer would confirm, choose or change a record, plumb
  raises the card from that question before you answer, so your reply is read against it; if you answer
  first, plumb raises the card and applies the answer in one step
- 'the prompt log keeps the origin of every entry: your typed messages are yours; a message from another
  Claude session, a task notification or hook text is logged with its origin and never counts as your
  words, never closes or opens a gate, and never backs a capture or an approval'
- the edit gate is keyed by session and by the one digest the hook logged, so another session’s message,
  or a different hashing of the same text, cannot hold or miss it
- an answer to a conflict card given in chat is read by plumb against that card; the agent-run conflict
  relay is refused again (DEC-051 reshaped)
wont:
- let the agent relay, quote or summarise your answer into a record for any card kind
- 'guess from word lists which messages are yours: the host marks a peer message, a notification or hook
  text as such, and plumb reads that mark'
assumptions:
- 'a question that changes a record means: confirm or reject a record, choose a conflict’s side, settle
  a stale link, or a decision that matters'
- SR-163 (a clear answer is recorded once) is the reading path these cards use
mode: incremental
