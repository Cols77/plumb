title: Ask only about intent and trade-offs; route what cannot settle to...
quote: Cards are only for complex decisions to make, allowing to see tradeoffs, context presented fully,
  and allowing the user to really study the pros and cons. Otherwise I guess usual question tool should
  be enough. Clarification should clarify high level decision invariants to allow to resolve most of the
  conflicts. It should resolve the philosophy. plumb tools should always look at the available intent
  to be able to try resolve issues before presenting them to the user.
readback: plumb asks you only about what you want and the trade-offs, at the level of the principle a
  group of details hinges on, and settles everything your recorded intent already answers without asking.
  A question that stops standing goes away by itself and is logged; the turn-end text says nothing about
  it. When plumb itself cannot settle something (a bug, missing information, a judge loop that does not
  converge), it works it out in a debug workflow and asks you only as a last resort.
will:
- before an issue reaches you, a judge reads it against your approved records and decisions and resolves
  what they answer, logged and reversible by saying so
- an open question is withdrawn by itself when its link is current again, its records are approved or
  superseded, a newer request replaces it, its branch is gone, an approved record answers it, or it duplicates
  another
- when several questions hinge on one principle, the principle is asked once through the question tool
  and the details are settled from your answer
- what plumb cannot settle or converge on goes to a background debug workflow; you get a question about
  it only in last resort, in plain words
- withdrawals, settlements and debug hand-offs are logged; the turn ends with one next action for the
  agent and no owner line about them (INT-114)
wont:
- ask you about anything your recorded intent already answers, or about plumb's own malfunctions before
  trying to resolve them
- count a proposed (not yet approved) record as settling anything
- 'answer a question for you: withdrawing one approves nothing'
- 'settle by word lists or rules: a judge derives each settlement, and with no judge reachable nothing
  is settled or withdrawn, and the agent is never held (INT-114)'
- show you cards, read-backs or record ids, or ask you to press anything
assumptions:
- '''approved'' means confirmed by the owner or judge-validated on the owner''s behalf (P3); both settle
  questions, a proposed record never does'
- an open question is any item awaiting the owner (a question-tool question, an intent conflict); cards
  as a pressable thing are retired (P12)
- a detail the principle does not settle but which is a real intent choice is asked as its own specific
  question, not sent to debugging
- the anchor SR-161 carries no feature; FEAT-008 (intent by exception, understood by models) is used as
  the closest
- 'Superseded in part by INT-114 (DEC-400, the owner''s answer of 2026-10-27): no turn-end line naming
