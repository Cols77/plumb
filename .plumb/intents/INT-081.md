title: Few records, one durable behaviour each, never lost
quote: I fear that there are actually many duplicates, or that the actual definition of an SR is actually

readback: 'The records are your intent written down, and there are few of them: each requirement states
  one durable behaviour you can observe, with examples and a proof. Every record is hashed so a change
  of meaning shows and its links go stale, and nothing is lost: a record is only retired, with a trail
  to what replaced it. A fix of a behaviour already stated goes under that requirement, never a new one,
  and each principle you state is one intent.'
will:
- Keep every record as a Markdown file named by its frontmatter id, with a hash that changes only when
  its meaning changes.
- Pin both ends of every link, report a moved end as stale, and change links only through logged repin,
  unlink and relink.
- Keep an append-only, hash-chained log, one never-edited file per entry, with tampering reported.
- Hold one durable observable behaviour per requirement; claim a fix under the requirement it restores.
- Retire a replaced record with a supersedes trail; it stops reaching you and stops being checked, and
  its uncarried examples' tests are unlinked.
- Capture your words as a pre-intent with context and write the formal records in one call, naming the
  requirements the new intent changes.
- Import pi-agent-factory's records read-only and idempotently, approving only what an upstream human
  consent pins.

- Delete a record without a supersedes trail.
- Create a new requirement for a fix of a behaviour already stated.

  P9).'
- Edit or remove a log entry outside the archive compaction of P15.
- Write to the pi-agent-factory source during an import.
