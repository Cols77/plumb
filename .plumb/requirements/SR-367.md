title: Known problems open in a pane from one band line
statement: When known problems are open, the band shows one collapsed line with how many are open; nothing
  more of them is in the band. Pressing it opens a pane docked beside the conversation, as the loop pane
  does, listing each known problem with its symptom, cause, fix, state and case history, open ones first.
  The pane follows the register while open.

  prompt: bcee0f05b14e8bf4

  prompt: bcee0f05b14e8bf4
