---
id: SR-367
title: Known problems open in a pane from one band line
statement: When known problems are open, the band shows one collapsed line with how many are open; nothing
  more of them is in the band. Pressing it opens a pane docked beside the conversation, as the loop pane
  does, listing each known problem with its symptom, cause, fix, state and case history, open ones first.
  The pane follows the register while open. When a known problem changes - its fix lands, it is proven,
  or it comes back in another session - the session that owns its case is told at its next prompt, once.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: two known problems are open and five were fixed lately
  when: the band draws
  then: it shows one line saying two known problems are open, and no line per problem
  quote: we don't want all the known problems to be shown in the band
  prompt: bcee0f05b14e8bf4
- id: EX-2
  given: the known problems line shows
  when: the owner presses it
  then: a pane opens beside the conversation listing each problem with its symptom, cause, fix, state
    and history
  quote: it should be collapsible and navigateable just like workflow tool is navigateable and opens some
    kind of tab that gives variosu informations
  prompt: bcee0f05b14e8bf4
- id: EX-3
  given: no known problem is open
  when: the band draws
  then: it shows no known problems line
- id: EX-4
  given: a session hit a known problem and owns its case
  when: the case is proven, or the problem comes back in another session
  then: that session is told at its next prompt, once; other sessions are told nothing
proof:
  plan: after landing and a plugin update, the band shows one known problems line; pressing it opens the
    pane with each problem's details
  kind: live-session
  post_merge: true
