id: SR-195
title: A requirement is one durable behaviour; the record set is version 2
statement: 'Each requirement states one durable, observable behaviour of plumb with examples and a proof,
  under a feature; a fix of a behaviour a requirement already states is recorded under that requirement
  (its commit trailer, or an NC when it was broken), never as a new requirement; an intent is one per
  principle. The version 2 set replaces the current one in place: every older record a version 2 record
  replaces is retired with a supersedes trail, its links stop counting, and one repin pass relinks the
  tests.'

  when: version 2 lands
  then: one requirement states the rule and the three are retired, superseded by it, with their tests
    relinked to it
  quote: I fear that there are actually many duplicates, or that the actual definition of an SR is actually
    blur currently in the plumb project.

  when: it is fixed

  when: it is captured
  then: one intent records it, and the requirements it changes are named at capture

    pointing at its successor
