---
id: SR-322
title: The log's tamper check holds with many writers
feature: FEAT-006
statement: plumb validate reports a log entry whose digest does not match its content and an unparsable
  entry file, but no longer an entry whose previous entry this checkout does not hold, since that entry
  may belong to another session, branch or a local kind. The merge check refuses a merge that deletes
  a committed log entry, naming it, unless the entry is kept in an archive segment of its kind.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an entry whose previous entry is another session's uncommitted entry
  when: plumb validate runs
  then: it reports no log error
- id: EX-2
  given: a committed entry edited by hand
  when: plumb validate runs
  then: it reports the entry as modified
- id: EX-3
  given: a branch that deletes a committed judgment entry
  when: it merges into main
  then: the merge is refused naming the entry
- id: EX-4
  given: a landing whose compaction moved committed entries into an archive segment
  when: it merges
  then: the merge is not refused for them
proof:
  plan: 'Two loops landing one after the other: each merge passes with validate at 0 errors; a scratch
    branch deleting a committed entry is refused.'
  kind: command
