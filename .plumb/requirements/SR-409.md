id: SR-409
title: The band is one line of class chips
statement: 'plumb''s band in Claude Code is one line in every session: plumb''s state with the count of
  questions waiting, then one chip per class that has something (loops with live and idle counts, studies,
  problems, judge, build), then Hide. A chip is absent when its class is empty, brass when the class wants
  the owner''s choice, plum when it informs, red when something is broken. No title, menu, choice, button
  row or per-item line is drawn in the band. While this session''s intent loop is open, lines two and
  three are the step strip and the task bar and nothing else. The band draws no card, and once the owner
  presses Hide it stays hidden until they press Show, whatever arrives.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: two study ideas wait, one study is new, one known problem is open and a loop is live in another
    session
  when: the band draws
  then: 'it is one line: plumb''s state, then the chips loops 1, studies 3 in brass, problems 1 in plum,
    then Hide; no title appears'
- id: EX-2
  given: no study, no problem, no judge fault and no build fault
  when: the band draws
  then: only plumb's state, the loops chip if any loop exists, and Hide are drawn
- id: EX-3
  given: this session has an intent loop open at build
  when: the band draws
  then: line one is the chips, line two the step strip, line three the task bar; the idea and new study
    that used to share the third line are counted in the studies chip
- id: EX-4
  given: a build misalignment and a judge that cannot settle
  when: the band draws
  then: one build chip in red and one judge chip in red; no red line per fault
- id: EX-5
  given: the owner pressed Hide
  when: a new study idea arrives
  then: the band stays its one dim line with Show until the owner presses Show
proof:
  plan: After the landing and a plugin reload, in a live session with two study ideas, one new study,
    one study out of date, one known problem open, one set-aside question and a loop live in another session,
    the band is one line of chips and nothing else; a screenshot of the band is the evidence
  kind: live-session
  post_merge: true
  evaluator: true
