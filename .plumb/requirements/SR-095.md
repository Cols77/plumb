---
id: SR-095
title: A requirement left with two versions after a merge is shown once, as a choice
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'When a merge leaves a requirement with two versions to choose from, plumb reports it once,
  on its choice card, and never as a list of its connected work that changed: the merge check, plumb''s
  integrity check, the drift report, the status, the turn summary, the review and the Obsidian vault all
  show it under the choice. Once the owner''s choice is recorded, plumb refreshes what it remembers about
  the requirement''s connections, so nothing about it remains (leftover from sub-project 6).'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a merge left the "deadline reminders" requirement with two versions
  when: the merge check runs
  then: the merge waits, shows the choice between the two versions of "deadline reminders", and lists
    nothing else about it
- id: EX-2
  given: the same two versions
  when: the drift report and the status are shown
  then: the requirement appears once, as waiting for the owner's choice between two versions
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner keeps feat-x's version
  when: the drift report runs
  then: nothing about "deadline reminders" remains
