---
id: SR-172
title: One principle, asked once, settles the detailed cards and...
statement: 'When several open cards, or the gaps and conflicts of a request, hinge on one principle, plumb
  asks it once (one card naming what it would settle, or the request check''s question for a commanded
  clarify) rather than each detail; once the owner''s decision on it is confirmed, a background job settles
  each dependent card from the decision''s words: withdrawn when answered, its link repinned when the
  meaning is kept, or a rewording proposed as an unconfirmed edit, each citing the decision, told in the
  one plumb line and undoable; a card the model cannot settle from the decision stays'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: twenty stale-link cards hinge on what counts as the owner’s answer
  when: the turn ends
  then: one principle card asks that question and lists the twenty cards it would settle; the twenty are
    not drawn
  quote: card writting should prefereably be minimal and target this type of intent resolution
  prompt: 03a951b08a8ba7c1
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner confirms the decision
  when: the settling job runs
  then: each of the twenty is withdrawn, repinned or gets a proposed rewording citing the decision, and
    the plumb line says how many of each
  quote: the user gives highlevel decisions that convert into lower level card answers
  prompt: 03a951b08a8ba7c1
- id: EX-3
  given: a dependent card the model cannot settle from the decision’s words
  when: the settling job runs
  then: the card stays open and the plumb line says one could not be settled
- id: EX-4
  given: a settlement the owner disagrees with
  when: the owner says undo on it
  then: the card is back as it was and the repin or rewording is reverted
- id: EX-5
  given: the owner types /plumb clarify on a request whose five open gaps all hinge on whether cards or
    the question tool ask the owner
  when: the check runs
  then: one question asks that principle and names the five gaps it settles; the five are not asked
  quote: Clarification should clarify high level decision invariants to allow to resolve most of the conflicts
  prompt: 16346cb668cf73ed
- id: EX-6
  given: a detailed gap that no principle settles
  when: the check runs
  then: it is asked on its own, as a specific question
proof:
  plan: 'a live session with grouped stale cards: one principle card, then the settlements after the decision'
  kind: live-session
