id: SR-088
title: Every owner action has an owner entry point; plumb's internal...
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'Every capability the owner acts on or needs to see is reachable through a /plumb command,
  plain words or a button, and the agent does the rest. plumb''s internal mechanisms (linking, repinning,
  hashing, test selection, log upkeep, ID sync and the like) stay internal: plumb and the agent run them,
  the owner is never asked to, and owner-facing text never mentions them. Owner-only actions (confirming
  and undoing, a group, a big plan, picking one of two versions, automatic confirmation on or off, the
  settings view, skipping the full test run) come only from the owner: words or a /plumb command the owner
  typed, which plumb saw arrive, or a button press. An action the agent starts on its own is refused.
  When the agent carries out the owner''s typed words or command, plumb records it as passed on by the
  agent with the owner''s exact words, so the owner can tell it apart from a button press. /plumb confirm
  with plain words never confirms anything the owner has not seen: it confirms at once only records named
  by number or shown in full in the agent''s previous answer; otherwise plumb finds the records the words
  mean, shows them, and confirms them on the owner''s next ok or press, or asks which one the owner means
  when the words fit several. One table lists every owner action with the owner''s way to reach it, and
  an end-to-end test drives every owner flow only through /plumb commands, messages and button presses
  (ruling R14).'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: JEV has passed its trial on the owner's past answers for the current model
  when: the owner types "/plumb auto-confirm on"
  then: automatic confirmation is on, recorded as passed on by the agent from that typed command, with
    its exact words
- id: EX-2
  given: no owner message asked for it
  when: the agent tries to turn automatic confirmation on
  then: it is refused and nothing changes
- id: EX-3
  given: two requirements about reminder timing that the owner was not shown in this session, and nothing
    else those words match
  when: the owner types "/plumb confirm the reminder timing"
  then: plumb shows the two requirements with a Confirm button and confirms nothing until the owner says
    ok or presses Confirm; then exactly those two, at the wording shown, are confirmed
- id: EX-4
  given: the decision DEC-017 "which channel sends reminders" awaits the owner
  when: the owner types "/plumb approve DEC-017"
  then: it is confirmed at once, recorded as passed on from the owner's typed command
- id: EX-5
  given: the list of plumb's commands, split into what the owner acts on and plumb's own internal mechanisms
  when: the owner-entry-point table's test runs
  then: every owner action is listed with the owner's way to reach it, and no internal mechanism (linking,
    repinning, hashing, test selection, log upkeep, ID sync) appears in the table, in any owner-facing
    text, or as a step the owner is asked to take
- id: EX-6
  given: the words "the reminders" match both the reminder timing and the reminder channel requirements
  when: the owner types "/plumb confirm the reminders"
  then: plumb shows both groups, asks which one the owner means, and confirms nothing
- id: EX-7
  given: the agent's previous answer showed the reminder timing requirement in full
  when: the owner types "/plumb confirm the reminder timing"
  then: it is confirmed at once from the owner's typed command
- id: EX-8
  given: the owner typed "/plumb confirm the reminder timing" and the requirement was confirmed
  when: plumb's integrity check runs and the merge glance is shown
  then: the check finds nothing wrong, and the glance lists it as "you typed /plumb confirm the reminder
    timing", apart from button presses
- id: EX-9
  given: a stale link that only needs repinning because its target was reformatted
  when: the stop hook runs
  then: plumb or the agent repins it without telling the owner; the owner hears of staleness only when
    what the record means may have changed
