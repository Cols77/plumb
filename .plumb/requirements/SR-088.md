id: SR-088
title: Every plumb feature has an owner entry point, and no owner step needs a shell
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'Every owner-facing plumb capability is reachable through a /plumb command, plain words or
  a button, and the agent does the rest. Owner-only actions (confirming and undoing, a group, a big plan,
  picking one of two versions, automatic confirmation on or off, the settings view, skipping the full
  test run) come only from the owner: words or a /plumb command the owner typed, which plumb saw arrive,
  or a button press. An action the agent starts on its own is refused. When the agent carries out the
  owner''s typed words or command, plumb records it as passed on by the agent with the owner''s exact
  words, so the owner can tell it apart from a button press. /plumb confirm with plain words never confirms
  anything the owner has not seen: it confirms at once only records named by number or shown in full in
  the agent''s previous answer; otherwise plumb finds the records the words mean, shows them, and confirms
  them on the owner''s next "ok" or press, or asks "which one do you mean?" when the words fit several.
  One table lists every plumb feature with the owner''s way to reach it, and an end-to-end test drives
  every owner flow only through /plumb commands, messages and button presses (ruling R14).'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: JEV has passed its trial on the owner's past answers for the current model
  when: the owner types "/plumb auto-confirm on"
  then: automatic confirmation is on, recorded as passed on by the agent from that typed command, with
    its exact words
- id: EX-2
  given: no owner message asked for it
  when: the agent tries to turn automatic confirmation on
  then: it is refused and nothing changes
- id: EX-3
  given: two requirements about reminder timing that the owner was not shown in this session, and nothing
    else those words match
  when: the owner types "/plumb confirm the reminder timing"
  then: plumb shows the two requirements with a Confirm button and confirms nothing until the owner says
    ok or presses Confirm; then exactly those two, at the wording shown, are confirmed
- id: EX-4
  given: the decision DEC-017 "which channel sends reminders" awaits the owner
  when: the owner types "/plumb approve DEC-017"
  then: it is confirmed at once, recorded as passed on from the owner's typed command
- id: EX-5
  given: the table of owner entry points and the list of plumb's commands
  when: the table's test runs
  then: every command is listed, either with the owner's way to reach it or as the agent's own step with
    the owner words that lead to it
- id: EX-6
  given: the words "the reminders" match both the reminder timing and the reminder channel requirements
  when: the owner types "/plumb confirm the reminders"
  then: plumb shows both groups, asks which one the owner means, and confirms nothing
- id: EX-7
  given: the agent's previous answer showed the reminder timing requirement in full
  when: the owner types "/plumb confirm the reminder timing"
  then: it is confirmed at once from the owner's typed command
- id: EX-8
  given: the owner typed "/plumb confirm the reminder timing" and the requirement was confirmed
  when: plumb's integrity check runs and the merge glance is shown
  then: the check finds nothing wrong, and the glance lists it as "you typed /plumb confirm the reminder
    timing", apart from button presses
