id: SR-106
title: Settling starts with a trial that calibrates on the owner's answers
feature: FEAT-008
statement: Until calibration passes for a record kind, every supported record is offered in trial batches
  of up to five (plumb would settle these; right?); the owner's yes settles it and labels it yes, no rejects
  it as a misreading; calibration reruns from logged judgments after each batch; when it passes the owner
  is told once and turns settling on with words or a button; settled records are labelled settled from
  your words, count as confirmed at the merge, are listed apart there, and return to proposed when their
  text or premise changes; a model or prompt change suspends settling until the evaluation set passes
  and calibration reruns.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: no passing calibration and three supported records
  when: the turn ends
  then: one trial batch shows the three; nothing is settled yet
- id: EX-2
  given: calibration passes for intent read-backs
  when: the next turn ends
  then: the owner is told once and offered to turn settling on for intent read-backs; it stays off until
    they do
- id: EX-3
  given: a settled record whose premise message is edited
  when: status runs
  then: the record is proposed again
