id: SR-106
title: Settling starts with a trial that calibrates on the owner's answers
feature: FEAT-008
statement: Until calibration passes for a record kind, every supported record is offered in trial batches
  of up to five (plumb would settle these; right?); the owner's yes settles it and labels it yes, no rejects
  it as a misreading; calibration reruns from logged judgments after each batch; when it passes for requirements
  (with their examples), the records the agent derives, settling for that kind switches itself on, the
  owner is told once how to switch it off, and the switch-on is logged with the calibration that allowed
  it; when it passes for intents, the owner is told once and turns settling on with the typed /plumb settle
  on command or a button (prose cannot); the owner switches any kind off by the typed /plumb settle off
  or a button, and plumb never switches a kind on again that the owner switched off; settled records are
  labelled settled from your words, count as confirmed at the merge, are listed apart there, and return
  to proposed when their text or premise changes; a model or prompt change suspends settling until the
  evaluation set passes and calibration reruns, after which a kind that switched itself on does so again
  unless the owner switched it off. A decision never settles and is always asked individually, nor a set
  member, an escalated set member or an edit of an owner-confirmed record. The judge's own confidence
  is logged as its score at judgment time and calibration reads it, so thresholds come from trial outcomes
  and are not fixed; the owner's trial yes and no, misread rejections and owner-words confirmations are
  the labels; one item per batch is drawn from below the threshold.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: no passing calibration and three supported records
  when: the turn ends
  then: one trial batch shows the three; nothing is settled yet
- id: EX-2
  given: calibration passes for intent read-backs
  when: the next turn ends
  then: the owner is told once and offered to turn settling on for intent read-backs; it stays off until
    they do
- id: EX-3
  given: a settled record whose premise message is edited
  when: status runs
  then: the record is proposed again
- id: EX-4
  given: calibration passed for intent read-backs and the owner writes "turn settling on" in prose
  when: the reader runs
  then: settling stays off for intents; plumb names the /plumb settle on command and the button
- id: EX-5
  given: the judge returns supported for a decision, a set member, or an edit of an owner-confirmed record
  when: the turn ends
  then: none is settled; each waits as a trial or confirm card
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner answers yes to a trial item
  when: calibration reruns
  then: the yes is a labelled yes (recorded as an owner confirmation via the button, not as a plumb settlement)
    and the logged judge score is used, with no new model call
- id: EX-7
  given: a trial batch of five
  when: the batch is built
  then: four are items the judge would settle and one is drawn from below the threshold, asked as plumb
    would not settle this; right?
- id: EX-8
  given: calibration passes for requirements
  when: the next turn ends
  then: settling for requirements is on without the owner doing anything, the summary says so once with
    how to switch it off, and the switch-on is logged with the calibration
- id: EX-9
  given: calibration passes for requirements and the owner had switched settling off for requirements
    with /plumb settle off
  when: the next turn ends
  then: settling for requirements stays off and plumb does not switch it on again
- id: EX-10
  given: a new model version suspends settling and the evaluation set then passes again
  when: calibration reruns
  then: requirements switch themselves back on, and intents settle again only if the owner had switched
    them on
