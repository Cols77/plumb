id: SR-106
title: Settling starts with a trial that calibrates on the owner's answers
feature: FEAT-008
statement: Until calibration passes for a record kind, every supported record is offered in trial batches
  of up to five (plumb would settle these; right?); the owner's yes settles it and labels it yes, no rejects
  it as a misreading; calibration reruns from logged judgments after each batch; when it passes the owner
  is told once and turns settling on with the typed /plumb settle on command or a button (prose cannot);
  settled records are labelled settled from your words, count as confirmed at the merge, are listed apart
  there, and return to proposed when their text or premise changes; a model or prompt change suspends
  settling until the evaluation set passes and calibration reruns. A decision never settles, nor a set
  member, an escalated set member or an edit of an owner-confirmed record. The judge's score is logged
  at judgment time and calibration reads it; the owner's trial yes and no, misread rejections and owner-words
  confirmations are the labels; one item per batch is drawn from below the threshold.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: no passing calibration and three supported records
  when: the turn ends
  then: one trial batch shows the three; nothing is settled yet
- id: EX-2
  given: calibration passes for intent read-backs
  when: the next turn ends
  then: the owner is told once and offered to turn settling on for intent read-backs; it stays off until
    they do
- id: EX-3
  given: a settled record whose premise message is edited
  when: status runs
  then: the record is proposed again
- id: EX-4
  given: calibration passed and the owner writes "turn settling on" in prose
  when: the reader runs
  then: settling stays off; plumb names the /plumb settle on command and the button
- id: EX-5
  given: the judge returns supported for a decision, a set member, or an edit of an owner-confirmed record
  when: the turn ends
  then: none is settled; each waits as a trial or confirm card
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner answers yes to a trial item
  when: calibration reruns
  then: the yes is a labelled yes (recorded as an owner confirmation via the button, not as a plumb settlement)
    and the logged judge score is used, with no new model call
- id: EX-7
  given: a trial batch of five
  when: the batch is built
  then: four are items the judge would settle and one is drawn from below the threshold, asked as plumb
    would not settle this; right?
