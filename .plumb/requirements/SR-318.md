id: SR-318
title: A video and its scene are committed next to their study
statement: A study's scene source and rendered mp4 live under .plumb/studies/ beside the study's Markdown
  and are committed with it, so the video plays on any clone without rendering again; a changed scene
  renders again and replaces the mp4.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a video render ends for study 'judge-cascade'
  when: the study is committed
  then: its scene .py and .mp4 are in .plumb/studies/ beside judge-cascade.md and in the commit
- id: EX-2
  given: a fresh clone with a committed video study
  when: the owner opens it in the room
  then: the video plays with no render
- id: EX-3
  given: the agent changes a study's scene
  when: the render ends
  then: the new mp4 replaces the old one
proof:
  plan: After the live loop, the commit shows the scene and the mp4 beside the study, and the room plays
    it.
  kind: live-session
