id: SR-244
title: JEV's question form is a setting, and a calibration counts only...
feature: FEAT-002
statement: 'jev.output sets how JEV is asked: yes/no by default, or the three-way choice over supported,
  beyond and contradicts, whose probability of supported is used and logged with the form. A calibration
  measured with one form is not used for the other.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: jev.output is unset
  when: JEV is asked
  then: it is asked yes/no
- id: EX-2
  given: jev.output is choice
  when: JEV is asked
  then: it is asked the three-way choice over supported, beyond and contradicts, the probability of supported
    is used, and the log names the form
- id: EX-3
  given: a calibration measured with yes_no
  when: jev.output is switched to choice
  then: the fast track does not run on the old calibration and JEV status says a calibration for the choice
    form is needed
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended: the owner says to switch JEV to the choice form and /plumb jev status then says
    a calibration for the choice form is needed. The owner decides whether this is the proof.'
