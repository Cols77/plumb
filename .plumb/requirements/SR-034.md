---
id: SR-034
title: plumb capture records a whole read-back in one call, all proposed
feature: FEAT-002
statement: plumb capture takes one document (the owner's verbatim quote, the read-back, will, won't, labelled
  assumptions, requirements with given/when/then examples, decisions with alternatives) and writes the
  INT, SR and DEC records with pinned derives_from, decides and implemented_by links, all proposed, IDs
  allocated through Store.next_id only, and prints the intent card the agent puts in its reply. The quote
  must be verbatim in the logged prompt when --prompt is given.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a capture document with a quote, a read-back, one requirement with two examples and one decision
  when: plumb capture runs
  then: one INT, one SR with EX-1 and EX-2 and one DEC exist, each proposed, the SR and DEC linked derives_from
    the INT, and the intent card is printed
- id: EX-2
  given: a capture document whose quote is not a verbatim span of the prompt named by --prompt
  when: plumb capture runs
  then: it exits 2 and writes nothing
- id: EX-3
  given: a document updating the examples of a confirmed SR
  when: plumb capture runs
  then: the SR becomes an unconfirmed edit and the output says it needs the owner again
- id: EX-4
  given: any capture document
  when: plumb capture --dry-run runs
  then: it prints the card and writes no file and no event
