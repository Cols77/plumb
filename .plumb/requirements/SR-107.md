---
id: SR-107
title: The proposed backlog is worked through in the trial
feature: FEAT-008
statement: Records proposed before SP8 are judged in one background pass, not metered per turn, and then
  offered as one card per feature ("these 18 about the merge check would be settled; right?"); the owner's
  yes confirms all the records on the card, labelled yes for calibration, and exceptions named in the
  answer are mapped per record by the reader; flagged ones are not on the feature card and raise no card
  of their own; the pass's end line counts them and they stay unconfirmed (SR-152); a record whose original
  words are not on file says so on its card and offers its read-back for the owner to accept or correct;
  while the pass runs the status line and a bar or plain lines say how many are judged (SR-108).
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 150 proposed records
  when: the worker starts the backlog pass
  then: one pass judges them all without a per-turn limit, and a job already done is not run again unless
    its record changed
- id: EX-2
  given: INT-010 whose quote is a fragment not found in the prompt log
  when: it is judged
  then: its card says the original words are not on file
- id: EX-3
  given: 18 records about the merge check judged supported
  when: the turn ends
  then: one feature card asks whether they would be settled, and the owner's yes confirms all 18, each
    labelled yes
- id: EX-4
  given: the feature card of 18
  when: the owner answers "yes, except SR-040"
  then: the reader maps SR-040 to rejected or open and 17 to accepted; only the 17 are confirmed
- id: EX-5
  given: three backlog records judged beyond or contradicts
  when: the turn ends
  then: none is on a feature card, none raises a card, and the pass's end line counts the three
- id: EX-6
  given: the pass is judging 160 records and has done 10
  when: the owner looks at the status line
  then: it reads "judging the backlog · 10/160"
- id: EX-7
  given: the model becomes unreachable midway through the pass
  when: the next turn starts
  then: the pass resumes from where it stopped and the records already judged are not judged again
