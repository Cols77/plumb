id: SR-098
title: A mixed answer list confirms its yes items
statement: 'When one owner message answers several records, some yes and some with corrections or reservations,
  the reader (a language model, INT-028) gives each record the reply showed its own answer from the whole
  message: accepted, rejected, change asked or open. plumb confirms only the records read as accepted
  (at the reader''s threshold, with the owner''s words found in the logged message, at the version shown)
  and leaves every other record unconfirmed. The agent''s relay confirms nothing, and no fixed word rules
  place corrections or reservations.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 'the reply showed INT-006, INT-013 and INT-016 and the owner''s logged message "INT-006: yes.
    INT-013: no. INT-016: We don''t want token budget."'
  when: the reader reads INT-006 as accepted, INT-013 as rejected and INT-016 as change asked
  then: INT-006 is confirmed from the owner's words and INT-013 and INT-016 stay unconfirmed
- id: EX-2
  given: the same message
  when: the agent runs plumb relay on any of those records
  then: it is refused and nothing is recorded
- id: EX-3
  given: 'the owner''s message "INT-006: yes. Actually I''d rather sleep on all of this."'
  when: the reader reads INT-006 as open, or its reading is unclear
  then: nothing is confirmed
- id: EX-4
  given: 'the owner''s message "INT-006: yes" then "INT-016:" at the end of a line and "We don''t want
    token budget" at the start of the next'
  when: the reader reads INT-006 as accepted and INT-016 as change asked
  then: INT-006 is confirmed and INT-016 is not
- id: EX-5
  given: the owner's message "Confirm SR-097, SR-098, INT-24" after a reply that showed SR-097 and SR-098
  when: the reader reads SR-097 and SR-098 as accepted
  then: both are confirmed, and INT-24, which names no shown record, confirms nothing
