  form asks one choice over supported, beyond and contradicts and reads the probability of supported;
  calibration records which form it measured and a calibration from one form is never used for the other'

  given: jev.output is unset
  when: JEV is asked

- id: EX-2
  given: jev.output is choice
  when: JEV is asked
  then: it is asked the three-way choice and the probability of supported is used; the log names the form
- id: EX-3
  given: a calibration measured with yes_no
  when: jev.output is switched to choice
  then: auto-approval does not run on the old calibration and jev status says a calibration for the choice
    form is needed
