---
id: SR-118
title: Older records' words are found by a model cascade and shown, not...
statement: For a record whose quote is not in a hook-logged prompt, JEV pairs it with the most likely
  owner message in past session transcripts, Sonnet picks the exact span, and Opus examines records whose
  words do not support them; the found words, their date and session are shown on the record's card. The
  exact-match check stays a text comparison. A transcript match alone never confirms; the owner's press
  or logged words do.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: INT-009 whose words are in a transcript of 4 Oct
  when: recovery runs
  then: its card shows that message's exact span and date, and INT-009 stays proposed until the owner
    presses Accept
- id: EX-2
  given: INT-015 whose words are in no transcript
  when: recovery runs
  then: its card says the words were not found
- id: EX-3
  given: a record whose best-matching message does not support it
  when: Opus examines it
  then: the card says the record may misread the owner and proposes a rewording
- id: EX-4
  given: a record whose best message holds only a span of fewer than four words that supports it, and
    the message is longer
  when: recovery runs
  then: the card says the words found are too short to show, and no span is shown
- id: EX-5
  given: past transcripts holding compaction summaries, transcript-only lines, tool results, command caveats,
    system reminders, task notifications, cross-session messages, and a worktree's or another project's
    transcript folder
  when: recovery reads the owner's messages
  then: only messages the owner typed in this project's own folder are read
- id: EX-6
  given: INT-009 the owner already confirmed
  when: recovery runs for INT-009 by name or for a requirement deriving from it
  then: INT-009 is skipped as already confirmed and no model is asked
