id: SR-268
title: The browser study room holds the questions that need studying, one...
feature: FEAT-007
statement: A local browser study room, for one project, shows the questions that need more than the question
  tool holds, one at a time with Back and Next, grouped as waiting, being processed, blocked (with the
  reason) and done; it never opens by itself, and the agent says where to answer a question that waits
  there. An answered question leaves the waiting list at once; one whose record changed after it was shown
  is blocked, not applied. The room's browser tests run only when asked for.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: three questions wait, the second carrying a diagram
  when: the owner opens the study room and presses Next
  then: it shows the second question with its diagram above it and '2 of 3'
- id: EX-2
  given: the room shows the last question
  when: the owner presses Next
  then: it stays on the last one; Back moves to the one before
- id: EX-3
  given: no question waits
  when: the owner opens the room
  then: it says nothing waits and draws no Back or Next
- id: EX-4
  given: questions wait in the room
  when: a turn ends
  then: the room does not open by itself; the agent says where to answer, and the turn-end plumb line
    carries no question count (SR-363)
- id: EX-5
  given: the owner answers a question
  when: the next prompt or turn end lists what waits
  then: that question is not among them, and the room shows it under 'done' once its answer is applied
    (under 'being processed' only while applying takes a noticeable time)
- id: EX-6
  given: three questions waiting and none answered
  when: the room is drawn
  then: all three show under 'waiting for you' and the other groups are empty or hidden
- id: EX-7
  given: the record behind a question changed after it was shown
  when: the owner's answer is submitted
  then: the answer is not applied to the changed content; the question shows under 'blocked' with that
    reason
- id: EX-8
  given: the test suite is run with no path, or naming the tests folder
  when: pytest collects
  then: no study-room browser test is collected; naming tests/browser or one of its files, or passing
    --browser, collects them
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended for the owner to decide: the AI serves the study room on a scratch project holding
    three questions; the owner opens it, steps through them with Back and Next, and sees each under its
    group.'
  kind: live-session
