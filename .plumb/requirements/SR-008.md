title: The merge boundary refuses unconfirmed touched requirements and lists approvals
feature: FEAT-001
statement: 'plumb validate --merge BASE exits 4 while any requirement touched by the change (through a
  changed record, a changed linked file or an SR: trailer in the range) is not confirmed, names the plumb
  approve command to run, and lists every approval in the range with agent-relayed ones marked with their
  quote and prompt digest.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: SR-001 is edited on the branch and not confirmed
  when: plumb validate --merge BASE runs
  then: it exits 4 and names plumb approve SR-001
- id: EX-2
  given: the touched SR-001 is approved at its current hash
  when: plumb validate --merge BASE runs
  then: it exits 0
- id: EX-3
  given: an owner approval and an agent-relayed approval in the range
  when: the merge boundary runs
  then: both are listed and the relayed one shows its quote and prompt digest
