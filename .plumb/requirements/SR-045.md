---
id: SR-045
title: A bounded hidden-dependency scan shows records that may silently depend on a change, as hints only
feature: FEAT-002
statement: When a confirmed record or an authoritative document changes, a scan of at most 5 candidates
  and 1 second proposes records or documents with no link to it that may depend on what changed, confirmed
  by the backend, and returns them as drift hints; they are never staleness marks, edges or interruptions.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: confirmed SR-004 changes and SR-012 shares its key terms with no link
  when: plumb drift runs
  then: a hint says SR-012 may depend on the change to SR-004 and no link state changes
- id: EX-2
  given: 50 candidate records overlap the change
  when: the scan runs
  then: at most 5 are judged and the scan ends within 1 second
