---
id: SR-212
title: An owner answer is captured with the question it answered, once,...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'plumb captures intent only when the owner gives it: /plumb intent, an answer to an intent
  conflict, a question-tool or study-room answer, or a message answering what the last reply showed. Each
  piece is kept as a pre-intent with the question, options and context it answered, and the judge receives
  the question with the answer. A clear answer closes its question without raising another; an unclear
  or partial one leaves it open; a dismissed one captures nothing.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the agent showed the 'where you answer a conflict' question in text with options 1, 2 and 3
  when: the owner replies '2'
  then: the question is answered with option 2, citing '2', and no new question about the reply '2' appears
- id: EX-2
  given: the agent's reply asked 'Merge now or after the review?'
  when: the owner replies 'after the review'
  then: the answer is captured with that question and no further question is raised for the reply
- id: EX-3
  given: a question with options 1, 2 and 3 shown in text
  when: the owner replies 'maybe 2, but I am not sure about the skills'
  then: the question stays open and nothing is captured as its answer
- id: EX-4
  given: a question about the reminder timing only
  when: the owner replies 'yes'
  then: only that question is answered; nothing the owner was not asked about is captured as their answer
- id: EX-5
  given: the agent asks 'Run the slow tests now or later?' with the question tool
  when: the owner picks 'later'
  then: plumb's log holds that question, both options and 'later' as the owner's answer in this session
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner's question-tool answer says 'yes, that is what I meant' about a requirement
  when: it is logged
  then: it is captured as a pre-intent with its question, and the requirement stays proposed until the
    judge cascade's verdict
- id: EX-7
  given: the owner dismisses the question without answering
  when: the tool returns
  then: nothing is logged as an answer
- id: EX-8
  given: an intent quoting "A" with the question offering A. Mostly in the background, B. A checkpoint
    every turn
  when: JEV judges the interpretation
  then: the claim it receives holds the question and the answer, not "A" alone
- id: EX-9
  given: a capture whose quote is "A" and no question
  when: the capture runs
  then: it warns that the answer needs its question and still records the intent
- id: EX-10
  given: a quote of a full sentence
  when: JEV judges it
  then: nothing is added to the quote
- id: EX-11
  given: a bare answer said to reply to a proposal plumb never showed
  when: the agent records it
  then: 'it is refused: the proposal it claims to answer was never shown'
- id: EX-12
  given: no question is open and no intent loop is running
  when: the owner writes 'fix the typo in the README'
  then: no intent is captured from it; the request goes on as ordinary work
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended for the owner to decide: in a live session on a scratch repository, the agent asks
    one question through the question tool and the owner picks an option (the plumb line says the answer
    was kept with its question and no new question follows); the owner then answers a text question with
    ''maybe, not sure'' (the question comes back once).'
  kind: live-session
