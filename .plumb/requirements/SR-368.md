title: plumb itself offers a study asked in plain words
statement: When the owner's message asks for a study in plain words, plumb's prompt hook proposes the
  study idea itself, as asked, with the owner's topic as its title, so the band offers it at once and
  the owner picks the depth there. The agent is told the idea is on the band and to write nothing until
  the owner accepts it; it never has to find out how plumb makes studies.

    it is offered

  prompt: bcee0f05b14e8bf4

  when: the prompt hook reads it
  then: no study is offered
