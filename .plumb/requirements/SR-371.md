---
id: SR-371
title: A study being written shows its progress in the band
statement: 'While the AI writes a study the owner accepted (Markdown, diagrams or video, which takes minutes),
  the band shows one line ''writing study: <title> · <step>'' with a bar that fills as the AI reports
  its steps, and the line goes when the study is added.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the owner accepted a study idea and the AI has reported no step yet
  when: the band is next drawn
  then: 'it shows ''writing study: <title> · starting'' and no bar'
- id: EX-2
  given: the AI ran 'plumb study progress <slug> --step "scene 1" --done 3 --total 5'
  when: the band is next drawn
  then: 'it shows ''writing study: <title> · scene 1'' with a bar three fifths full and 60%'
- id: EX-3
  given: a study is being written with progress reported
  when: '''plumb study add'' writes the study'
  then: the study is new, the progress is gone and the band shows 'new study' instead of the writing line
- id: EX-4
  given: a study that is not accepted
  when: the AI runs 'plumb study progress' on it
  then: plumb refuses and writes nothing
