---
id: SR-371
title: A study being written shows its progress in the band
statement: 'While the AI writes a study the owner accepted (Markdown, diagrams or video, which takes minutes),
  the band''s studies chip shows a study is being written, and the writing section of the studies class
  of plumb''s pane lists it as a row ''… <title> · <step>'' with a bar: empty before the AI reports its
  first step, then filling as it reports them; the row goes when the study is added.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the owner accepted a study idea and the AI has reported no step yet
  when: the studies class is next drawn
  then: its writing section shows '… <title> · starting' and an empty bar
- id: EX-2
  given: the AI ran 'plumb study progress <slug> --step "scene 1" --done 3 --total 5'
  when: the studies class is next drawn
  then: its writing section shows '… <title> · scene 1' with a bar three fifths full and 60%
- id: EX-3
  given: a study with clips was added while progress is reported
  when: the clips render and are checked
  then: the writing row stays on the new study, and goes when the last clip passes its check or 'plumb
    study progress <slug> --finish' runs; a study with no clip loses it when added
- id: EX-4
  given: a study that is not accepted and not being written
  when: the AI runs 'plumb study progress' on it
  then: plumb refuses and writes nothing
proof:
  plan: After the landing, the owner opens a study with a diagram in the room and sees the diagram drawn,
    scrolling when wide and keeping its size
  kind: live-session
  post_merge: true
