id: SR-105
title: What awaits the owner comes as cards with suggested resolutions;...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'The end-of-turn summary shows the owner, in order: what they confirmed by their words, one
  line naming what was settled from their words (each with Undo), and cards; each card names the topic
  in the owner''s terms, the issue, the evidence, and two or three suggested resolutions with the recommended
  one first, written by the agent from plumb''s facts; card kinds are clarify, trial batch, goes beyond
  your words, contradicts, and couldn''t check. A settled record counts as confirmed in status, the merge
  boundary and validation, and is listed apart. Undo is a button or the typed /plumb undo, never a model''s
  reading of prose.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: two records settled this turn
  when: the turn ends
  then: 'one line reads "Settled from your words: <title>; <title>" with Undo for each'
- id: EX-2
  given: the judge returned beyond for a read-back
  when: the turn ends
  then: a card names what goes beyond the owner's words and offers keep it, drop it, or reword
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner undoes a settled record
  when: the next turn ends
  then: the record is proposed again and shown as a card
- id: EX-4
  given: a contradicts card offering two versions
  when: the owner answers "the second, plus the first's date"
  then: the reader maps it to change carrying that version, and the card's question is answered
- id: EX-5
  given: a trial batch of three items
  when: the owner answers "yes to the first two, no to the third"
  then: the reader maps accepted, accepted, rejected; the third is rejected as a misreading
- id: EX-6
  given: a settled record
  when: the owner writes "undo that" in prose
  then: nothing is undone; plumb names the Undo button and the /plumb undo command
