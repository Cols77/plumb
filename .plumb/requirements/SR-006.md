---
id: SR-006
title: Logs are append-only and hash-chained; tampering is reported
feature: FEAT-001
statement: events.jsonl and evidence.jsonl are append-only JSONL streams in which each entry carries its
  digest and the previous entry's digest; plumb validate reports a modified, removed or reordered line,
  while a union-merged fork from parallel branches is accepted.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an events log with three chained entries
  when: a committed line is edited by hand
  then: plumb validate reports a log error naming that line
- id: EX-2
  given: two branches that each appended entries
  when: git merges them with merge=union
  then: the merged log is accepted
- id: EX-3
  given: two processes appending at once
  when: both append
  then: the lines do not interleave
