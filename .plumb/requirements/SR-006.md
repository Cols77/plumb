---
id: SR-006
title: Logs are append-only and hash-chained; tampering is reported
feature: FEAT-001
statement: Every log entry is its own hash-chained file under .plumb/log/<kind>/, in two streams (evidence
  for test runs and merge evidence, events for every other kind); each entry carries its digest and the
  previous entry's digest; plumb validate reports a modified entry, a removed entry that a later one names
  and an unparsable file, while a forked chain from parallel branches is accepted; legacy events.jsonl
  and evidence.jsonl lines are still read and checked until they are migrated.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an events log with three chained entries
  when: a committed line is edited by hand
  then: plumb validate reports a log error naming that line
- id: EX-2
  given: two branches that each appended entries
  when: git merges them
  then: no log file conflicts, and the forked chain is accepted
- id: EX-3
  given: two processes appending at once
  when: both append
  then: the lines do not interleave
