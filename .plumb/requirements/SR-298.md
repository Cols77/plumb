---
id: SR-298
title: The judge backlog is queued at session start and at each landing,...
feature: FEAT-002
statement: 'At session start and in each landing job, every record waiting on a judge with no job queued
  for its current version is queued, in jobs below the judge''s size limit, keeping an intent''s records
  in one job when they fit. A job over the limit is split before it is sent, never failed for its size.
  With no judge reachable, SR-245 holds: one line, nothing decided by rule.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 193 records waiting on a judge and none queued
  when: a session starts
  then: jobs for all 193 are queued in the background and the session start is not slowed
- id: EX-2
  given: an intent whose records make a 62,000-character job
  when: it is queued
  then: it goes as two or more jobs each under the limit, and none fails as too large
- id: EX-3
  given: a record already queued at its current version
  when: the session starts
  then: it is not queued twice
- id: EX-4
  given: no judge is reachable
  when: the backlog would be queued
  then: one line says which judge and why, and nothing else changes
proof:
  plan: 'Session start with records waiting: the status shows the waiting count falling to zero as the
    jobs run; a 62,000-character intent is judged in split jobs.'
