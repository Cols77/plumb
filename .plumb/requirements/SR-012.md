id: SR-012
title: What needs the owner at the end of a turn reaches them as cards,...
feature: FEAT-001
statement: 'At the end of a turn, each thing that needs the owner reaches them as one of the two card
  shapes of SR-129 (a read-back or a choice), answered in the owner''s own words or with a button. A card
  is raised only for a real intent change, a record the judge could not settle, or a regression. Records
  that settled show nothing (SR-134: no digest). The end-of-turn hook delivers the cards; it prints no
  list-style alignment summary of proposed records.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a turn that proposed SR-030, which the judge settled, and touched nothing confirmed
  when: the turn ends
  then: the owner sees nothing about SR-030, no digest and no card
- id: EX-2
  given: a test that passed with recorded evidence now fails
  when: the turn ends
  then: the owner gets a regression card naming the test, what it checks and the suggested resolutions
- id: EX-3
  given: an intent captured this turn that the judge could not settle
  when: the turn ends
  then: the owner gets a card with what it says, what will and won't be done, its labelled assumptions
    and suggested resolutions, never only an ID or a line in a summary
- id: EX-4
  given: a turn that proposed twelve requirements of one feature, none settled
  when: the turn ends
  then: the owner gets read-back cards (SR-129), not a list of twelve lines
- id: EX-5
  given: nothing needs the owner and nothing settled
  when: the turn ends
  then: the end-of-turn hook shows nothing to the owner
