id: SR-012
title: What needs the owner at the end of a turn reaches them as cards,...
feature: FEAT-001
statement: 'At the end of a turn, each thing that needs the owner reaches them as a card (SR-105): the
  topic, the issue in plain words, and suggested resolutions, answered in the owner''s own words or with
  a button once the SP7 panel exists. A card is raised only for a real intent change, an unconfirmed decision
  that matters, a record the judge could not settle, or a regression. Records that settled show only in
  the one-line settled digest with Undo. The end-of-turn hook delivers the cards; it prints no list-style
  alignment summary of proposed records.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a turn that proposed SR-030, which the judge settled, and touched nothing confirmed
  when: the turn ends
  then: the owner sees SR-030 only in the settled digest with Undo, and no card
- id: EX-2
  given: a test that passed with recorded evidence now fails
  when: the turn ends
  then: the owner gets a regression card naming the test, what it checks and the suggested resolutions
- id: EX-3
  given: an intent captured this turn that the judge could not settle
  when: the turn ends
  then: the owner gets a card with what it says, what will and won't be done, its labelled assumptions
    and suggested resolutions, never only an ID or a line in a summary
- id: EX-4
  given: a turn that proposed twelve requirements of one feature, none settled
  when: the turn ends
  then: the owner gets one card for that feature (DEC-029), not a list of twelve lines
- id: EX-5
  given: nothing needs the owner and nothing settled
  when: the turn ends
  then: the end-of-turn hook shows nothing to the owner
