---
id: SR-012
title: The end-of-turn summary is short, shows proposed records, asks only on triggers
feature: FEAT-001
statement: The stop hook prints a short alignment summary listing newly proposed read-backs and examples
  with status proposed, and asks the owner a question only on a real intent change, an unconfirmed decision
  that matters, or a regression.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a turn that proposed SR-030 and touched nothing confirmed
  when: the stop hook runs
  then: the summary lists SR-030 as proposed and asks no question
- id: EX-2
  given: a test that passed with recorded evidence now fails
  when: the stop hook runs
  then: the summary asks about the regression
- id: EX-3
  given: a turn that proposed an INT with a won't item and a labelled assumption, and an SR with three
    examples including an edge case
  when: the stop hook runs
  then: the summary shows the won't item, the assumption and every example, and names any record past
    the cap with plumb show instead of hiding it behind a count
