---
id: SR-021
title: 'Judgment is advisory and has no fallback: an unreachable judge...'
feature: FEAT-001
statement: plumb's judgment interface uses the configured judge (JEV, or a judge model); when it is not
  configured, has no key, times out or fails, plumb raises an error naming the judge and the cause, and
  no heuristic answers in its place (INT-029). Its judgments are advisory only. A hook reports the error
  without blocking the prompt.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: no JEV key is configured
  when: the prompt hook judges an intent change
  then: plumb says no judgment model is configured and raises an error naming the setting; no rule-based
    judge answers
- id: EX-2
  given: a JEV key is configured and JEV times out
  when: a judgment is requested
  then: plumb raises an error naming JEV and the timeout; nothing falls back to the heuristic, and the
    hook reports the error without blocking the prompt
- id: EX-3
  given: thresholds validated against jev-1.13.0
  when: JEV resolves typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917, or jev-1.14
  then: the dated OpenRouter name is the same version and a confident answer is accepted with version_drift
    false; jev-1.14 is drift and the answer escalates
