---
id: SR-021
title: 'Judgment is optional and advisory: heuristic default, JEV only with a key'
feature: FEAT-001
statement: plumb's judgment interface defaults to a heuristic backend, uses JEV only when a key is configured,
  falls back on any JEV failure, and its judgments are advisory only.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: no JEV key is configured
  when: the prompt hook judges an intent change
  then: the heuristic backend answers
- id: EX-2
  given: a JEV key is configured and JEV times out
  when: a judgment is requested
  then: plumb falls back to the heuristic and the hook does not fail
