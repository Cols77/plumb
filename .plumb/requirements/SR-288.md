---
id: SR-288
title: A trace.exempt glob added on a branch has no approval route yet
feature: FEAT-001
statement: plumb approve --exempt was the only route that confirmed a trace.exempt glob added on a branch,
  and it is refused now (SR-210); the judge and the question tool approve records only. Until a route
  is chosen (one question-tool question in the owner's words with no ids, or a judge verdict that writes
  the approval entry), plumb validate --merge keeps such a glob unhonoured, exits 4, and tells the owner
  nothing can approve it yet and to remove it to merge. An approval entry written before keeps confirming
  its globs.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: confirmed SR-001 at the merge base and a changed src/z.py no requirement owns
  when: the agent appends src/** to trace.exempt in .plumb/config.yaml and the merge runs
  then: src/z.py is still listed as untraced, "trace.exempt src/**" is listed as added in this range,
    the merge says nothing can approve a new trace.exempt glob yet and to remove it to merge, and exits
    4
- id: EX-2
  given: the same range
  when: anyone runs plumb approve --exempt, with or without the session marker
  then: 'it prints "plumb: approve no longer exists; nothing was recorded", exits 3, and the merge still
    exits 4'
- id: EX-3
  given: the same range and an approval entry written before that lists src/**
  when: the merge runs
  then: the glob is honoured and the merge exits 0
