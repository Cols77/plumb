id: SR-019
title: A failure that passes on a same-tree rerun is recorded as flaky
feature: FEAT-001
statement: When a test fails and passes on a rerun on the same tree, plumb records it as flaky with both
  attempts, not as a pass or a regression.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a test fails once
  when: it passes on the same-tree rerun
  then: evidence records flaky-pass with both attempts
