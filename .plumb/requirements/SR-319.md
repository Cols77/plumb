---
id: SR-319
title: The study room plays a study's video
statement: The study room plays a study's committed video inline with play, pause and seek; a video that
  is missing, unreadable or outside the project is replaced by a fallback line and the study's text stays
  readable.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a study with a rendered video
  when: the owner opens it in the room
  then: the video shows above the text with play, pause and seek
- id: EX-2
  given: a study whose video file is missing or not a valid mp4
  when: it is opened
  then: a fallback line says the video is unavailable and the text is shown
- id: EX-3
  given: a study naming a video path outside the project
  when: it is opened
  then: nothing is loaded from that path; the fallback line is shown
proof:
  plan: In the live loop, the finished video plays in the room; a study with its mp4 removed shows the
    fallback line; screenshots opened on the owner's screen.
  kind: live-session
