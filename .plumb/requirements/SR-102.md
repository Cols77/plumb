---
id: SR-102
title: A judge model decides whether a record is supported by the owner's...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: A model independent of the coding agent judges each new or changed record against its premise
  (the owner's whole logged message or messages, the question a short answer answered, a parent intent
  the owner confirmed) and the owner's confirmed or settled records, leaving labelled assumptions out
  of what is judged, and returns supported, beyond (naming what the owner's words do not support) or contradicts
  (naming what and with which record), with a confidence. A supported verdict whose premise is an option
  picked among several is never settled; it goes to a trial or confirm card (SR-091).
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: INT-016's first read-back (one line per record, a token budget) and the owner's message asking
    for a smarter way that does not bloat the context
  when: the judge runs
  then: 'beyond: one line per record and a token budget'
- id: EX-2
  given: INT-013's first read-back (maintenance mode) and the owner's "consider stopping developments
    on pi-agent-factory if it takes too much RAM"
  when: the judge runs
  then: 'beyond: maintenance mode is stated as decided'
- id: EX-3
  given: INT-006's read-back and the owner's answer B with its question
  when: the judge runs
  then: supported
- id: EX-4
  given: a requirement whose example contradicts an example of a confirmed requirement
  when: the judge runs
  then: contradicts, naming both examples
- id: EX-5
  given: a decision whose only premise is the owner's answer A picked among options A and B
  when: the judge returns supported
  then: the record is not settled and waits as a trial or confirm card
