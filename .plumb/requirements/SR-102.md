---
id: SR-102
title: A judge model decides whether a record is supported by the owner's...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: A model independent of the coding agent, asked whether the owner would object to the record
  given their words, judges each new or changed record (one judge job per capture covers all of them)
  against its premise (the owner's whole logged message or messages, the question a short answer answered,
  a parent intent the owner confirmed) and the owner's confirmed or settled records, leaving labelled
  assumptions out of what is judged, and returns supported, beyond (naming what the owner's words do not
  support) or contradicts (naming what and with which record), with its own confidence, logged with the
  verdict. The premise message is committed to the repository log (text and digest) when the record first
  takes it as premise, so it outlives the session. A supported verdict whose premise is an option picked
  among several is never settled; it goes to a trial or confirm card (SR-091).
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: INT-016's first read-back (one line per record, a token budget) and the owner's message asking
    for a smarter way that does not bloat the context
  when: the judge runs
  then: 'beyond: one line per record and a token budget'
- id: EX-2
  given: INT-013's first read-back (maintenance mode) and the owner's "consider stopping developments
    on pi-agent-factory if it takes too much RAM"
  when: the judge runs
  then: 'beyond: maintenance mode is stated as decided'
- id: EX-3
  given: INT-006's read-back and the owner's answer B with its question
  when: the judge runs
  then: supported
- id: EX-4
  given: a requirement whose example contradicts an example of a confirmed requirement
  when: the judge runs
  then: contradicts, naming both examples
- id: EX-5
  given: a decision whose only premise is the owner's answer A picked among options A and B
  when: the judge returns supported
  then: the record is not settled and waits as a trial or confirm card
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner answered "A" to a stored question offering A and B
  when: the judge runs on the record built from that answer
  then: the question is part of the premise it is given, and the judge is asked whether the owner would
    object to the record given that question and answer
- id: EX-7
  given: a capture writes an intent and six requirements from one owner message
  when: the capture finishes
  then: one judge job covers the seven records, and the message's text and digest are in the committed
    log
- id: EX-8
  given: a fresh clone with no session log and a record whose premise message was committed
  when: the judge runs
  then: it finds the premise in the committed log and judges the record against it
