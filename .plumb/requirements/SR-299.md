---
id: SR-299
title: A judge question that forgets its records still reaches the owner
feature: FEAT-002
statement: 'The judge''s answer format requires each question to name the records it settles; when a question
  still comes back without them, plumb takes the records of the job instead of rejecting it. A question
  without a quote of the owner''s words is usable: it reaches the owner without the "You said" line. Only
  a question missing its options or using plumb''s internal words is retried once; still unusable after
  the retry, it goes to a debug case and its records keep waiting until the debug case fixes the question.
  The verdicts already given are applied.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 'the judge answers like the 2026-10-16 job: three questions, each without the records it settles'
  when: plumb reads the answer
  then: the three questions reach the owner through the question tool and no verdict waits
- id: EX-2
  given: the judge's answer format given to the model
  when: the judge model is asked
  then: the format is lenient, a question needs only its text, and the judge's instructions and plumb's
    retry enforce the records and 2 to 4 options (DEC-208)
- id: EX-3
  given: a question with one option only
  when: plumb reads it
  then: the job is retried once; if it fails again a debug case is written, the question's records keep
    waiting, and the other verdicts are applied
- id: EX-4
  given: a question with options but no quote of the owner's words
  when: plumb reads it
  then: it reaches the owner without the "You said" line and is not retried
proof:
  plan: Replaying the logged 2026-10-16 answer gives three owner questions and no waiting line.
  kind: command
