---
id: SR-028
title: A requirement widened by a link, or a path exempted, goes to the owner at the merge
feature: FEAT-001
statement: Links are left out of a record's hash, so an approval of a requirement also lists the implemented_by
  and verified_by ends it owns at that moment. plumb validate --merge BASE treats a requirement that gained
  an owning end in the range (by plumb link or a frontmatter edit) that no approval of it at its current
  hash lists as an extension of it, shows it as extended with each new end in the trigger-2 question,
  and exits 4 until the owner approves it again. A trace.exempt glob added in the range is listed in the
  same question and not honoured until the owner confirms it (plumb approve --exempt in the owner's terminal,
  or plumb relay --exempt on an affirmation that names the exemption). A path that was source under the
  merge base's config stays source, so narrowing source_dirs, test_dirs or the infrastructure globs hides
  no change.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: confirmed SR-001 at the merge base and a changed src/z.py that no requirement owns
  when: the agent, under the session marker, runs plumb link SR-001 implemented_by src/z.py and the merge
    runs
  then: SR-001 stays confirmed, but the merge lists "SR-001 (extended)" with "new link in this range implemented_by
    src/z.py" and exits 4; after the owner approves SR-001 it exits 0
- id: EX-2
  given: the same range
  when: the agent adds the implemented_by src/z.py link by editing SR-001's frontmatter
  then: the merge lists the same new link and exits 4
- id: EX-3
  given: the same range
  when: the agent appends src/** to trace.exempt in .plumb/config.yaml
  then: src/z.py is still listed as untraced, "trace.exempt src/**" is listed as added in this range,
    the merge exits 4, plumb approve --exempt is refused under the marker, and after the owner runs it
    the merge exits 0 and lists the exemption among the approvals
- id: EX-4
  given: the same range
  when: the agent sets test_dirs to [src, tests]
  then: src/z.py is still listed as untraced and the merge exits 4
- id: EX-5
  given: a relayed approval of a trace.exempt change
  when: the owner's quote affirms but does not name the exemption
  then: the relay is refused; a quote such as "ok, exempt src/gen/*" records it
