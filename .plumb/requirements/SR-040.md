---
id: SR-040
title: plumb triage groups proposed records by faithfulness and the owner confirms the low-risk group
  in one step
feature: FEAT-002
statement: plumb triage scores each proposed record for faithfulness to the owner's words (JEV or the
  heuristic) and shows the low-risk group first and riskier records individually; decisions that matter,
  escalated specs and their members, and edits of owner-confirmed records are always individual. plumb
  triage --approve low-risk in the owner's terminal approves exactly the listed hashes; in a session,
  the one-line offer names up to about 5 records by title, then +N more (see plumb triage) (R13 Q2), and
  an affirming reply is relayed with plumb relay --group, which approves exactly the pinned list. Triage
  never approves by itself.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: INT-017 with a read-back covered by its quote and a DEC with matters true
  when: plumb triage runs
  then: INT-017 is in the low-risk group and the DEC is shown individually
- id: EX-2
  given: the session marker is set
  when: plumb triage --approve low-risk runs
  then: it exits 3 and writes nothing
- id: EX-3
  given: the last summary offered the low-risk group INT-017, SR-034
  when: the owner replies "ok" and the agent runs plumb relay --group low-risk with that quote
  then: one agent-relayed approval pins exactly those two hashes
- id: EX-4
  given: no judgment backend answers
  when: plumb triage runs
  then: it says "no judgments available" and lists every record individually
- id: EX-5
  given: an intent whose quote no hook-logged prompt holds (captured without --prompt, or self-recorded)
  when: plumb triage runs
  then: it is shown individually as quote not verified, never rated faithful to your words
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner's logged words "Given 2, when neg is called, then it returns -2. Do not change add."
  when: a proposed read-back will change add, or an example says it returns 2
  then: plumb triage shows that record individually as contradicts your words, never in the low-risk group
