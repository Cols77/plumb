id: SR-040
title: plumb triage groups proposed records by faithfulness and the owner confirms the low-risk group
  in one step
feature: FEAT-002
statement: 'plumb triage shows each proposed record with the judge''s verdict (SR-102): supported records
  form the low-risk group, shown first; records the judge found beyond the owner''s words, contradicting
  them, or could not check, decisions that matter, escalated specs and their members, and edits of owner-confirmed
  records are always individual. Triage never approves by itself: in the owner''s terminal, plumb triage
  --approve low-risk approves exactly the listed hashes after a y/N; in a session the one-line offer names
  up to about 5 records by title, then +N more, and the owner''s reply to it is read by the reader (SR-101),
  which confirms exactly the offered list it accepted.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: INT-017 whose read-back the judge found supported and a decision that matters
  when: plumb triage runs
  then: INT-017 is in the low-risk group and the decision is shown individually
- id: EX-2
  given: the session marker is set
  when: plumb triage --approve low-risk runs
  then: it exits 3 and writes nothing
- id: EX-3
  given: the last summary offered the low-risk group INT-017 and SR-034
  when: the owner replies "ok" and the reader accepts it
  then: exactly those two are confirmed with the owner's words
- id: EX-4
  given: no judge model answers
  when: plumb triage runs
  then: it says no verdicts are available and lists every record individually, rating none low-risk
- id: EX-5
  given: an intent whose quote no hook-logged prompt holds
  when: plumb triage runs
  then: it is shown individually as quote not verified, never as supported by your words
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner's logged words "Given 2, when neg is called, then it returns -2. Do not change add."
    and a proposed read-back that will change add
  when: plumb triage runs
  then: the judge's verdict is contradicts and the record is shown individually with what it contradicts,
    never in the low-risk group
