---
id: SR-219
title: A principle is asked once and settles the questions that hinge on it
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'When several open questions, or the gaps of a request in the intent loop, hinge on one principle,
  plumb asks the principle once through the question tool, saying in plain words what it would settle,
  instead of each detail. Once answered, the judge reads every record of those questions with the owner''s
  answer: none is asked again, except one it cannot settle from the answer''s words, which is asked as
  its own question; an answer the owner says was wrong is reopened; the owner''s turn-end text does not
  count them (SR-373).'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: twenty open stale-link questions hinge on what counts as the owner's answer
  when: the turn ends
  then: one question-tool question asks that principle and says it would settle twenty questions; the
    twenty are not asked
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner answers the principle question
  when: the judge next reads the records
  then: every record of its questions is judged with the owner's answer and none is asked again
- id: EX-3
  given: a dependent question the judge cannot settle from the answer's words
  when: the judge reads it
  then: it is asked again as its own specific question
- id: EX-4
  given: an answer was applied to a question and the owner disagrees
  when: the owner says in conversation that it was wrong
  then: the question is reopened and asked again
- id: EX-5
  given: the owner opens /plumb intent on a request whose five open gaps all hinge on one principle
  when: the clarification runs
  then: one question asks that principle and names in plain words the five gaps it settles; the five are
    not asked
- id: EX-6
  given: a detailed gap that no principle settles
  when: the clarification runs
  then: it is asked on its own, as a specific question
- id: EX-7
  given: an approved record is edited so its meaning changes older intent, with three records derived
    from the same principle
  when: the change needs the owner's arbitration
  then: one question about the principle is asked with the derived records named by title, never one question
    per record
- id: EX-8
  given: only one open question hinges on a principle
  when: the turn ends
  then: the question is asked as it is, with no extra principle question
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended for the owner to decide: in a live session, raise several issues that hinge on one
    principle; the owner sees a single question-tool question about the principle, answers it, and the
    next plumb line counts what was withdrawn, repinned and reworded, with nothing else asked.'
  kind: live-session
