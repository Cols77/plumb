title: The owner's own answer settles what it answers
statement: An answer the owner gave, quoted in a record with where it came from, settles every question
  it answers from the moment it is recorded, while the record still waits for the judge; the judge checks
  only that the record's wording matches the owner's words. A record only the agent wrote settles nothing
  until it is approved. A question already settled this way is never put to the owner.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the owner said on 2026-10-18 'a typed answer counts; the judge asks again only when it truly
    leaves the question open', recorded in a decision the judge has not approved
  when: the judge raises a question that answer settles
  then: the question is settled from that answer and never reaches the owner
- id: EX-2
  given: a proposed decision the agent wrote, with no quote from the owner
  when: a question hinges on it
  then: the decision settles nothing and the question is asked
- id: EX-3
  given: the judge finds a record's wording does not match the owner's quoted words
  when: it checks the record
  then: the answer settles nothing, and the mismatch is raised as one question

    Hide staying hidden; waiting questions arriving through the question tool, at most three per turn
    and grouped by principle, each offering to study it in the room when there is more to see; no question
    the owner already answered coming back; and the turn-end line counting what still waits.'
