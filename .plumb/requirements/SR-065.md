id: SR-065
title: /plumb confirm prepares a confirmation the owner's own words then give
feature: FEAT-005
statement: 'plumb confirm never reads words. Without arguments it prepares the triage offer and the riskier
  records as cards, which the confirm turn''s stop hook shows in the summary before anything can be confirmed.
  A /plumb confirm naming IDs is an exact owner action: it confirms those records at once, recorded as
  passed on from the owner''s typed command. What the owner says in prose about those cards is read by
  the reader (SR-101), not by this command.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: any state
  when: plumb confirm runs with no arguments
  then: no approval event is written, and the triage offer and the riskier records are prepared as cards
    that the stop hook shows
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner typed "/plumb confirm SR-034"
  when: the command runs
  then: SR-034 is confirmed at once, recorded as passed on from the owner's typed command
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner typed "/plumb confirm the login thing"
  when: the command runs
  then: nothing is confirmed and plumb says to name IDs or to answer in a message
