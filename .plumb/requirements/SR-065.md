id: SR-065
title: /plumb confirm prepares a confirmation the owner's own words then give
feature: FEAT-005
statement: plumb confirm never writes an approval. Without words it prepares the triage offer and the
  riskier records as cards; with plain words it resolves them and prepares each match as an intent card.
  The confirm turn's stop hook shows the owner every prepared card and the offer line in the summary and
  only then marks them shown and the group relayable; the owner's next affirming prompt is relayed under
  R2. A /plumb confirm naming IDs is itself an affirmation naming them and is relayed at once.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: any state
  when: plumb confirm the login thing runs
  then: no approval event is written, the matched records are in the stated set not yet shown, and the
    turn's stop hook shows them to the owner as full intent cards and marks them shown
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner typed "/plumb confirm SR-034"
  when: the agent relays it with that quote
  then: SR-034 is confirmed agent-relayed
