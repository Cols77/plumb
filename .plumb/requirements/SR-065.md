id: SR-065
title: /plumb confirm prepares a confirmation the owner's own words then give
feature: FEAT-005
statement: 'plumb confirm never reads words. Without arguments it prepares the records awaiting confirmation,
  the low-risk group''s records included, each as a full card; no offer line asks for one "ok" on records
  that were not shown in full. The confirm turn''s stop hook shows these cards in the summary before anything
  can be confirmed. A /plumb confirm naming IDs is an exact owner action: it confirms those records at
  once, recorded as passed on from the owner''s typed command. What the owner says in prose about those
  cards is read by the reader (SR-101), not by this command.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: any state
  when: plumb confirm runs with no arguments
  then: no approval event is written, the records awaiting confirmation (low-risk ones included) are prepared
    as full cards that the stop hook shows, and no triage offer line is drawn
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner typed "/plumb confirm SR-034"
  when: the command runs
  then: SR-034 is confirmed at once, recorded as passed on from the owner's typed command
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner typed "/plumb confirm the login thing"
  when: the command runs
  then: nothing is confirmed and plumb says to name IDs or to answer in a message
