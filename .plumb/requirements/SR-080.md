---
id: SR-080
title: Intent in flight is shared through refs/plumb/claims
feature: FEAT-006
statement: 'When a worktree captures a change to an existing record or new intent in a feature, plumb
  records a lightweight claim in the shared ref refs/plumb/claims (git compare-and-swap, shared by every
  worktree of the repository): the records, their features, the branch and a one-line summary of the intent.
  A claim is a signal, never a lock: nothing is refused because of it. It clears when its branch is merged
  into the default branch or deleted, or when the owner drops that work (ruling R17).'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a session on branch feat-x captures a change to SR-005 "relay checks"
  when: the capture completes
  then: refs/plumb/claims holds a claim naming SR-005, branch feat-x and a one-line summary of the request
- id: EX-2
  given: two worktrees record a claim at the same moment
  when: both write the register
  then: both claims are kept and no update is lost
- id: EX-3
  given: a claim recorded on branch feat-x
  when: feat-x is merged into main, or deleted
  then: the claim no longer appears in the register
- id: EX-4
  given: another branch claims SR-005
  when: this worktree edits or captures SR-005
  then: nothing is refused, locked or held; plumb only informs
- id: EX-5
  given: feat-x's change to SR-005 was merged into main with a merge commit, and feat-x then got one more
    commit
  when: main changes SR-005 again (capture, the clarify read-back or a direct edit) or shows the brief
    or status
  then: 'feat-x''s merged version of SR-005 is no longer in flight: no conflict card, question or decision
    is raised and no line names feat-x'
