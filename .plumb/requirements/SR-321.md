---
id: SR-321
title: Only the log kinds plumb needs elsewhere are committed
feature: FEAT-006
statement: 'Log entries another clone needs for plumb to stay consistent and work are committed: approval,
  config, calibration, test-run, mod-hello, revocation, escalation-declined, renumber, import, conflict-resolution,
  waiver, proof-confirmed, browser-hello and claims-ingest. Judgment entries are telemetry: the live files
  are ignored by git and each landing moves them into the kind''s tracked archive segment (SR-376); the
  offer kind no longer exists. The owner''s words stay out of git (DEC-116): prompt, pre-intent, question-answer,
  answer-bound, premise and owner-words are written where they were but ignored, and other checkouts of
  the repository read them from there. An approval, revocation, conflict-resolution or waiver keeps its
  outcome (the ids, labels, side chosen and the digests of the owner''s words) and the words themselves
  are an owner-words entry that a reader joins by digest; a machine without them takes the outcome as
  it stands and re-checks nothing against words it does not hold. The owner''s yes to a proof is the committed
  outcome proof-confirmed. Every other kind (verdict, trial, card-press, card-settled, card-withdrawn,
  gate, read-entry, job-note, triage, merge-evidence, repin, relink, unlink, eval-run, jev-setup, intent-gate,
  intent-done, card-unsettled) is written where it was but ignored by git, and its readers behave as before
  on the machine that wrote it.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a session that appends a verdict and an approval
  when: git status runs
  then: the approval is listed as untracked and the verdict is not
- id: EX-2
  given: a clone with no verdict entries but the approvals and judgments
  when: plumb status runs
  then: every approved record shows approved and no requirement shows unjudged that the other clone showed
    judged
- id: EX-3
  given: a new log kind added to the code
  when: the suite runs
  then: a test fails until the kind is listed as shared or local
proof:
  plan: 'Two loops in two worktrees write log entries at once and land one after the other: main''s checkout
    shows no untracked shared entry left behind and no local entry committed.'
  kind: command
