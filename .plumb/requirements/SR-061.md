---
id: SR-061
title: The owner sees a one-line status card with a plain-language intent summary
feature: FEAT-005
statement: The status line and the first summary line render a status card from status.json without recomputing
  anything - the state (aligned, awaits you, drift, failing, regression), the intent being worked on in
  plain words, the counts with "(N by JEV)" when auto-approvals exist, and full pending or auto-approve
  suspended when they hold - with an ASCII form.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a snapshot with 12 of 14 SR confirmed, 2 by JEV, and one proposed record
  when: the status line renders
  then: it shows "awaits you", the intent title and "SR 12/14 ok (2 by JEV)"
- id: EX-2
  given: no auto-approval exists
  when: the status card renders
  then: no "by JEV" count appears
- id: EX-3
  given: cards.ascii true
  when: the status card renders
  then: it contains only ASCII characters
