---
id: SR-336
title: A study is proposed ahead of a hard decision
statement: When questions waiting for the owner hinge on something plumb's picture of the owner says they
  do not know yet, plumb proposes one study on it before asking them, and each such question offers 'study
  it first' as an option, which opens that study in the room. Studies of general interest are proposed
  only when no question waits.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: three waiting questions hinge on the merge boundary and the owner's picture does not include
    it
  when: plumb paces them
  then: it proposes one study on the merge boundary first, and each question offers 'study it first'
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner chooses 'study it first'
  when: the answer is applied
  then: the room opens on that study and the question waits for the owner's answer
- id: EX-3
  given: no question waits
  when: a loop step ends
  then: a study of general interest may be proposed, as DEC-207 says
proof:
  plan: 'In a live Claude Code session on this repository, the owner sees: no card in plumb''s band and
    Hide staying hidden; waiting questions arriving through the question tool, at most three per turn
    and grouped by principle, each offering to study it in the room when there is more to see; no question
    the owner already answered coming back; and the turn-end line counting what still waits.'
  kind: live-session
