id: SR-081
title: Intent conflicts are caught at capture, before any implementation
feature: FEAT-006
statement: 'plumb capture and plumb clarify check the other branches'' claims before any implementation.
  The same record claimed elsewhere is caught deterministically; new intent in a feature another branch
  claims is judged for compatibility by the judgment backend (JEV when configured, else the heuristic).
  A contradiction shows an early intent-conflict card ("feat-x says A, this request says B: which one,
  or both?") and is recorded once as a proposed decision that matters, so the owner is asked at once and
  every branch sees the question and its answer; a compatible overlap gives only a one-line warning (ruling
  R17). The owner''s answers to these cards go through the reader (SP8), or an exact typed pick of a branch''s
  name; no word rules read them.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: branch feat-x claimed a change to SR-005 "relay checks"
  when: this worktree captures a different change to SR-005
  then: an early conflict card shows feat-x's version and this request's version, and one proposed decision
    that matters decides SR-005
- id: EX-2
  given: branch feat-x claimed new intent in FEAT-002
  when: this worktree captures a new requirement in FEAT-002 that the backend judges contradictory
  then: the early conflict card is shown and the decision is recorded
- id: EX-3
  given: branch feat-x claimed new intent in FEAT-002
  when: this worktree captures a new requirement in FEAT-002 the backend does not judge contradictory
  then: only a one-line warning naming the record and feat-x appears and no decision is written
- id: EX-4
  given: the conflict on SR-005 was already raised as a decision on branch feat-x
  when: this worktree captures SR-005 again
  then: no second decision is written and the line names that decision, with the owner's answer once given
- id: EX-5
  given: feat-x claimed SR-005
  when: plumb clarify runs on a request about SR-005
  then: its output carries the early conflict card before any record is written
- id: EX-6
  given: branch main raised the early conflict on SR-005 against feat-x and the reply before the owner's
    prompt showed its card
  when: the owner says "keep feat-x's", "keep this one" or "both" and the reader maps those words to the
    card's question
  then: the decision holds that choice, it is confirmed from the owner's words (labelled self-recorded
    on a host without a prompt hook), and feat-x's next awareness line names that answer without a further
    turn
- id: EX-7
  given: the early decision on SR-005 holds main's version as its preset choice
  when: the owner says "ok DEC-001, keep feat-x's"
  then: the reader maps it to feat-x's version, and a bare ok on the decision confirms nothing; the answer
    is never recorded as main's version
- id: EX-8
  given: branch feat-x claimed new intent in FEAT-002 and only the word-check backend, or an unsure JEV
    verdict, judges an overlapping new requirement not contradictory
  when: plumb capture or the clarify read-back runs
  then: the line names both versions and asks the agent to put them to the owner if they differ; it never
    says "judged compatible, nothing to ask"
- id: EX-9
  given: the clarify read-back showed the early conflict card before anything was written
  when: the owner answers in the card's words and capture then raises the decision
  then: the answer read against the card the stop hook showed confirms the decision with the owner's choice
- id: EX-10
  given: feat-b raised an early decision on SR-005 against feat-a, main merged one of them, and the decision
    is unanswered
  when: /plumb conflicts or the stop hook shows its card on main, or the owner's words are read or a branch
    is picked there
  then: both versions are labelled by branch and never as "this work"; "keep this one" or "theirs" is
    refused there and a branch's name records exactly that version; while a merge leaves an intent conflict
    on SR-005, only that merge card is shown and its answer answers the decision
- id: EX-11
  given: feat-x claimed a change to SR-005
  when: another branch changes SR-005 differently by a direct edit, an edit after the clarify read-back,
    or capture, or creates with plumb new a requirement in SR-005's feature that the backend judges contradictory,
    and the claim is written (capture, the stop hook or plumb inflight sync)
  then: the early conflict card is shown and one proposed decision that matters is raised, and the owner's
    "keep feat-x's" is recorded as feat-x's version; a read-back card the owner answers before anything
    is written is raised as that decision at the answer
- id: EX-12
  given: the owner answered the early conflict on SR-005 with "keep feat-x's" and main's SR-005 still
    holds another version
  when: main's next prompt or stop hook runs, status is shown, or an ok of SR-005 is relayed
  then: every turn names SR-005 as differing from the owner's answer until it matches, status lists it,
    and the ok is refused naming the decision
- id: EX-13
  given: the owner answered the early conflict on SR-005 by keeping main's version, the answer merged
    into feat-x, and a later request changed SR-005 again on feat-x (or a branch feat-y was made from
    main after a later confirmed change)
  when: feat-x's or feat-y's next prompt or stop hook runs, status or the brief is shown, or the owner's
    ok of SR-005 is relayed there
  then: SR-005 is not named as holding the version the owner turned down, and the ok is not refused for
    it; only a record that still holds the turned-down version is named (the answer is a signal, never
    a lock)
- id: EX-14
  given: feat-x started from main; main then changed SR-005 "relay checks" and committed it, so main's
    claim cleared
  when: feat-x captures different words for SR-005, an edit is claimed by the stop hook, or the clarify
    read-back runs on a request about SR-005
  then: the early conflict card shows main's version and this work's, one proposed decision that matters
    is raised once, and feat-x's status, session brief and prompt say SR-005 was changed on main after
    this branch started; nothing is raised once feat-x holds main's version or leaves SR-005 as it started
