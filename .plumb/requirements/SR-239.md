---
id: SR-239
title: An independent judge checks every new or changed record against...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'A model independent of the coding agent judges each new or changed record (one job per capture)
  against its premise: the owner''s whole logged message or pre-intent with its context, the question
  a short answer answered, and the approved records, labelled assumptions left out. It returns supported,
  beyond or contradicts with what and its confidence, logged; a doubt reaches the owner only as a question-tool
  question. A question a recorded decision already answers is not asked again: its records wait, labelled
  as answered by that decision, and the judge rejudges them with the decision in view.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an intent's first interpretation adds one line per record and a token budget, and the owner's
    message asked only for a smarter way that does not bloat the context
  when: the judge runs
  then: 'beyond: one line per record and a token budget'
- id: EX-2
  given: an interpretation states maintenance mode as decided, and the owner said "consider stopping developments
    on pi-agent-factory if it takes too much RAM"
  when: the judge runs
  then: 'beyond: maintenance mode is stated as decided'
- id: EX-3
  given: an interpretation built from the owner's answer B with its question
  when: the judge runs
  then: supported
- id: EX-4
  given: a requirement whose example contradicts an example of an approved requirement
  when: the judge runs
  then: contradicts, naming both examples
- id: EX-5
  given: the owner answered "A" in the question tool to a question offering A and B
  when: the judge runs on the record built from that answer
  then: the question is part of the premise, and the judge is asked whether the owner would object to
    the record given that question and answer
- id: EX-6
  given: a capture writes an intent and six requirements from one owner message
  when: the capture finishes
  then: one judge job covers the seven records
- id: EX-7
  given: the judge finds a record goes beyond or contradicts the owner's words
  when: the verdict is logged
  then: no card and no read-back line is drawn; the owner gets the judge model's question about that finding
    through the question tool, naming the record by title
- id: EX-8
  given: a requirement written with a labelled assumption the owner never stated
  when: the judge runs
  then: the assumption is not judged and does not make the verdict beyond
- id: EX-9
  given: the judge asks a question whose answer a recorded decision already holds
  when: the verdict is read
  then: the owner is not asked; the question's records wait labelled as answered by that decision, and
    the next judge run has the decision in view
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended: in a live session the owner states a small intent and the agent deliberately adds
    one requirement the owner never asked for; the owner gets one question-tool question naming that requirement
    as going beyond their words, and nothing else. The owner decides whether this is the proof.'
  kind: live-session
