---
id: SR-116
title: A card shows everything needed to choose
statement: 'Each owner card carries, and the mod and the end-of-turn text show: the record as it now reads;
  what changed, with its evidence (the diff of the linked code, the failing test, or the earlier and current
  wording); the owner''s words the record traces to; for each option what it does and what it leaves undone;
  and the reason for the recommended one. A card missing any of these that applies to its kind is not
  offered; the agent fills it first.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a stale-link card for SR-005 because confirm.py now accepts the Confirm button
  when: the card is shown
  then: it shows SR-005's current wording, the confirm.py change itself, the owner's words SR-005 came
    from, and for each option what it would change, with why option 1 is recommended
- id: EX-2
  given: a card whose diff is 60,000 characters
  when: it is shown
  then: the diff is folded with its size and can be opened in full; it is not cut silently
- id: EX-3
  given: an agent files a card with only a topic and a one-line issue
  when: the turn ends
  then: the card is refused as incomplete and the agent is told what is missing
- id: EX-4
  given: cards are waiting and the mod is not built yet
  when: the agent replies
  then: the reply says cards are waiting and does not restate them as a list to answer in prose
