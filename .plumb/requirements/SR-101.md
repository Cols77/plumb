---
id: SR-101
title: A reader model works out the owner's answer to each record shown
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'A model independent of the coding agent reads the owner''s logged free-prose message against
  the records the previous reply showed (each at its version) and the open cards, and returns per item:
  accepted, rejected (misread or intent changed), change asked (what), or open, with the words that carry
  it and a confidence. plumb confirms an item only when the message is in the prompt log, the item was
  shown at that version, the evidence occurs in the message, and the answer is accepted with high confidence;
  the confirmation records the owner''s words. Only the hook-started worker applies a result, and only
  for a job a hook queued and signed. A typed /plumb approve or confirm naming IDs is an exact owner action
  that bypasses the reader and confirms at once. Fixed rules (affirmation words, objection words, clause
  splitting, plain-words matching) are not used to understand the owner, and owner-only settings change
  only by a typed command or a button.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the previous reply showed INT-008 and SR-088 in full
  when: the owner writes "INT-008 ok, I am not sure I agree with SR-088. In terms of UX, some features
    might have to remain internal to plumb."
  then: 'INT-008 is confirmed with the words "INT-008 ok" and SR-088 gets a change: some features stay
    internal to plumb'
- id: EX-2
  given: the previous reply showed SR-097, SR-098 and INT-024
  when: the owner writes "Confirm SR-097, SR-098, INT-24"
  then: all three are confirmed (INT-24 read as INT-024)
- id: EX-3
  given: the previous reply showed SR-040 only
  when: the owner writes "ok, and confirm SR-041 too"
  then: SR-040 is confirmed; SR-041 is shown as a card first and confirmed on the next answer
- id: EX-4
  given: the reader answers accepted with medium confidence
  when: plumb applies it
  then: nothing is confirmed and the item stays on its card
- id: EX-5
  given: a record edited after it was shown
  when: the owner accepts it
  then: nothing is confirmed and the new version is shown
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner types "/plumb approve DEC-017" and DEC-017 was not shown
  when: plumb handles the message
  then: DEC-017 is confirmed at once, recorded as passed on from the owner's typed command, and the reader
    is not called
- id: EX-7
  given: a result file for a read job, written by the agent without the worker's signature or with other
    input hashes than a queued job
  when: plumb looks at the results
  then: nothing is confirmed and the forged result is reported
- id: EX-8
  given: the owner writes in prose "turn settling on"
  when: the reader returns accepted
  then: settling stays off; the owner-only setting changes only by a typed command or a button
