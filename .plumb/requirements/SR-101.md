---
id: SR-101
title: A reader model works out the owner's answer to each record shown
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'A model independent of the coding agent reads the owner''s logged free-prose message against
  the records the previous reply showed (each at its version) and the open cards, and returns per item:
  accepted, rejected (misread or intent changed), change asked (what), or open, with the words that carry
  it and a confidence; a card''s resolutions map onto these four answers. plumb confirms an item only
  when the message is in the prompt log, the item was shown at that version, the evidence occurs in the
  message, and the answer is accepted with high confidence; the confirmation records the owner''s words.
  Only the hook-started worker applies a result, from job inputs it recomputed from the logs; a file the
  agent writes confirms nothing. A typed /plumb approve or confirm naming IDs is an exact owner action
  that bypasses the reader and confirms at once; an agent-run prose relay no longer exists. Fixed rules
  (affirmation words, objection words, clause splitting, plain-words matching) are not used to understand
  the owner, and owner-only settings and Undo change only by a typed command or a button.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the previous reply showed INT-008 and SR-088 in full
  when: the owner writes "INT-008 ok, I am not sure I agree with SR-088. In terms of UX, some features
    might have to remain internal to plumb."
  then: 'INT-008 is confirmed with the words "INT-008 ok" and SR-088 gets a change: some features stay
    internal to plumb'
- id: EX-2
  given: the previous reply showed SR-097, SR-098 and INT-024
  when: the owner writes "Confirm SR-097, SR-098, INT-24"
  then: all three are confirmed (INT-24 read as INT-024)
- id: EX-3
  given: the previous reply showed SR-040 only
  when: the owner writes "ok, and confirm SR-041 too"
  then: SR-040 is confirmed; SR-041 is shown as a card first and confirmed on the next answer
- id: EX-4
  given: the reader answers accepted with medium confidence
  when: plumb applies it
  then: nothing is confirmed and the item stays on its card
- id: EX-5
  given: a record edited after it was shown
  when: the owner accepts it
  then: nothing is confirmed and the new version is shown
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner types "/plumb approve DEC-017" and DEC-017 was not shown
  when: plumb handles the message
  then: DEC-017 is confirmed at once, recorded as passed on from the owner's typed command, and the reader
    is not called
- id: EX-7
  given: a result file or job file for a read job, written by the agent
  when: the worker looks at it
  then: the worker recomputes the job's inputs from the logs and applies only what it computed itself;
    the agent's file confirms nothing and is reported
- id: EX-8
  given: the owner writes in prose "turn settling on" (or "undo that")
  when: the reader returns accepted
  then: settling stays off and nothing is undone; plumb replies with the typed command or button to use,
    because owner-only settings and Undo change only by a typed command or a button
- id: EX-9
  given: a logged prompt reading "no, do not approve SR-001"
  when: the agent runs plumb relay for SR-001 from its shell with a quote from that prompt
  then: 'plumb refuses and nothing is confirmed: an agent-run prose relay no longer exists; only the typed
    command and the worker confirm'
- id: EX-10
  given: the owner types "/plumb confirm the reminder timing"
  when: plumb handles the message
  then: the command takes IDs only and confirms nothing; the words go to the reader as prose, on the records
    the previous reply showed
- id: EX-11
  given: a card offering keep, drop or reword for an addition, and a card offering two versions
  when: the owner answers "keep it", "drop it", "reword to X", or "the second, plus the first's date"
  then: the reader maps them to accepted, rejected, change (X) and change (the second version with the
    first's date)
