---
id: SR-093
title: An intent can say when it is done
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'An intent may say when it is done: a short list in the owner''s terms. When the agent clarifies
  a request, plumb tells the agent (never the owner) what the request leaves open: its scope (what will
  be done), its non-goals (what won''t), its success (when it is done) and its failure cases (examples
  of errors or refusals). plumb''s alignment check names every "done when" item that no example covers
  yet (ruling R18.3).'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the owner said "it's done when I get a reminder three days before each deadline"
  when: the agent records the intent with that "done when" item
  then: 'the intent card shows "Done when: you get a reminder three days before each deadline"'
- id: EX-2
  given: a request that states no success criterion
  when: the agent clarifies it
  then: plumb's hints to the agent say success is not stated, and the owner is asked nothing about it
- id: EX-3
  given: a "done when" item that no example covers
  when: plumb checks alignment
  then: it names that item as a gap in plain words
- id: EX-4
  given: an example that covers that "done when" item
  when: plumb checks alignment again
  then: the gap is gone
