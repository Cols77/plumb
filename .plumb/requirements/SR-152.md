title: The judge's doubts are lines on the read-back, not cards
statement: When the judge finds a record goes beyond or contradicts the owner's words, the request's read-back
  card gets one line saying so in the second person, and no separate card is raised for that record
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a request whose read-back recorded a requirement the owner never asked for
  when: the judge reads it as going beyond their words
  then: the read-back card has a line 'You did not ask for <title>; Yes confirms it too, so choose Not
    quite to drop it' and no card is raised for that record
- id: EX-2
  given: a record that contradicts a confirmed one
  when: the judge reads it
  then: the read-back line names both by title and says which one you confirmed
- id: EX-3
  given: a request with no such finding
  when: its read-back is drawn
  then: it is unchanged
- id: EX-4
  given: a doubt about a record of one request, and the read-back of another request
  when: the other read-back is drawn
  then: it does not carry the line; the line goes only on its own request's read-back
