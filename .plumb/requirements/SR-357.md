---
id: SR-357
title: Known issues reach every session
statement: plumb keeps a register of known issues (symptom, cause, fix, state), matched by symptom rather
  than by name. The session-start hook brings the open ones and the recently fixed ones to every session,
  and a symptom matching one, in any session, is reported against it. On a plugin older than main, a fixed
  issue's symptom is reported as fixed in main, reload plugins, not as a recurrence.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 'the register holds the open issue: a judge that answered is reported as unreachable'
  when: a fresh session starts
  then: the session is told that issue is open
- id: EX-2
  given: an issue is fixed in main and the session runs an older plugin
  when: its symptom appears
  then: plumb says it is fixed in main and to reload plugins, and reopens nothing
- id: EX-3
  given: the session runs main's build
  when: a fixed issue's symptom appears
  then: 'it is a recurrence: the old case reopens'
proof:
  plan: after landing, a fresh session is told of a known issue; reproducing its symptom reopens the old
    case
  kind: live-session
  post_merge: true
