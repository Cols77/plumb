id: SR-104
title: Model jobs run in the background through the host's own dispatch,...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'Reading, judging and clarifying are jobs queued by the hooks and run by a background worker,
  so no hook waits on a model; each job runs through the one configured dispatcher: Claude Code runs claude
  -p with a plumb agent, no tools, a small model and the job''s answer schema, and plumb''s hooks do nothing
  inside it; Hermes runs a delegated child session; OpenRouter only when configured. A dispatcher that
  fails makes the job fail with an error the owner sees; nothing is confirmed, settled or rejected and
  the job is retried on the next turn. There is never a second route or a rule-based fallback.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the owner's message arrives
  when: the prompt hook runs
  then: it queues the read job and returns within its budget without waiting for the model
- id: EX-2
  given: the Claude Code dispatcher runs a job
  when: plumb's hooks fire inside that headless session
  then: they exit at once and queue nothing
- id: EX-3
  given: the configured model is unreachable
  when: the turn ends
  then: the summary says plumb could not check the owner's answers, nothing is confirmed, and the job
    is retried next turn
- id: EX-4
  given: the same job inputs queued twice
  when: the worker runs
  then: the job runs once
