---
id: SR-369
title: Tab moves between the band's waiting study ideas
statement: When study ideas wait for the owner's choice, the band names the one in front by its title
  and, when more than one waits, how many; pressing Tab moves to the next idea, wrapping round, and the
  depths and 'not now' act on the idea shown. A choice is recorded only for that idea.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: two ideas wait, 'How a question travels to you' and 'How merges are held', and no loop is open
  when: the band is drawn
  then: it names 'How merges are held' and says 1 more waits
- id: EX-2
  given: the band names 'How merges are held' with one more waiting
  when: the owner presses Tab
  then: the band names 'How a question travels to you', and a second Tab brings back 'How merges are held'
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner tabbed to 'How a question travels to you'
  when: they choose diagrams
  then: only that idea is accepted at diagrams; 'How merges are held' still waits
proof:
  plan: after reloading the plugin, with two study ideas waiting, the band names both; Tab moves between
    them and choosing a depth on one accepts only that one
  kind: live-session
