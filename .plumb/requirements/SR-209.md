id: SR-209
title: A judge-validated interpretation approves on the owner's behalf;...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: The agent rewords the owner's pre-intent into formal records; the judge cascade validates that
  interpretation against the owner's words and context, and a validated record is approved on the owner's
  behalf, marked judge-approved, with the trail from pre-intent to record. The owner approves nothing
  by command, button or read-back card and is asked only to arbitrate contradictory intent. No word list
  decides anything.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the owner's message about the reminder timing is in the prompt log and the judge cascade validates
    the agent's formal requirement against it
  when: the verdict is recorded
  then: the requirement is approved, marked judge-approved, citing the owner's words and the pre-intent
    it came from
- id: EX-2
  given: the last reply mentioned the rounding rule, the amount type and the reminder intent
  when: the owner writes "the rounding is fine but amounts should be floats"
  then: the rounding requirement is approved with the owner's words, the amount decision is reworded by
    the agent and goes back to the judge, and the reminder intent stays proposed
- id: EX-3
  given: no question is open
  when: the owner writes a bare "ok"
  then: no record changes state and no question is asked
- id: EX-4
  given: a record edited after the question about it was put to the owner
  when: the owner answers
  then: the answer applies to the version asked about only; the edit goes back to the judge cascade
- id: EX-5
  given: a session start with low-risk records
  when: the brief prints
  then: no offer asks the owner to say ok and no read-back card is shown
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner types "/plumb approve DEC-017"
  when: the prompt hook reads it
  then: no approval runs, no id is asked for, the records' states are unchanged by the command, and the
    words are read by meaning as the owner's answer
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended: in a live session the owner states a small intent in plain words; plumb''s status
    then shows the formal requirement judge-approved with the trail back to the owner''s sentence, without
    the owner having read or approved anything. The owner decides whether this walk is the proof.'
  kind: live-session
