title: Every judge approval is marked with its judge and counted; an...
feature: FEAT-002
statement: 'Each judge approval names its judge, score and model version wherever the record is listed,
  and the band''s status counts them; the turn''s end shows no line for approvals alone (SR-265). An automatic
  approval entry that does not carry what the cascade requires (a JEV score above 0.9 and the calibrated
  threshold, or a judge model''s clear verdict after a question round) is inert: the record counts as
  unapproved, and plumb validate reports the entry as a warning.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the judge model approved SR-185 during the turn
  when: the turn ends
  then: no plumb line is shown for it; the band's status counts one judge approval, and SR-185 shows as
    judge-approved (auto-model, haiku, 0.8) wherever it is listed
- id: EX-2
  given: 'a worker wrote a by: auto-jev approval at score 0.6, under the cascade''s 0.9 floor'
  when: the approvals are read
  then: the approval is refused, SR-185 counts as proposed, and plumb validate reports the inert entry
    as a warning
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended: after a judge approval in a live session the turn-end line counts it and the status
    shows it judge-approved with its judge and score; a hand-made entry under the bar is shown reported
    as a warning with the record still unapproved. The owner decides whether this is the proof.'
  kind: live-session
