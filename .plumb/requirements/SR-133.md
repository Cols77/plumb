id: SR-133
title: A trial card is the read-back of one record
statement: A trial card shows the owner's source words found by meaning with their date, or says they
  were not found; the record's proposal in the owner's terms, including a decision's chosen option and
  its alternative; and Yes, No, Fix it; at most three trial cards are open at a time. Each press is a
  labelled owner answer, so Yes confirms the record (by owner, via button, label yes); No marks plumb's
  reading wrong, counts as a real 'no' for calibration, and the record stays; each button names its own
  record's offer, so a press from an out-of-date draw cannot land on another record; a card left open
  is not asked again until its record changes
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a decision whose source words are in a transcript of 4 Oct
  when: its trial card is shown
  then: the card quotes those words with the date, states the chosen option and the alternative, and offers
    Yes, No, Fix it
- id: EX-2
  given: a record whose source words were not found
  when: its trial card is shown
  then: it says the words were not found and shows the proposal alone, never an unrelated message
- id: EX-3
  given: five records eligible for a trial
  when: cards are shown
  then: three are open, and the next one opens when one is answered
- id: EX-4
  given: three open trial cards
  when: the owner presses Yes on the first, No on the second and Yes on the third
  then: two approvals by owner via button labelled yes and one capture-error rejection are logged, the
    records stay, and after the next turn end jev status counts 1 real 'no' and 2 yes
- id: EX-5
  given: a trial card the owner pressed Leave open on
  when: its record changes
  then: the card is open again and repeats in the next draw; while nothing changed it stays left open
