---
id: SR-004
title: Approvals pin exact content; an edit leaves the record unconfirmed
feature: FEAT-001
statement: An approval pins the hash of each record it confirms; a record is confirmed only while the
  latest unrevoked approval pins its current hash, becomes an unconfirmed edit when edited after approval,
  and returns to proposed when its approval is revoked; a requirement is covered only when every example
  has a verified_by test.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: SR-001 approved at its current hash
  when: its statement is edited
  then: SR-001 shows as unconfirmed-edit, not confirmed
- id: EX-2
  given: SR-001 never approved
  when: its status is read
  then: it is proposed
- id: EX-3
  given: a confirmed SR-001 approval
  when: the owner revokes it
  then: SR-001 returns to proposed
