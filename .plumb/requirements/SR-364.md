---
id: SR-364
title: A video study starts from a storyboard
statement: 'Before writing the scene of any clip of a video study, the agent writes the clip''s storyboard
  committed beside the study: the one question the clip answers, 2-3 things the viewer can explain afterwards,
  the context it starts from (where the topic sits in plumb and which records it comes from), and its
  beats, each naming what is shown and a subtitle that explains why, on screen long enough to read; the
  last beat recaps the goals and points to the study''s text. The agent decides how many beats and how
  long; plumb renders no clip whose storyboard lacks the question, the goals, the context, or a subtitle
  with time to read it on every beat.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an accepted idea 'How the judge cascade runs' at depth video
  when: the agent prepares a clip
  then: a storyboard with the question, the goals, the context and the beats is written and committed
    beside the study before any scene exists
- id: EX-2
  given: a storyboard with no goals, no context, or a beat with no subtitle
  when: a render is asked for
  then: plumb starts no render and says which part of the storyboard is missing
- id: EX-3
  given: a storyboard for 'How the judge cascade runs'
  when: its beats are read
  then: the first beat places the cascade in plumb (what comes before and after it) and the last beat
    recaps the goals and points to the study's text
- id: EX-4
  given: a storyboard of 8 beats lasting two minutes, each subtitle with time enough to read
  when: a render is asked for
  then: 'plumb renders it: the agent decides a clip''s length'
- id: EX-5
  given: a beat whose 30-word subtitle lasts 4 seconds
  when: a render is asked for
  then: plumb starts no render and says that beat needs at least 10 seconds for its subtitle
proof:
  plan: 'After landing, in a live intent loop: a video study on ''How the judge cascade runs'' is proposed,
    the owner accepts it at depth video in the band; the owner sees its storyboard, the reviewer''s frame-check
    verdict, and the video playing in the room with subtitles on; every screenshot (PNG) is opened on
    the owner''s screen.'
