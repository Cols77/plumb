---
id: SR-364
title: A video study starts from a storyboard
statement: 'Before writing any scene for a video study, the agent writes a storyboard committed beside
  the study: the one question the video answers, 2-3 things the viewer can explain afterwards, the context
  it starts from (where the topic sits in plumb and which records it comes from), and 3 to 6 beats, each
  naming what is shown and a subtitle that explains why; the last beat recaps the goals and points to
  the study''s text. plumb renders no video whose storyboard lacks the question, the goals, the context
  or a subtitle on every beat.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an accepted idea 'How the judge cascade runs' at depth video
  when: the agent prepares the video
  then: a storyboard with the question, the goals, the context and the beats is written and committed
    beside the study before any scene exists
  quote: You really have to think what you're going to show in that video
  prompt: 260a5c4091f4a619
- id: EX-2
  given: a storyboard with no goals, no context, or a beat with no subtitle
  when: a render is asked for
  then: plumb starts no render and says which part of the storyboard is missing
  quote: what the user needs to understand from that video
  prompt: 260a5c4091f4a619
- id: EX-3
  given: a storyboard for 'How the judge cascade runs'
  when: its beats are read
  then: the first beat places the cascade in plumb (what comes before and after it) and the last beat
    recaps the goals and points to the study's text
  quote: the information seems a bit de-contextualised
  prompt: 260a5c4091f4a619
- id: EX-4
  given: a storyboard of 8 beats or a planned length over 90 seconds
  when: a render is asked for
  then: plumb starts no render and says the video must fit in 3 to 6 beats and 90 seconds
proof:
  plan: 'After landing, in a live intent loop: a video study on ''How the judge cascade runs'' is proposed,
    the owner accepts it at depth video in the band; the owner sees its storyboard, the reviewer''s frame-check
    verdict, and the video playing in the room with subtitles on; every screenshot (PNG) is opened on
    the owner''s screen.'
