statement: 'When no logged message holds the owner''s words, the judgment says premise: quote-only with
  the reason, and such a judgment never fast-tracks the record; calibration counts how many labels rest
  on quote-only premises and JEV status says so. A judgment on the whole message carries no mark.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an intent with no logged prompt
  when: the judge runs

    fast-tracks the record'
- id: EX-2
  given: 25 of 46 labelled intents have a quote-only premise
  when: calibration runs
  then: 'the calibration entry records quote_only: 25 of 46 and the JEV status line says so'
- id: EX-3
  given: a record whose premise is the whole message
  when: it is judged
  then: the entry carries no quote-only mark

    prompt and sees how many labels rest on the quote alone. The owner decides whether this is the proof.'
