---
id: SR-009
title: The session brief is about 30 lines of context, not procedure
feature: FEAT-001
statement: plumb brief prints a short intent brief (about 30 lines, hard cap 40) naming the features,
  intents, records awaiting confirmation, drift, decisions and open NCs; it names records and never carries
  long procedures. The session-start hook injects its compact form by default (a few lines and a pointer,
  SR-032) and this brief with hooks.session_brief full.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a repository with 60 intents and 30 open NCs
  when: plumb brief runs
  then: it prints at most 30 lines and ends the long lists with "+N more (plumb brief --all)"
- id: EX-2
  given: a repository with FEAT-001, INT-001 and proposed SR-001
  when: plumb brief runs
  then: it names FEAT-001, INT-001 and SR-001 awaiting your confirmation
