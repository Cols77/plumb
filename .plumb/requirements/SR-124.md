---
id: SR-124
title: Trial presses in the mod are labelled answers calibration counts
statement: 'A trial batch reaches the owner in the mod with Yes and No per record. Each press is a labelled
  owner answer: Yes confirms the record (by owner, via button, label yes), No is a capture-error rejection
  that calibration counts as a real ''no''. A record answered in its batch is not offered again; the calibration
  reruns from the logged scores once the batch is answered (SR-106), counting every press of it; a batch
  left open is not asked again in another batch until its record changes.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: three supported requirements the log holds back and no passing calibration
  when: the turn ends and the mod draws
  then: one trial card is drawn with Yes and No for each of the three records
- id: EX-2
  given: a drawn trial batch of three
  when: the owner presses Yes on the first, No on the second and Yes on the third
  then: two approvals by owner via button labelled yes and one capture-error rejection are logged, the
    trial entry is logged, and after the next turn end jev status counts 1 real 'no' and 2 yes
- id: EX-3
  given: a trial batch of three where the owner pressed Yes on one record
  when: the mod draws again
  then: the same card offers only the two records not yet answered, each with the offer first drawn for
    it
- id: EX-4
  given: a trial batch of three where the owner pressed Yes on one record and nothing else
  when: the turn ends, then the owner presses Yes on the other two and the next turn ends
  then: no calibration runs at the first turn end; at the second it reruns from the logged scores with
    no model call and counts all three yes
- id: EX-5
  given: a trial batch the owner pressed Leave open on after answering one record
  when: the turn ends
  then: no new trial card asks about its unanswered records; the left-open card stays listed
- id: EX-6
  given: the mod's trial batch with two records
  when: the owner presses No on the second after Show all
  then: the press names that record's offer and button, and the redraw offers only the first record under
    its own words
