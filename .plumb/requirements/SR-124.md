id: SR-124
title: Trial presses in the mod are labelled answers calibration counts
statement: 'A trial batch reaches the owner in the mod with Yes and No per record. Each press is a labelled
  owner answer: Yes confirms the record (by owner, via button, label yes), No is a capture-error rejection
  that calibration counts as a real ''no''; the No marks plumb''s reading wrong and the record stays (the
  owner is not told it was dropped, the agent is told not to remove or rewrite it). A record answered
  in its batch is not offered again (trial batches only; every other card keeps all its questions); each
  button names its record''s offer, so a press from an out-of-date draw cannot land on another record;
  the calibration reruns from the logged scores once the batch is answered (SR-106), counting every press
  of it; a batch left open is not asked again in another batch until its record changes, and then it comes
  back.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: three supported requirements the log holds back and no passing calibration
  when: the turn ends and the mod draws
  then: one trial card is drawn with Yes and No for each of the three records
- id: EX-2
  given: a drawn trial batch of three
  when: the owner presses Yes on the first, No on the second and Yes on the third
  then: two approvals by owner via button labelled yes and one capture-error rejection are logged, the
    trial entry is logged, and after the next turn end jev status counts 1 real 'no' and 2 yes
- id: EX-3
  given: a trial batch of three where the owner pressed Yes on one record
  when: the mod draws again
  then: the same card offers only the two records not yet answered, each with the offer first drawn for
    it
- id: EX-4
  given: a trial batch of three where the owner pressed Yes on one record and nothing else
  when: the turn ends, then the owner presses Yes on the other two and the next turn ends
  then: no calibration runs at the first turn end; at the second it reruns from the logged scores with
    no model call and counts all three yes
- id: EX-5
  given: a trial batch the owner pressed Leave open on after answering one record
  when: the turn ends
  then: no new trial card asks about its unanswered records; the left-open card stays listed
- id: EX-6
  given: the mod's trial batch with two records
  when: the owner presses No on the second after Show all
  then: the press names that record's offer and button, and the redraw offers only the first record under
    its own words
- id: EX-7
  given: a drawn trial batch of three
  when: the owner presses No on the second record
  then: the owner's line and the agent's note say plumb's reading was marked wrong and the record stays,
    neither says dropped, the agent's note says not to remove or rewrite it, and the record is unchanged
- id: EX-8
  given: a backlog card of two questions with one answered, and a trial batch of two with one answered
  when: the mod draws
  then: the backlog card still shows both questions; the trial batch shows only the unanswered one
- id: EX-9
  given: a trial batch where the owner pressed Yes on one record and Leave open on another
  when: one of its records changes
  then: the card is open again and repeats in the next draw; while nothing changed it stays left open
- id: EX-10
  given: the mod drew a trial batch with SR-001 first and SR-009 second
  when: a press made on that draw names SR-001's button after a redraw where SR-001 is gone and SR-009
    stands first
  then: the press finds no button and nothing is pressed; a key naming SR-009's offer still presses SR-009
