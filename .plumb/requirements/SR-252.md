id: SR-252
title: JEV's calibration on the owner's own answers raises the fast-track floor
feature: FEAT-002
statement: 'Calibration pairs JEV''s score logged at judgment time with the owner''s own labels: question-tool
  answers confirming or correcting an interpretation, approvals by the owner''s words, and reversals labelled
  ''no'' as P4 says. A record kind passes only with mixed evidence: at least 20 owner labels and 5 ''no''
  examples, at most half generated, no ''no'' wrongly accepted. Each run logs threshold, false-accept
  rate and recall with model version and prompt digest. No trial card asks for labels. A passing calibration
  raises the fast-track floor to its threshold; none is needed for the fast track (DEC-143).'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: fewer than 20 labelled intents
  when: calibration runs
  then: the intent kind is not passing and intents are fast-tracked on the 0.9 floor alone
- id: EX-2
  given: a labelled set where one misread record got the judge's confidence 0.93
  when: calibration chooses the threshold
  then: the threshold is above 0.93
- id: EX-3
  given: no judge model is configured
  when: calibration runs (/plumb jev calibrate)
  then: it reports that judging is unavailable, naming judgment.backend, exits with an error, and writes
    no calibration event
- id: EX-4
  given: the owner corrects the judge model's interpretation of SR-185 in the question tool
  when: calibration next runs
  then: the correction counts as a real 'no' paired with JEV's score logged at judgment time, with no
    new model call
- id: EX-5
  given: 25 requirement labels whose 5 'no' examples include 3 generated variations
  when: calibration runs
  then: the requirement kind is not passing, because more than half of its 'no' examples are generated
- id: EX-6
  given: the fast track off, no passing calibration, and three records the judge finds supported
  when: the turn ends
  then: no trial or read-back card is shown; the records go the question route and the owner's answers
    become labels
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended: the owner asks plumb how well the judge is calibrated and is shown, per record kind,
    how many of their own answers and ''no'' examples it rests on, how many are generated, and the threshold
    it chose; a fixture with too few real ''no'' examples is shown not passing. The owner decides whether
    this is the proof.'
  kind: command
