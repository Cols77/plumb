id: SR-388
title: plumb's self-fix runs in separate stages, several cases side by side
statement: 'plumb''s self-fix of a debug case runs as separate stages by separate agents: a diagnosis
  that reads only and names the cause and the code path, a fix that writes the failing test and the change,
  and a review by an agent that did not write the fix and sees only the case, the change and the records
  it touches; plumb''s own checks then run as before. The models stay as already decided: the middle model
  for the cause, the test, the fix and the review, the largest only after a round failed. A round that
  fails hands the review''s and the checks'' findings to the next round. Open cases are worked side by
  side, each in its own worktree, up to the worker''s limit; only their test runs wait behind the one-heavy-job
  lock.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a planted defect in a plumb step
  when: the self-fix runs
  then: the case's log names three stages with the model of each, the diagnosis changed no file, and the
    review ran as a different agent run than the fix
- id: EX-2
  given: a fix whose test passes but does not exercise the cause the diagnosis named
  when: the review ends
  then: the round fails, nothing is committed, and the next round is given the review's finding
- id: EX-3
  given: 3 open cases and a limit of 2
  when: the background self-fix runs
  then: two cases are worked at once in their own worktrees and their test runs go one at a time
- id: EX-4
  given: three rounds that each failed the review or the checks
  when: the third ends
  then: nothing is committed and the case is a defect report naming the rounds, as before
- id: EX-5
  given: the same planted defects
  when: they are fixed the old way and the staged way
  then: the owner is shown, side by side, how many were fixed, in how many rounds and in what wall time
proof:
  plan: 'Acceptance tests with scripted agents: the three stages and their models are logged, a review
    finding fails the round, two cases run at once with their test runs in turn. Then plumb evaluate complex
    with fix rounds, before and after: cases fixed, rounds and wall time side by side.'
