id: SR-387
title: The background worker sends independent model calls out together
statement: The background worker sends the independent model calls of a session's queue out together,
  up to a limit that is a setting; its default is set by the owner from wall times measured on this machine,
  and 1 behaves as before. The answers are applied one at a time, in the queue's order, under the worker's
  lock. A job that needs another is sent only after that one ended well, and a call that fails fails only
  its own job, retried as before.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 6 independent judge jobs queued and a limit of 3
  when: the worker runs
  then: at most 3 model calls are out at any moment and the 6 answers are applied in the queue's order
- id: EX-2
  given: a job that needs another job of the queue
  when: the worker runs
  then: it is sent only after the job it needs ended well
- id: EX-3
  given: three calls out and one fails
  when: the other two answer
  then: their answers are applied, the failed job counts as one failure and is retried with a larger budget
    as before
- id: EX-4
  given: the limit set to 1
  when: the worker runs
  then: the jobs run one after another as before
- id: EX-5
  given: the judge backlog of this repository
  when: it is judged with the limit at 1 and then at 3
  then: the owner is shown both wall times
proof:
  plan: Acceptance tests with a stubbed dispatcher that records how many calls are out at once and the
    order of the applies; then the wall time of this repository's judge backlog at limit 1 and at limit
    3, shown side by side.
