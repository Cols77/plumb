id: SR-260
title: The merge is the one hold on unapproved or conflicting intent
feature: FEAT-003
statement: 'One lightweight merge check runs when git records a merge, rebase, cherry-pick or revert step
  (quick, no tests) and at push, where the pre-push hook runs the same check with the covering tests and
  the full suite only when full verification is pending (P10). A merge is held only by what the owner
  named (DEC-252): a conflict (an open intent conflict, colliding ids, or a contradiction the judge is
  sure of), a question waiting for the owner on a touched record, a failing test (a test linked to a touched
  requirement whose latest recorded run fails, read from the evidence with no test run), and, for a merge
  into the default branch, a requirement the merge delivers (named by an incoming commit''s SR trailer)
  whose proof the agent has not shown working or the owner has not confirmed through the question tool
  (DEC-256, DEC-257); and by integrity (DEC-255): stale links the merge adds, new links to files no test
  covers, record files unreadable or left out of the commit, a broken log (a merge that deletes a committed
  log entry not kept in an archive segment) and any validate error in the merged result. Records waiting
  on a judge, links and exemptions added since an approval, and paths no trailer declares are listed and
  never hold; an exemption stays unhonoured until approved. A rebase, cherry-pick or revert step is held
  while it meets an open intent conflict on a record. The check exits 1 for integrity, 5 for a failing
  test or an unconfirmed proof and 4 for a conflict or an open question, reporting the first of 1, 5 and
  4 that applies; its report fits one screen.'
