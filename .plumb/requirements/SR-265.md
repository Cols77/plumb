---
id: SR-265
title: The end of a turn carries at most one plumb line
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'At the end of a turn the owner''s text carries at most one short line from plumb, in plumb''s
  colour where the host allows it, saying what changed for the owner: a question waiting, an answer applied,
  a regression, what the turn touched and whether its checks pass; it never carries a question, options
  or a list of records, and a turn where nothing changed for the owner shows nothing.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a turn that raised one question needing a diagram
  when: the turn ends
  then: 'the text shows one plumb line such as ''plumb: 1 question waits in the study room'' and no question
    or options'
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner's answer to a question was applied during the turn
  when: the turn ends
  then: one plumb line says the answer was applied, naming the question's subject in the owner's words,
    never an id
- id: EX-3
  given: a host with no band or mod, such as Codex
  when: a turn ends with a question raised
  then: the plumb line is printed as plain text and the question goes through that host's question tool;
    nothing is printed as a card
- id: EX-4
  given: a turn where nothing changed for the owner
  when: it ends
  then: plumb prints nothing
- id: EX-5
  given: a turn that changed the deadline reminders and whose 7 covering checks passed
  when: the answer ends
  then: 'one line reads like "plumb: aligned, touched ''deadline reminders'', 7 checks pass" and the answer''s
    text is unchanged'
- id: EX-6
  given: an ordinary turn with nothing that matters
  when: it ends
  then: no pop-up note appears
- id: EX-7
  given: a turn that wrote a requirement the judge approved and touched nothing confirmed
  when: the turn ends
  then: the owner gets no question and no line about it beyond the status count
- id: EX-8
  given: a test that passed with recorded evidence now fails
  when: the turn ends
  then: the plumb line names the behaviour that stopped working in the owner's words; the agent gets the
    failing test as text, and the owner is asked only if fixing it needs a trade-off
- id: EX-9
  given: a turn that wrote twelve requirements of one feature, none yet approved by the judges
  when: the turn ends
  then: at most one plumb line says they are being checked; no list of twelve lines and no read-back
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended for the owner to decide: in a live session the owner watches three turns: one that
    changes nothing for them (no plumb line), one that touches a requirement with passing checks (one
    coloured plumb line under the answer, the answer unchanged), and one that raises a question (one line
    saying a question waits, the question itself in the question tool).'
  kind: live-session
