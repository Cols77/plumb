title: A record-changing question always has a card before the owner answers
statement: When the agent’s reply asks the owner something whose answer would confirm, reject or change
  a record, choose a conflict’s side or settle a stale link, plumb raises that question as a card at the
  turn end (from the reply, with its options) so the owner’s answer is read against it; when the owner
  answers such a question that has no card yet, plumb raises the card then and applies the answer to it,
  citing the owner’s words
