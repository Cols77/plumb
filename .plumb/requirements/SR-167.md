id: SR-167
title: A record-changing question always has a card before the owner answers
statement: When the agent’s reply asks the owner something whose answer would confirm, reject or change
  a record, choose a conflict’s side or settle a stale link, plumb raises that question as a card at the
  turn end (from the reply, with its options) so the owner’s answer is read against it; when the owner
  answers such a question that has no card yet, plumb raises the card then and applies the answer to it,
  citing the owner’s words
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 'the agent’s reply asks: Reword SR-096 to follow INT-028, or keep it? with no card'
  when: the turn ends
  then: a choice card for SR-096 with those two options is in the owner’s panel, and the agent is told
    it was raised
  quote: we should have a mechanism that prevents what can go wrong
  prompt: 70d3fa51775f21ac
- id: EX-2
  given: that card exists
  when: 'the owner replies: reword it'
  then: the card is answered with that option, citing the reply, and the agent is told; no new read-back
    card appears
- id: EX-3
  given: the agent asked such a question and the owner answered before any turn end raised a card
  when: the prompt hook reads the answer
  then: plumb raises the card from the question and applies the answer to it, in one step, logged with
    both
- id: EX-4
  given: 'the agent’s reply asks: Run the slow tests now or later?'
  when: the turn ends
  then: 'no card: the answer changes no record'
