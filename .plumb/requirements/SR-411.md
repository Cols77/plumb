id: SR-411
title: 'The studies class: ideas, new, writing, out of date, read'
statement: 'When the agent proposes a study idea plumb announces it in the band through the studies chip,
  in brass, with no toast. The studies class of the pane is sectioned: ideas, new, writing with its bar,
  out of date, and read folded by default. An idea''s expanded row shows its why and the depths to choose
  from (Markdown only, with diagrams, video), the agent''s recommended one marked, and not now; choosing
  a depth accepts the idea and the agent writes the study, which the pane lists as new; a new study''s
  action opens it in the browser room and it leaves the new section. A written study whose records changed
  is listed out of date naming what changed, with rewrite and not now as its actions; nothing is rewritten
  without the owner''s yes. A choice acts on the expanded row only. The room never opens by itself, and
  operating-system notifications are not used.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the agent proposes a study idea
  when: the band draws
  then: the studies chip appears or its count grows, in brass; no toast, no title in the band
- id: EX-2
  given: two ideas wait
  when: the owner opens studies and expands the second idea
  then: its why and the four choices are drawn under it; choosing with diagrams accepts that idea only,
    the first stays waiting
- id: EX-3
  given: a study is new
  when: the owner expands it and presses open in the room
  then: the room opens on that study and the row moves to the read section
- id: EX-4
  given: a record of a written study changed
  when: the owner opens studies
  then: the study is in the out of date section naming what changed, with rewrite and not now; pressing
    not now leaves it marked and rewrites nothing
- id: EX-5
  given: an accepted study is being written
  when: the owner opens studies
  then: the writing section shows its title, its step and a brass bar
