id: SR-191
title: The judge model's supported verdict confirms requirements and...
statement: 'When the verdict cascade''s judge model finds a proposed requirement or decision supported,
  and that record''s premise is the owner''s verified words (not the quote alone), the record is confirmed
  by: auto-model with the verdict''s confidence and the model''s name, the end-of-turn summary lists each
  such confirmation once in the owner''s words, and one Undo press or the owner''s word revokes it; an
  intent is never confirmed by a model verdict, and JEV''s verdict confirms as SR-113 says'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: SR-185 is proposed, its premise is the owner's logged message, and the judge model says supported
    at 0.8
  when: the judge job applies its verdicts
  then: 'SR-185 is confirmed by: auto-model (haiku, 0.8) and the next summary lists it'
- id: EX-2
  given: INT-067 is proposed and the judge model says supported
  when: the job applies
  then: INT-067 stays proposed; the summary says it waits for the owner's words
- id: EX-3
  given: a decision whose premise is the quote alone
  when: the model says supported
  then: it stays proposed and the verdict is logged quote-only
- id: EX-4
  given: SR-185 was confirmed by the model
  when: the owner presses Undo on the summary line or says to undo it
  then: the confirmation is revoked and SR-185 is proposed again, counted as a 'no' label
proof:
  plan: 'a scratch repository with a proposed requirement, a logged message and the judge model scripted
    to say supported: run the judge job and show the confirmation, the summary line and the undo'
  kind: command
