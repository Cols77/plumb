---
id: SR-092
title: The clarify read-back questions itself and labels assumptions instead of asking
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'plumb-clarify questions its own read-back before showing it: unexamined absolutes, unstated
  tradeoffs, unsupported claims and contradictions become labelled assumptions by default, and a question
  only when the answer changes what gets built. plumb gives these to the agent as hints. Nothing is recorded
  for them and nothing waits on them. The read-back speaks at the owner''s level (ruling R18.2).'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the request "reminders must always arrive instantly"
  when: the read-back is shown
  then: 'it carries an assumption such as "Assumed: instantly means within a minute" and asks no question'
- id: EX-2
  given: the request "never keep anyone's email address, and email them their reminders"
  when: the agent clarifies it
  then: exactly one question is asked, since the answer changes what gets built
- id: EX-3
  given: any clarification
  when: it ends
  then: nothing about the self-questioning is recorded and nothing waits on it
- id: EX-4
  given: a read-back that needs the idea of a confirmed requirement
  when: it is shown
  then: it explains that idea in one plain sentence before using it
