id: SR-291
title: The band shows the intent loop's step and the step's progress
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'While an intent loop is open, plumb''s band grows to at most three lines: the loop''s title
  with a strip of the loop''s steps (clarify, plan, build, proven) marking done, current and to come;
  and a bar for the current step''s tasks with the task in plain words and done of total. The section
  can be folded to the title alone (SR-305). A new study is counted in the first line''s studies chip,
  never drawn as a line of its own. When the loop ends the band returns to its one line.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an intent loop whose clarify gate passed and whose plan has 7 items, 3 written
  when: the owner looks at the band
  then: it shows 'clarify ✓ plan ● build ○ proven ○' and a brass bar 'writing the plan · 3/7'
- id: EX-2
  given: the loop is in clarify with 2 of 4 questions answered
  when: the owner looks at the band
  then: the bar reads 'clarifying · 2/4'
- id: EX-3
  given: the build step with an unknown total
  when: it runs
  then: the bar names the step and the elapsed time with no fill
- id: EX-4
  given: the owner's loop ends with intent done
  when: the next turn starts
  then: the band is back to its one line
- id: EX-5
  given: no intent loop is open
  when: plumb works on a long job
  then: the band stays one line with the progress SR-272 describes
- id: EX-6
  given: a gate refuses
  when: the owner looks at the band
  then: the current step is marked as stopped, in one line naming what it waits for
- id: EX-7
  given: the build step runs as a Claude Code workflow of 4 slices and the colours slice's gate has passed
  when: the owner looks at the band
  then: the bar reads 'build · slice 2/4 · colours gate passed', reported by that gate through plumb,
    while Claude Code's own workflow view keeps the agent-level detail
- id: EX-8
  given: a workflow phase's gate fails
  when: the owner looks at the band
  then: the bar names the phase whose gate failed and plumb lists no agents of its own
- id: EX-9
  given: the loop ended with a study still new
  when: the owner looks at the band
  then: it is one line; the study is reached from the studies chip in that line, never a second line
