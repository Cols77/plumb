---
id: SR-076
title: A content conflict becomes an intent conflict the owner resolves
feature: FEAT-006
statement: When both sides change the same field of a record differently (a statement, a title, an example,
  the body), the driver keeps the record parseable and writes an intent conflict (IC-###) into it holding
  the base, both versions and both branch labels; the record's hash then matches neither side, so it stays
  unconfirmed; plumb shows it as one conflict card with both versions and what merged on its own, with
  an optional JEV compatibility judgment that is advisory; only the owner chooses (mine, theirs, or a
  combined wording), in the owner's terminal or by the owner's own words relayed and checked like an approval,
  and the choice is logged.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: main changed SR-005's statement one way and feat-x changed it another way
  when: feat-x is merged into main
  then: SR-005 carries IC-001 with both statements and the labels main and feat-x, and SR-005 is unconfirmed
- id: EX-2
  given: IC-001 is open
  when: /plumb review or the merge check shows it
  then: one conflict card shows both statements, what merged on its own and the owner's choices in plain
    words
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner says "keep feat-x's version"
  when: the agent relays it
  then: SR-005 takes feat-x's statement, the conflict block is gone, a resolution event records the owner's
    quote, and SR-005 is confirmed again if feat-x's version was confirmed
- id: EX-4
  given: no logged owner words choose a side
  when: the agent runs the choice in its shell
  then: it is refused with exit 3 and nothing changes
- id: EX-5
  given: JEV is not configured
  when: the conflict card is shown
  then: it says no compatibility judgment is available, and choosing works the same
