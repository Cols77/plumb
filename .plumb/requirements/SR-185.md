---
id: SR-185
title: A judge job applies the verdicts it has; only the unanswered part...
statement: When the verdict cascade answers some records of a judge job and cannot answer the rest (the
  judge model unreachable, the diff too large, or an answer out of schema), the verdicts it has are applied
  and logged at once, the unanswered records stay in the job and wait, and one line says once per session
  window what waits and why; nothing is decided by rule for the unanswered part
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a judge job of five records, JEV clears two, and the judge model is unreachable
  when: the job runs
  then: the two verdicts are applied and logged as JEV's, the three others wait, and one line says they
    wait for the judge model
  quote: resolve the items still open after the merge
  prompt: 1b1e36e84c989a38
- id: EX-2
  given: the judge model answers out of schema for a job
  when: the job runs
  then: its records wait with the same waiting line, not an unresolved error
- id: EX-3
  given: the diff of a job is too large for the judge model
  when: the job runs
  then: its records wait with the waiting line that names the size
- id: EX-4
  given: every record of a job is answered
  when: the job runs
  then: nothing waits and no line is printed
proof:
  plan: 'a scratch repository with a judge job of several records, JEV scripted to clear some and the
    model scripted unreachable: show the cleared verdicts in the log and the waiting line once'
  kind: command
