id: SR-293
title: Studies are few and on point
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'During an intent loop the AI proposes at most one study idea per step, and only when no question
  waits for the owner (a study proposed ahead of waiting questions sits outside this count, as SR-336
  says), and none when nothing valuable stands out, chosen from: the key architectural choices being made
  or built, areas the owner likely understands little, what they need to know to choose well later, open
  conflicts waiting for their choice, and features worth considering. An idea is a title and one line
  of why, with the depth the AI recommends; the AI writes nothing before the owner accepts it. Once the
  owner accepts and picks a depth (the Markdown page only, with diagrams, or a video), the AI writes the
  study at that depth: a page in the browser room in plain words with the examples from the records, diagrams
  drawn when that depth is chosen.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the plan step introduces a new background job
  when: the AI picks a study
  then: it proposes the idea 'How plumb's background jobs run', recommending the depth with diagrams,
    and writes nothing until the owner accepts it
- id: EX-2
  given: an intent conflict waits for the owner's choice
  when: the AI picks a study
  then: it may propose a study laying out both sides so the owner can choose knowingly
- id: EX-3
  given: the picture says the owner already knows the topic of the step
  when: the AI picks a study
  then: it proposes none for that step
- id: EX-4
  given: the build step adds code that would make a new feature easy
  when: the AI picks a study
  then: it may propose that feature as a study, labelled a suggestion
- id: EX-5
  given: a loop with three steps
  when: it ends
  then: at most three studies were proposed
- id: EX-6
  given: the AI sees a study worth proposing in the plan step
  when: it proposes it
  then: only the idea exists (title, why, recommended depth) and no study page is written
- id: EX-7
  given: the owner accepted an idea and chose 'diagrams'
  when: the AI writes the study
  then: the room shows it in plain words with its diagrams drawn
- id: EX-8
  given: the owner accepted an idea and chose 'Markdown only'
  when: the AI writes the study
  then: the page has no diagram, even where the AI recommended one
- id: EX-9
  given: the owner declines an idea
  when: the AI next picks studies
  then: nothing is written and the idea is not proposed again
