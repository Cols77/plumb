id: SR-249
title: An older record's words are looked for in the owner's own past...
feature: FEAT-008
statement: For a record whose words plumb never saw logged, the cascade looks for them in this project's
  past session transcripts, reading only messages the owner typed there; the judge model's question quotes
  the words found with their date, or says none were found and asks what the owner meant. A transcript
  match alone approves nothing, and an already approved record is skipped.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an older record whose words plumb never saw, found in a past transcript of 4 Oct
  when: the cascade reaches it
  then: it is not fast-tracked; the judge model's question quotes the found words with their date, and
    the transcript match alone approves nothing
- id: EX-2
  given: an older record whose words are in no transcript
  when: the cascade reaches it
  then: the judge model's question says the words were not found and asks what the owner meant
- id: EX-3
  given: past transcripts holding compaction summaries, tool results, system reminders, task notifications,
    cross-session messages, and a worktree's or another project's transcript folder
  when: plumb looks for an older record's words
  then: only messages the owner typed in this project's own folder are read
- id: EX-4
  given: INT-009 is already approved
  when: the cascade runs for a requirement deriving from it
  then: INT-009 is skipped and no model is asked about it
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended: on a scratch project with an older unapproved record whose words sit in a past transcript,
    the owner sees one question quoting those words with their date, and the record stays proposed until
    the answer. The owner decides whether this is the proof.'
  kind: live-session
