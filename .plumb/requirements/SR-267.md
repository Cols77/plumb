id: SR-267
title: A question to the owner stands alone and holds what is needed to...
feature: FEAT-005
statement: 'plumb puts something to the owner only as a question, through the question tool or the study
  room: one question, options each with a one-line consequence and what it leaves undone, the recommended
  one first with its reason, the evidence, and which request raised it; readable cold, in the owner''s
  words, with no ids, digests or plumb vocabulary. There is no card to read and press, and no question
  is restated as prose.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a merge conflict on a requirement between main and a branch
  when: its question is put to the owner
  then: it names the requirement by title, shows both versions and a proposed combined one with what each
    choice does, and no IC- or SR- id appears
- id: EX-2
  given: an early conflict between two worktrees
  when: its question is put to the owner
  then: it has the same shape and no ids
- id: EX-3
  given: a question read the next day in the study room
  when: the owner opens it
  then: it names the request it came from with its date and what changes with the answer, and needs nothing
    from the conversation
- id: EX-4
  given: a question's text contains 'settled', 'trial', a record id or a digest
  when: plumb puts it to the owner
  then: 'that word is not shown: the owner''s own terms are used instead'
- id: EX-5
  given: the agent files a question lacking options, consequences or the reason for the recommended one
  when: the turn ends
  then: the question is refused as incomplete and the agent is told what is missing
- id: EX-6
  given: a question whose evidence is a 60,000-character diff
  when: it is shown in the study room
  then: the diff is folded with its size and can be opened in full; it is not cut silently
- id: EX-7
  given: a code change under a confirmed requirement that the recorded intent cannot settle
  when: its question is put to the owner
  then: it shows the requirement's current wording, the change itself, the owner's words it traces to,
    and for each option what it would change, with why the recommended one is first
- id: EX-8
  given: questions are waiting
  when: the agent replies
  then: it asks them through the question tool and does not restate them as a list to answer in prose
- id: EX-9
  given: the agent is unsure whether its own records make sense or converge, with no contradiction in
    the owner's intent
  when: plumb checks
  then: 'nothing is put to the owner: the judges decide, and what cannot settle goes to the debug workflow
    (P5)'
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended for the owner to decide: in a live session on a scratch repository two worktrees
    change one requirement differently; the owner receives one question-tool question naming the requirement
    by title, both versions and a combined one, the recommended option first with its reason, and no id;
    then the agent files a question without consequences and is shown refused.'
  kind: live-session
