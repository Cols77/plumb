---
id: SR-086
title: Confirm buttons record the owner's confirmation of exactly what was shown
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'The band, the panel and /plumb answers carry Confirm and Show all buttons on what awaits the
  owner. A press is something only the owner can do; the model cannot press. The panel proves the press
  to plumb with a secret only it holds for that session, and plumb records the owner''s own confirmation
  of exactly the records shown with that button, at the wording shown; a record changed since it was drawn
  is left out and named. A typed "ok" still works. One path in plumb serves every owner-only action offered
  as a button: confirming, undoing a confirmation, a group, a big plan, picking one of two versions, automatic
  confirmation on or off, and skipping the full test run at push (rulings R2, R19).'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the band shows two requirements awaiting the owner
  when: the owner presses Confirm
  then: plumb records the owner's confirmation of exactly those two at the wording shown, and the band
    says they are confirmed
- id: EX-2
  given: one of the two shown requirements was edited after the band drew it
  when: the owner presses Confirm
  then: only the unchanged one is confirmed and the other is named as changed since it was shown
- id: EX-3
  given: an agent session
  when: the agent tries plumb's button-press step from its own shell
  then: it is refused and nothing is written
- id: EX-4
  given: a big plan whose check still finds gaps
  when: the band draws it
  then: no Confirm button is offered for it
- id: EX-5
  given: three records offered in one group
  when: the owner presses Show all
  then: the cards of all three records are shown, and Confirm still confirms exactly those three
- id: EX-6
  given: a card asks whether to keep the CSV export
  when: the owner presses Leave open
  then: nothing is confirmed, settled or rejected, a card-press with answer open is logged, and the card's
    status becomes left open
- id: EX-7
  given: a card asks whether to keep the CSV export
  when: the owner presses Ask for a change
  then: 'nothing is confirmed, a card-press with answer change is logged, the question is marked change
    requested, the message box is prefilled with "Change to ''CSV export'': " (DEC-032), and the owner''s
    next message is read against that card'
- id: EX-8
  given: the owner pressed Ask for a change and then sent a message about something else
  when: the reader reads it
  then: the card is not confirmed and stays open with its change request
- id: EX-9
  given: an agent writes an approval saying by owner, via button, with no offer drawn for that record
    at that version
  when: status is computed
  then: the record is not confirmed and validation reports the entry
- id: EX-10
  given: a trial card whose suggested answers include no
  when: the owner presses No
  then: the item is rejected exactly as a typed "no" would reject it, and a card-press with answer rejected
    is logged
