---
id: SR-182
title: 'The verdict judge is a cascade: JEV first, a judge model below its...'
statement: A verdict on whether a record is supported by the owner’s words asks JEV first; when JEV’s
  probability is at or above the threshold calibrated on the owner’s labels with no false accept, the
  verdict is JEV’s and may feed auto-approval; below it, or with JEV not configured, the configured judge
  model (judge_model, haiku by default, sonnet allowed) gives the verdict used by cards and the tidy job,
  and a model verdict never feeds auto-approval; with neither reachable the verdict waits and the step
  says so once
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: JEV gives 0.9 for INT-003’s read-back and the calibrated threshold is 0.43
  when: the verdict is asked
  then: the verdict is supported on JEV’s answer, the judge model is not called, and auto-approval may
    use it
  quote: Looks perfect, go for it
  prompt: b6e084d7a7376206
- id: EX-2
  given: JEV gives 0.3 for SR-005’s example restatement
  when: the verdict is asked
  then: the judge model judges it; its verdict is shown on the card and used by the tidy job; auto-approval
    does not use it
- id: EX-3
  given: judge_model is set to sonnet
  when: a verdict falls below JEV’s threshold
  then: Sonnet gives it, logged with the model’s name
- id: EX-4
  given: no JEV key and no judge model reachable
  when: the verdict is asked
  then: the verdict waits, the step says so once, and nothing is decided by rule
proof:
  plan: 'a scratch repository with a logged owner message and a proposed record: run the verdict with
    JEV scripted high, then low, and show which judge answered and that only the first may auto-approve'
