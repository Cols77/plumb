---
id: SR-308
title: An intent loop tidies its own working files when it ends
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'When plumb intent done passes, the loop removes its own working files: its loop state, its
  findings and plan files, its capture documents, its judge results and its withdrawn or answered cards;
  records, commits and log entries stay. Another loop''s files are never touched.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a loop with a plan file, two findings files and a capture document
  when: intent done passes
  then: those files are gone and its records stay
- id: EX-2
  given: another session's open loop
  when: this loop ends
  then: the other loop's files are untouched
- id: EX-3
  given: intent done is refused for a missing proof
  when: it returns
  then: nothing is removed
proof:
  plan: 'This loop''s own end: its working files are gone, the other loops'' are not.'
