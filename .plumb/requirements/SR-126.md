title: The panel says why it is not showing
statement: When the panel cannot reach plumb, it writes one line naming the reason once per session instead
  of drawing nothing silently
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: no Python for plumb is found
  when: a session starts
  then: one dim line says plumb's panel is off because no Python for plumb was found
- id: EX-2
  given: plumb refuses the panel's hello
  when: a session starts
  then: one line says the panel is off and gives plumb's reason
- id: EX-3
  given: the panel connects
  when: a session starts
  then: no such line appears
