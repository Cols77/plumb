id: SR-129
title: A card is a read-back or a choice, and stands alone
statement: Every card the owner sees is either the read-back of one request (proposal in the owner's words,
  flippable assumptions, at most one won't) or one choice (one question, options each with a one-line
  consequence, the recommended option first, two lines saying which request raised it and what changes
  with the answer); it is readable with no memory of the conversation, in the owner's words, with no ids,
  digests or plumb vocabulary
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a request whose read-back has five will lines, two won't, four assumptions and three examples
  when: its card is shown
  then: the card has the proposal in one to three sentences, the assumptions the owner could disagree
    with, and the one won't that rules out something expected; examples, ids and the quote are left to
    plumb show
- id: EX-2
  given: a card read the next day from the backlog
  when: the owner opens it
  then: it names the request it came from with its date and what changes with the answer, and needs nothing
    from the conversation
- id: EX-3
  given: a card's text contains 'settled', 'trial', a record id or a digest
  when: plumb renders it
  then: 'that word is not on the card: the owner''s own terms are used instead'
- id: EX-4
  given: an agent files a stale-link card lacking options, consequences or the reason for the recommended
    one (SR-116 EX-3)
  when: the turn ends
  then: the card is refused as incomplete and the agent is told what is missing
