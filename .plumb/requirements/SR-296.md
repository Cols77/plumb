---
id: SR-296
title: One landing job runs at each merge into main
feature: FEAT-003
statement: 'When work merges into main, a background landing job runs, in order: the tidy of what the
  landed records supersede (SR-286), the log compaction (SR-287), the judge backlog queued, then every
  count the status still shows (stale links, failing or unrun acceptance tests, validate errors, records
  waiting on a judge that cannot be queued) written down as debug cases. The plumb line at the next turn
  end says what was tidied, how many judge jobs were queued and how many cases went to debugging. Running
  it again for the same landing changes nothing.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a landing whose records supersede two documents
  when: the merge into main completes
  then: the tidy commit removes them, the log is compacted, and the next plumb line names both
- id: EX-2
  given: 40 records waiting on a judge after a landing
  when: the landing job runs
  then: judge jobs for all 40 are queued before the next turn, and nothing waits for a turn end to queue
    them
- id: EX-3
  given: the status still counts 3 stale links after the tidy
  when: the landing job ends
  then: 3 debug cases are written and the plumb line says 3 cases went to debugging
- id: EX-4
  given: the landing job already ran for this merge
  when: it runs again
  then: nothing is deleted, queued or written
- id: EX-5
  given: a fast-forward merge into main
  when: it completes
  then: the landing job runs as for a merge commit
- id: EX-6
  given: the landing job fails on one step
  when: the failure is caught
  then: the following steps still run and the failure itself becomes a debug case
- id: EX-7
  given: a merge into main that the quick check held and the owner finished with git commit
  when: the commit is made
  then: the post-commit hook starts the landing job as a git merge does
