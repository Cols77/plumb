id: SR-187
title: The clear part of a message is taken; only the unclear part is...
statement: 'When the owner''s message does only part of what the reader looks for, the reader applies
  the clear part and leaves the rest: a side of a conflict picked in plain words (not the option''s exact
  label) picks that option; a message that answers a card and adds a new request has the answer applied
  to the card and the new request read back; a part that is not clear stays open and is the only part
  asked about again'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a conflict card whose options are Keep this, Keep the other and Both
  when: the owner replies 'keep what main has'
  then: the reader names the option that side means and the conflict is settled on it
  quote: Try to find the question that answers globally to the questions you have
  prompt: 1b1e36e84c989a38
- id: EX-2
  given: a choice card with options 1, 2 and 3 shown in text
  when: the owner replies '2, and also make the pane resizable'
  then: the card is answered with option 2 and a read-back card is raised for the pane request only
- id: EX-3
  given: the same conflict card
  when: the owner replies 'hmm, either could work'
  then: the conflict stays open and nothing is recorded
- id: EX-4
  given: a message that answers a card and adds a request the reader cannot read
  when: the request check runs
  then: the card is answered and the read-back for the request says what was unclear
proof:
  plan: 'a scratch repository with a conflict card and a scripted reader: a plain-words side picks the
    option, an answer plus a request answers the card and raises one read-back'
