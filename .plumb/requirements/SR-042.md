---
id: SR-042
title: plumb jev calibrate measures the backend on the owner's own decisions before any auto-approval
feature: FEAT-002
statement: plumb jev calibrate builds a labelled set from the log (records the owner confirmed and never
  revoked as positives; revoked records and deterministic perturbations as negatives), scores it per claim
  kind, records thresholds, false-accept rate and recall with the resolved model version as a calibration
  event, and passes only with enough items and negatives and no false accept. With the heuristic it reports
  that calibration does not apply.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: fewer than 20 labelled intent read-backs
  when: plumb jev calibrate runs
  then: the intent-readback kind is not passing and auto-approve on refuses
- id: EX-2
  given: a labelled set where one revoked read-back scores 0.93
  when: calibration chooses the threshold
  then: the threshold is above 0.93
- id: EX-3
  given: the heuristic backend
  when: plumb jev calibrate runs
  then: 'it reports "heuristic: no auto-approval, calibration not applicable" and writes no calibration
    event'
