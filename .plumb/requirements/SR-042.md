---
id: SR-042
title: plumb jev calibrate measures the backend on the owner's own decisions before any auto-approval
feature: FEAT-002
statement: 'Calibration of settling is read from the owner''s own answers: the labelled set per record
  kind is the owner''s logged trial answers (yes, and no as a misreading), their confirmations by words
  or buttons as yes, and revoked or undone records as no, each paired with the judge''s logged confidence
  at judgment time (SR-106). plumb records thresholds, false-accept rate and recall with the resolved
  model version and the prompt digest as a calibration event, and a kind passes only with mixed evidence:
  at least 20 of the owner''s past decisions, at least 5 ''no'' examples, at most half of them generated,
  and no wrong accept of any ''no'' example. A model or prompt change suspends settling until calibration
  reruns. With no judge model it reports that calibration does not apply.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: fewer than 20 labelled intent read-backs
  when: calibration runs
  then: the intent kind is not passing and settling for intents cannot be switched on
- id: EX-2
  given: a labelled set where one misread record got the judge's confidence 0.93
  when: calibration chooses the threshold
  then: the threshold is above 0.93
- id: EX-3
  given: no judge model is configured
  when: calibration runs
  then: it reports that calibration does not apply and writes no calibration event
