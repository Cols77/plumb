---
id: SR-042
title: plumb jev calibrate measures the backend on the owner's own decisions before any auto-approval
feature: FEAT-002
statement: 'plumb jev calibrate builds a labelled set from the log per record kind (records the owner
  confirmed and never revoked as yes; revoked records, records edited after an auto-approval, and generated
  variations as no), records thresholds, false-accept rate and recall with the resolved model version
  as a calibration event, and passes only with mixed evidence (R13 Q1): at least 20 of the owner''s past
  decisions and at least 5 ''no'' examples, at most half of them generated, and no wrong accept of any
  ''no'' example; the real/generated split is shown in plumb jev status and the event. With the heuristic
  it reports that calibration does not apply.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: fewer than 20 labelled intent read-backs
  when: plumb jev calibrate runs
  then: the intent-readback kind is not passing and auto-approve on refuses
- id: EX-2
  given: a labelled set where one revoked read-back scores 0.93
  when: calibration chooses the threshold
  then: the threshold is above 0.93
- id: EX-3
  given: the heuristic backend
  when: plumb jev calibrate runs
  then: 'it reports "heuristic: no auto-approval, calibration not applicable" and writes no calibration
    event'
