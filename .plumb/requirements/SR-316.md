---
id: SR-316
title: A study you accepted can be shown as a video, rendered in the...
statement: When the owner picks 'video' as the depth of an accepted study idea, plumb starts a background
  render of the agent's Manim scene once its storyboard is complete (SR-364); the study shows its text
  at once with 'video rendering…', the video appears when the render ends and passes its check against
  the storyboard (SR-366), and a failed render or check leaves the text with the reason. No render starts
  for a depth other than video.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an accepted idea 'How the judge cascade runs' with the agent recommending video
  when: the owner picks 'video'
  then: the study opens with its text and 'video rendering…', and the turn does not wait for the render
- id: EX-2
  given: a video render in progress
  when: it ends and the reviewer's check passes
  then: the study shows the video in place of 'video rendering…'
- id: EX-3
  given: the scene raises an error while rendering
  when: the render ends
  then: the study keeps its text and says the video could not be rendered and why; no render is retried
    by itself
- id: EX-4
  given: the owner picks 'text only' or 'diagrams'
  when: the study is written
  then: no render starts
- id: EX-5
  given: the machine has no LaTeX
  when: a video is rendered
  then: the scene uses plain text and shapes and the video still renders
proof:
  plan: In a live intent loop, the agent proposes a study idea recommending video; the owner accepts and
    picks video; the study shows 'video rendering…', then the video plays in the room; every screenshot
    (PNG) is opened on the owner's screen.
  kind: live-session
