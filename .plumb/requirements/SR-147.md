---
id: SR-147
title: The clarify card prints its questions, not the read-back's lines
statement: When the hook prints a clarify card, before building or at the end of a turn, the card holds
  the topic, the questions and the suggested answers; the read-back's will, won't, assumed and example
  lines are not printed, though they stay on the stored card so answering it still confirms the assumptions
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a clarify card with two questions and a read-back of four will lines, one won't and two assumptions
  when: the hook prints it
  then: the output has the topic line, the two questions and the suggested answers, and no line starting
    with 'I will', 'I will not', 'Assumed' or 'For example'
- id: EX-2
  given: the same card
  when: the owner answers it
  then: the two labelled assumptions are confirmed exactly as before
- id: EX-3
  given: a stale-link card whose evidence says a test passed earlier and fails now
  when: the hook prints it
  then: its evidence line is printed as before
