title: A record is confirmed through its read-back, never asked about alone
statement: The records a read-back creates are confirmed by the owner's answer to that read-back; a record
  is re-asked only when it changed or a merge brought a second version, and then as a choice showing both
  versions side by side
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a read-back that created an intent, two requirements and a decision
  when: the owner answers ok
  then: all four are confirmed by that one answer and no card asks about any of them again
- id: EX-2
  given: a decision changed since the owner saw it
  when: the owner presses No on the old card
  then: the owner sees one choice card with the version they answered and the new one side by side, never
    a title with yes/no
- id: EX-3
  given: a merge brings two versions of one requirement
  when: the cards are shown
  then: one choice card shows both versions and what each means, with 'keep this one', 'keep the other'
    and 'both' as options
