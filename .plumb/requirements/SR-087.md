---
id: SR-087
title: plumb gives Claude its background once and shows nothing twice
feature: FEAT-007
statement: With the in-app panel active, plumb's short background on what is being built is given to Claude
  once per session and stays exactly the same until it changes, instead of being repeated with every message;
  the session-start check then leaves it out, and the one-line updates about newly relevant records are
  unchanged. Notes for the owner never reach the model, and notes for the model are never shown to the
  owner as if they had typed them. plumb remembers what it already gave or showed. When the panel fails
  to load, or Claude Code has no in-app panels, plumb's background checks show and give everything as
  before (ruling R19).
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the panel is active and three messages change no intent
  when: the three requests are sent to Claude
  then: plumb's background is identical in all three and is not repeated in any message
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner confirmed a new intent
  when: the next request is sent
  then: the background changes once
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner pressed Confirm on two records
  when: the next turn starts
  then: the owner saw a note and the model received one note naming what was confirmed; the owner's note
    never reached the model
- id: EX-4
  given: the panel failed to load
  when: a session runs a turn
  then: plumb's background checks print the status card and the summary and give the background at session
    start, as before
- id: EX-5
  given: the same card with the same content
  when: two turns end
  then: it is drawn once
