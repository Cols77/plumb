id: SR-360
title: A study is built from the records and traced to them
statement: Before a study is written, plumb gathers the requirements, decisions, examples and test states
  on its topic; the agent writes from that set and the study keeps links to those records. When one of
  them changes, the room marks the study out of date naming what changed and the band offers to rewrite
  it at the same depth; nothing is rewritten without the owner's yes. A topic with no records says so
  in the study.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the owner accepted a study on how merges are held
  when: it is written
  then: it is written from the gathered records on merges and lists them
  quote: we need proper intent clarification for the gaps you identified for study generation
  prompt: 7de21ead90d4109e
- id: EX-2
  given: a study links to a requirement
  when: that requirement is reworded
  then: the room marks the study out of date naming it and the band offers a refresh
- id: EX-3
  given: the topic has no records
  when: the study is written
  then: it says no record covers the topic
proof:
  plan: after landing, a requested study names its records; rewording one marks it out of date and the
    band offers a refresh
