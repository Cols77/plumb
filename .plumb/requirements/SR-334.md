id: SR-334
title: A backlog of questions is asked by principle, a few at a time
statement: When questions wait for the owner, plumb groups them by the principle they hinge on and asks
  only that principle, at most three questions per turn, between tasks; the answer settles the questions
  that hinge on it. A question that blocks the current task is asked at once. The others wait, and the
  turn-end line counts them.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 62 questions wait and 50 of them hinge on four principles
  when: a turn ends between tasks
  then: the owner is asked at most three questions, each a principle, and the turn-end line counts the
    rest
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner answers a principle
  when: the judge reads the questions that hinge on it
  then: those questions are settled from the answer and are not asked
- id: EX-3
  given: a question blocks the task the agent is doing
  when: it is raised
  then: it is asked at once, even if three were already asked this turn
- id: EX-4
  given: questions wait and the agent is in the middle of a task they don't block
  when: the agent goes on
  then: none of them is asked until the task ends
proof:
  plan: 'In a live Claude Code session on this repository, the owner sees: no card in plumb''s band and
    Hide staying hidden; waiting questions arriving through the question tool, at most three per turn
    and grouped by principle, each offering to study it in the room when there is more to see; no question
    the owner already answered coming back; and the turn-end line counting what still waits.'
