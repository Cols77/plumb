id: SR-294
title: A new study is announced in plumb's colours, never forced open
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'When the AI proposes a study idea, plumb announces it in its band, where it shows its progress,
  with a brass line naming the idea, and no toast. Pressing it (a click, or the arrow keys and Enter)
  shows the idea''s why and the depths to choose from: the Markdown page only, with diagrams, or a video
  (shown as not available until video studies exist), the AI''s recommended one marked, and ''not now''.
  Choosing a depth accepts the idea; the AI then writes the study, and the band announces it as a new
  study, which pressing opens in the browser room on that study. The room never opens by itself, and operating-system
  notifications are not used.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the AI writes a study during the plan step
  when: it is ready
  then: 'the band, under the step strip and bar, shows a brass line ''new study: How plumb''s background
    jobs run'' and no toast appears'
- id: EX-2
  given: a study was announced
  when: the owner does nothing
  then: the browser room stays closed and the study waits in it
- id: EX-3
  given: the band shows a new study line
  when: the owner moves to it with the arrow keys and presses Enter, or clicks it
  then: the browser room opens on that study
- id: EX-4
  given: the owner opened the study from the band
  when: the band is next drawn
  then: the study line goes away
- id: EX-5
  given: a study is still new when the loop ends with intent done
  when: the band is next drawn
  then: the band is its one line, ending with a short brass 'new study' that can be pressed; no second
    line
- id: EX-6
  given: the band's one line shows 'new study'
  when: the owner presses it (a click, or the arrow keys and Enter)
  then: a small menu in the band lists the new studies by title, and choosing one opens the browser room
    on it
- id: EX-7
  given: the band shows a study idea
  when: the owner presses it
  then: they see its why and the choices 'Markdown only', 'with diagrams' (recommended), 'video (not available
    yet)' and 'not now'
- id: EX-8
  given: the owner chose 'with diagrams' on an idea
  when: the agent's next turn starts
  then: the agent is told the idea was accepted at that depth and writes the study
- id: EX-9
  given: the owner chose 'not now'
  when: the band is next drawn
  then: the idea's line is gone and nothing was written
