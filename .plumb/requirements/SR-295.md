id: SR-295
title: What needs the owner's action at a turn's end reaches them as a...
feature: FEAT-007
statement: When a turn ends with something only the owner can settle (a conflict awaiting their choice,
  a regression on a confirmed requirement, a drift whose meaning changed), the stop hook hands it to the
  agent as text with the instruction to ask it; the agent puts it to the owner through the question tool
  in plain words with options, or offers a study when there is more to read; the owner's text carries
  at most the one plumb line (SR-265), never the card.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a turn ends with an open conflict awaiting the owner's choice
  when: the stop hook runs
  then: 'the owner''s text shows at most ''plumb: 1 question for you'' and the agent''s channel carries
    the conflict with the instruction to ask it with the question tool'
- id: EX-2
  given: a regression on a confirmed requirement at turn end
  when: the next turn starts
  then: the agent asks the owner through the question tool what to do, with the failing example in plain
    words
- id: EX-3
  given: a turn ends with only stale links and untraced files the agent can fix
  when: the stop hook runs
  then: the owner sees nothing about them and the agent's channel names them
- id: EX-4
  given: a turn ends with a conflict that needs both sides read
  when: the agent routes it
  then: it may offer a study laying out both sides, choosing nothing
proof:
  plan: 'In the live loop, end a turn with an open conflict and stale links: the owner''s text shows one
    plum line or nothing, no card text, and the agent asks the conflict through the question tool; every
    screenshot (PNG) is opened in a window on the owner''s screen when the proof is shown, not only saved.'
  kind: live-session
