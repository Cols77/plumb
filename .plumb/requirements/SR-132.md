id: SR-132
title: A card lives as long as its request
statement: An unanswered card is withdrawn when a newer read-back on the same thing replaces it or when
  its branch is merged; the merge summary names in one line the records that went in unconfirmed; a withdrawn
  card confirms nothing and its records stay proposed
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a card asking which findings to resolve, then a newer request naming them
  when: the newer read-back is recorded
  then: the older card is withdrawn and only the new one, if any, is open
- id: EX-2
  given: a branch with two open cards
  when: it is merged
  then: 'both are withdrawn and the merge summary says ''went in unconfirmed: <titles>'' once'
- id: EX-3
  given: a card from a previous session whose branch is gone
  when: the backlog is shown
  then: it is not in the backlog
- id: EX-4
  given: a withdrawn card
  when: plumb status runs
  then: its records are still listed as proposed
