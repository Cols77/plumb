id: SR-248
title: 'A record is approved by the judge cascade: JEV above 0.9...'
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'A proposed intent, requirement or decision whose premise is the owner''s verified words is
  approved without a question when the fast track is on and JEV scores it above 0.9 and above its calibrated
  threshold; the 0.9 floor is JEV''s. Otherwise the judge model returns question-tool questions; after
  the owner answers, JEV judges again, and the record is approved when JEV clears the floor or the judge
  model says the intent is clear (its self-reported confidence is logged, never compared to a bar). With
  the fast track off, nothing is approved without a question round. An edit of a record the owner confirmed
  by their own answer, or one member of a set, is never fast-tracked: the edit goes to the judge model''s
  questions, and a set is approved whole (P6). Tests, links or a consistency check never approve.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the fast track is on, calibration passed with threshold 0.93, and INT-067 is proposed with the
    owner's logged message as its premise
  when: JEV scores INT-067 at 0.96
  then: INT-067 is approved by auto-jev with the score, marked judge-approved, and no question is asked
- id: EX-2
  given: the fast track is on and calibrated, and the owner said "yes" to one proposal shown whole
  when: JEV judges the interpretation with a confidence above 0.9
  then: the record is approved by auto-jev with the score, marked judge-approved, with the owner's "yes"
    kept with the proposal it answered; no question is asked
- id: EX-3
  given: the same calibration, and SR-185 is proposed
  when: JEV scores SR-185 at 0.92, above 0.9 but below the calibrated 0.93
  then: SR-185 is not fast-tracked; the judge model returns questions put to the owner through the question
    tool
- id: EX-4
  given: the fast track is on and calibrated, and JEV scores SR-185 at 0.7
  when: the owner answers the judge model's questions in the question tool and JEV judges again at 0.95
  then: SR-185 is approved, logged with both verdicts and the owner's answers with the questions they
    answered
- id: EX-5
  given: after the owner's answers JEV scores SR-185 at 0.85 and the judge model (haiku) says the intent
    is clear at 0.8
  when: the cascade applies its verdicts
  then: SR-185 is approved by auto-model (haiku, 0.8), marked judge-approved, and no further question
    is asked
- id: EX-6
  given: a decision whose premise is the quote alone, not the owner's verified words
  when: JEV scores it at 0.97
  then: 'it is not fast-tracked: the verdict is logged quote-only and the judge model asks the owner about
    it'
- id: EX-7
  given: a requirement whose linked code exists, whose tests pass and that a model finds consistent
  when: the end of a turn runs
  then: it stays proposed and no 'settled by context' line or offer appears
- id: EX-8
  given: the owner types /plumb settle on intent
  when: the prompt hook reads it
  then: plumb says that switch no longer exists and nothing changes
- id: EX-9
  given: the fast track is on and calibrated, and the agent edits a requirement the owner confirmed by
    their own answer
  when: JEV scores the edit at 0.97
  then: 'it is not fast-tracked: it stays an unconfirmed edit and the judge model''s questions go to the
    owner through the question tool'
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended: in a live session with the fast track on and calibrated, the owner states two small
    intents, one plain and one ambiguous; plumb''s status then shows the plain one judge-approved by JEV
    with its score and no question asked, while the ambiguous one brings question-tool questions and,
    after the owner''s answers, shows approved with both verdicts in its trail. The owner decides whether
    this walk is the proof.'
  kind: live-session
