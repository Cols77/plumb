---
id: SR-287
title: Old log entries are compacted into one archive segment per kind
feature: FEAT-001
statement: 'At a merge into main the tidy job moves into one archive segment per kind every log entry
  older than the records it served: an entry all of whose named records have since been superseded, retired
  or changed; test-run evidence a newer run of the same tests replaces; and an entry naming no record
  that is older than the previous landing. Each entry keeps its digest and prev; validate checks the chain
  through the segments and reports any change inside them. A session start never reads a segment; calibration,
  the judge jobs, verify and explain read through them as they read live entries.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: about 7,800 judgment entries, most of them about records the version 2 landing superseded
  when: the tidy job compacts the log
  then: the entries about superseded records sit in the judgment kind's archive segment, the ones about
    current records stay as entry files, and plumb validate is clean
- id: EX-2
  given: a live entry whose prev names an entry now in an archive segment
  when: plumb validate runs
  then: the prev resolves in the segment and no chain break is reported
- id: EX-3
  given: an entry inside an archive segment edited by hand
  when: plumb validate runs
  then: it reports the kind, the segment and the entry whose digest no longer matches
- id: EX-4
  given: a compacted log
  when: a session starts
  then: the brief and the hooks read only live entry files and open no archive segment
- id: EX-5
  given: test-run evidence that makes a current test fresh
  when: the tidy job compacts the log
  then: that evidence stays live and plumb verify keeps the test fresh
- id: EX-6
  given: a branch created before the compaction that appended entries chained onto now-archived ones
  when: it merges into main
  then: git merges without conflict and plumb validate is clean
- id: EX-7
  given: the agent asks why a retired record was retired
  when: plumb explains it
  then: the answer reads the archive segment for the entries that record the retirement
- id: EX-8
  given: the log was already compacted for this landing
  when: the tidy job runs again
  then: no segment or entry file changes
- id: EX-9
  given: test-run evidence for a test that a newer run of the same test has replaced
  when: the tidy job compacts the log
  then: the older evidence moves to the evidence kind's segment and the newer one stays live
- id: EX-10
  given: a merge-evidence entry naming no record, older than the previous landing
  when: the tidy job compacts the log
  then: it moves to its kind's segment
- id: EX-11
  given: an approval of a current record, and the prompt entry its pre-intent names
  when: the tidy job compacts the log
  then: both stay live as entry files
- id: EX-12
  given: an approval of a record the landing superseded
  when: the tidy job compacts the log, and the agent then asks plumb explain about that record
  then: the approval moves to the approval kind's segment and the explanation still names who approved
    the record and when
- id: EX-13
  given: the owner's labelled answers and reversals, some about records the landing superseded
  when: calibration runs before and after the compaction
  then: it counts the same labelled set, with the same number of 'no' examples, both times
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended: show the entry counts per kind before and after the version 2 compaction, a clean
    plumb validate, and a session-start trace of the files read with no archive segment among them. The
    owner decides whether this is the proof.'
