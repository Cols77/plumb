id: SR-190
title: A judgment says when it had only the quote
statement: 'When a record''s premise is its quote alone because no logged prompt holds it, every judgment
  and verdict logged for it carries premise: quote-only with the reason (no prompt named, prompt not found,
  quote not verbatim), a calibration''s log entry and plumb jev status report how many items were judged
  on the quote alone, and nothing is decided by rule from it'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an intent with no prompt field
  when: JEV judges its read-back
  then: 'the judgment entry says premise: quote-only, reason: no prompt named'
- id: EX-2
  given: 25 of 46 labelled intents have a quote-only premise
  when: plumb jev calibrate runs
  then: 'the calibration entry records quote_only: 25 of 46 and plumb jev status prints that line'
- id: EX-3
  given: a record whose premise is the whole message
  when: it is judged
  then: the entry carries no quote-only mark
proof:
  plan: 'a scratch repository with one intent lacking a prompt and one with it: judge both and show the
    marks and the calibration count'
  kind: command
