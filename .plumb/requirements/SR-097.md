---
id: SR-097
title: A short answer is judged together with the question it answered
statement: When the owner's quote is a short answer (a letter, yes, ok), the intent stores the question
  it answered, and judgment and triage compare the read-back with question plus answer.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an intent quoting "A" with the question offering A. Mostly in the background, B. A checkpoint
    every turn
  when: JEV judges the read-back
  then: the claim it receives holds the question and the answer, not "A" alone
- id: EX-2
  given: a capture whose quote is "A" and no question
  when: plumb capture runs
  then: it warns that the answer needs its question and still records the intent
- id: EX-3
  given: a quote of a full sentence
  when: JEV judges it
  then: nothing is added to the quote
