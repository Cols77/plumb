---
id: SR-305
title: Pressing the band's step strip opens a pane showing the plumb loop
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'While an intent loop is open, the band''s step strip can be pressed (a click, or the arrow
  keys and Enter as for Claude Code''s own workflow entry); it opens plumb''s docked pane beside the conversation,
  in plum and brass, showing what plumb knows of the loop: each step with its gate and the findings left,
  the plan''s tasks done and left, the records the loop wrote or changed, and each requirement''s proof
  shown or not. It lists no agents or tokens (Claude Code''s workflow view shows those). Esc closes it.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a loop whose clarify and plan gates passed with 2 low findings each and whose build has 3 of
    4 tasks done
  when: the owner presses the step strip
  then: the pane shows clarify ✓ and plan ✓ with their low findings, build ● 3/4 with the task left named,
    and proven ○
- id: EX-2
  given: the loop's proof shows SR-291 working and SR-292 not shown
  when: the pane is open
  then: it lists the requirements by title, the first marked shown and the second not shown yet
- id: EX-3
  given: the loop runs as a Claude Code workflow
  when: the pane is open
  then: it shows no agent names, counts or tokens
- id: EX-4
  given: the pane is open
  when: the owner presses Esc
  then: the pane closes and the band stays
- id: EX-5
  given: no intent loop is open
  when: the owner looks at the band
  then: the strip is not drawn and nothing opens the pane for a loop
proof:
  plan: In a live loop, press the step strip with the arrow keys and Enter, screenshot the pane with steps,
    findings, tasks, records and proofs, then Esc; every screenshot (PNG) is opened in a window on the
    owner's screen when the proof is shown, not only saved.
  kind: live-session
