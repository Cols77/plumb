id: SR-363
title: The turn-end line counts no waiting questions
statement: The owner's one plumb line at a turn end no longer counts questions waiting for the owner;
  it still says when an answer was applied, what the turn touched with its checks, and a behaviour that
  stopped working. A turn with nothing else for the owner shows nothing.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: three judge questions were raised and asked during the turn
  when: the turn ends
  then: no 'questions for you' text appears
- id: EX-2
  given: a question the owner dismissed still waits
  when: the turn ends with nothing else for the owner
  then: plumb prints no line; the band still counts the waiting question
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner's answer was applied and the turn's 4 checks passed
  when: the turn ends
  then: one line says the answer was applied and the 4 checks pass, with no question count
proof:
  plan: In the same live session the turn ends with no 'N questions for you' line.
