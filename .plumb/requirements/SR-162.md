id: SR-162
title: A model withdraws cards your confirmed records already answer
statement: A background job, using the session's judge on its cheap model, reads each open card against
  the owner's confirmed records and withdraws a card only when a confirmed record answers it (quoting
  that record) or when it duplicates another open card; unsure keeps the card; it never answers a card
  and never counts a proposed record
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an open card asking whether a conflict may be answered through the agent in chat, and a confirmed
    decision saying it may
  when: the job runs
  then: the card is withdrawn, citing that decision's words
  quote: I shouldnt have to ask for those clean up resolutions
  prompt: bc4daa9a82466082
- id: EX-2
  given: the same card and only a proposed decision
  when: the job runs
  then: the card stays
- id: EX-3
  given: two open stale-link cards on SR-062 asking the same question
  when: the job runs
  then: one is withdrawn as a duplicate naming the other
- id: EX-4
  given: the judge cannot be reached
  when: the job runs
  then: nothing is withdrawn and one line says the check could not run
proof:
  plan: 'a live session with open cards answered by confirmed records: the turn end withdraws them and
    says so'
  kind: live-session
