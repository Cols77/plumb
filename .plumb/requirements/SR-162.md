id: SR-162
title: plumb resolves from the confirmed records what they already...
statement: 'A background job, using the session''s judge on its cheap model, reads each open card against
  the owner''s confirmed records and withdraws a card only when a confirmed record answers it (quoting
  that record) or when it duplicates another open card; unsure keeps the card; it never answers a card
  and never counts a proposed record. The same reading runs before an issue becomes a card: a merge conflict,
  a stale link, an untraced change or a gap that a confirmed record or decision settles is resolved from
  it, logged with that record and undoable; only what the confirmed records do not settle is presented'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: an open card asking whether a conflict may be answered through the agent in chat, and a confirmed
    decision saying it may
  when: the job runs
  then: the card is withdrawn, citing that decision's words
  quote: I shouldnt have to ask for those clean up resolutions
  prompt: bc4daa9a82466082
- id: EX-2
  given: the same card and only a proposed decision
  when: the job runs
  then: the card stays
- id: EX-3
  given: two open stale-link cards on SR-062 asking the same question
  when: the job runs
  then: one is withdrawn as a duplicate naming the other
- id: EX-4
  given: the judge cannot be reached
  when: the job runs
  then: nothing is withdrawn and one line says the check could not run
- id: EX-5
  given: a merge conflict on a requirement where a confirmed decision already chose one side's principle
  when: the conflict is found
  then: the side the decision implies is applied and logged with the decision; no card
  quote: plumb tools should always look at the available intent to be able to try resolve issues before
    presenting them to the user
  prompt: 16346cb668cf73ed
- id: EX-6
  given: a stale link whose target moved but still covers the requirement, per its tests and a confirmed
    example
  when: the stale check runs
  then: the link is repinned silently and logged
- id: EX-7
  given: an issue the confirmed records do not settle, or two confirmed records that contradict
  when: plumb reads them
  then: it is presented to the owner, with the records it looked at
- id: EX-8
  given: no judge can be reached
  when: the issue is found
  then: plumb says so and the issue is presented, not resolved by rule
proof:
  plan: 'a live session with open cards answered by confirmed records: the turn end withdraws them and
    says so'
  kind: live-session
