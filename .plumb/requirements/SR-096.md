id: SR-096
title: A rejected read-back is recaptured; it counts as a 'no' only when...
statement: 'When the owner rejects a proposed read-back, the agent recaptures it and the new version is
  judged and offered again. A rejection is recorded only from the owner''s own answer: the reader (a model
  independent of the coding agent) reading the owner''s logged message, or the owner''s Reject button;
  the coding agent cannot relay a rejection. The rejected version, pinned by its hash, counts as a real
  ''no'' for plumb jev calibrate only when the owner''s answer says it misread their words (a capture
  error); a rejection because the owner''s intent changed, or one whose reason the reader cannot tell,
  gives it no label.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 'INT-016 proposed at hash H and the owner''s logged message "INT-016: that records your solution,
    not what I asked"'
  when: the reader maps it to rejected as a misreading and the agent recaptures INT-016 at hash H2
  then: H is a real 'no' of the intent-readback kind and H2 is proposed, unlabelled until the owner answers
- id: EX-2
  given: 'INT-013 proposed at hash H and the owner''s logged message "INT-013: no. I don''t know what
    to do with pi-agent-factory just yet"'
  when: the reader maps it to rejected because the intent changed and the agent recaptures INT-013
  then: H gets no calibration label
- id: EX-3
  given: a rejection where the reader does not say the read-back misread the owner's words
  when: it is recorded
  then: it is recorded as an intent change, with no label, and the agent recaptures
- id: EX-4
  given: 'the words "INT-013: no" and no prompt the hook logged holds them'
  when: a rejection is claimed from them
  then: nothing is read and no rejection is recorded, as for an approval
- id: EX-5
  given: 'a logged prompt "INT-016: that records your solution, not what I asked"'
  when: the agent runs plumb relay --reject for INT-016 with a quote from it
  then: plumb refuses and records nothing; only the reader on the owner's message or the owner's button
    records a rejection
