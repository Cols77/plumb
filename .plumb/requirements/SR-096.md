---
id: SR-096
title: A rejected read-back is recaptured; it counts as a 'no' only when...
statement: When the owner rejects a proposed read-back, the reader reads the owner's rejection from the
  logged message, the agent recaptures the read-back, and the new version is judged and offered again.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: 'INT-016 proposed at hash H and the owner''s words "INT-016: that records your solution, not
    what I asked"'
  when: the agent relays the rejection as a capture error and recaptures INT-016 at hash H2
  then: H is a real 'no' of the intent-readback kind and H2 is proposed, unlabelled until the owner answers
- id: EX-2
  given: 'INT-013 proposed at hash H and the owner''s words "INT-013: no. I don''t know what to do with
    pi-agent-factory just yet"'
  when: the agent relays the rejection as an intent change and recaptures INT-013
  then: H gets no calibration label
- id: EX-3
  given: a rejection whose words do not say whether the read-back misread them
  when: the agent relays it
  then: it is recorded as an intent change, with no label, and the agent recaptures
- id: EX-4
  given: 'the owner''s words "INT-013: no" and no prompt the hook logged holds them'
  when: the agent relays a rejection
  then: it is refused, as an approval would be
