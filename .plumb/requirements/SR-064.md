---
id: SR-064
title: Owner commands take plain words and answer in the owner's terms
feature: FEAT-005
statement: plumb resolve maps the owner's words to records, paths and tests (IDs and paths first, then
  titles, statements, examples, quotes and features), and every owner command (explain, impact, show,
  review, align, view) accepts plain words through it, lists candidates in the owner's terms when the
  match is unclear, and answers with titles, quotes, examples and test states; the /plumb slash command
  routes explain, status, review, impact and view through the agent.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: SR-001 titled "add returns the sum of two numbers"
  when: plumb explain how numbers are added runs
  then: it explains SR-001 with the owner's quote and examples
- id: EX-2
  given: two records match "the export" equally
  when: plumb resolve the export runs
  then: both are listed in the owner's terms and none is chosen
- id: EX-3
  given: the installed /plumb command
  when: it is read
  then: it names explain, status, review, impact and view with plain-language arguments
- id: EX-4
  given: SR-002 confirmed by JEV, or by the owner
  when: plumb show SR-002 or plumb explain SR-002 runs, or the vault note is read
  then: it says "confirmed by JEV" or "confirmed by you", never a bare "confirmed"
- id: EX-5
  given: the words "the login thing" naming SR-010
  when: a host (the MCP server, an adapter) calls plumb.present.api resolve, show, explain or impact
  then: resolve's best match is SR-010 and the views answer as the CLI does, writing nothing; brief is
    the plumb brief output
- id: EX-6
  given: the owner's Windows console, where a piped stdout is cp1252 and PYTHONUTF8 is not set
  when: the agent runs plumb status --card, confirm, review, explain, resolve, impact, align or triage
  then: each prints its glyphs (the card's state dot, the confirm card's arrow) as UTF-8 and never fails
    with UnicodeEncodeError
