---
id: SR-309
title: The judge's questions reach the owner whole and once
feature: FEAT-002
statement: 'A judge question shown to the owner has a header of whole words within the question tool''s
  limit, exactly one recommended marker on the recommended option, and either a quote of the owner''s
  words that the question is about, including words found in their past session transcripts quoted with
  their date, or no quote. It is never a question the owner already answered: a recorded decision settles
  it, the question is dropped, and its records wait labelled with that decision for the judge to rejudge
  with it in view.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the judge proposes the header 'Existing validate errors'
  when: the question is shown
  then: the header is whole words within 12 characters, not 'Existing val'
- id: EX-2
  given: the judge labels the recommended option '... (recommended)'
  when: the question is shown
  then: it carries one recommended marker
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner already chose that the hand clean-up lands first
  when: the judge asks it again
  then: the question is dropped as answered and its records wait labelled with that decision
- id: EX-4
  given: a question about pasted commands
  when: it quotes the owner
  then: the quote is the owner's words about pasting, or no quote
- id: EX-5
  given: the owner's words about the question found only in a past session transcript of 4 Oct
  when: the question is shown
  then: it quotes them as "You said on 4 Oct"
proof:
  plan: 'Replaying the 2026-10-17 judge answer: two questions reach the owner with whole headers and one
    marker, and the answered one is dropped.'
  kind: command
