---
id: SR-213
title: Each captured piece is rationalized into one coherent intent set
feature: FEAT-008
statement: 'Every captured piece is rationalized against the recorded intent so the set stays coherent
  on its own and between its elements: an answer that adds to an existing intent widens that intent rather
  than creating a second record, and one that changes or drops older intent is never recorded silently;
  it becomes one intent-conflict question to the owner through the question tool.'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: recorded intent says reminders go by email
  when: the owner answers in the question tool 'also by SMS'
  then: 'the answer is rationalized into the set: the reminder intent now covers email and SMS, not two
    separate records'
- id: EX-2
  given: recorded intent says reminders go by email only
  when: the owner answers 'send them by SMS instead'
  then: 'the change of older intent is not recorded silently: it becomes one intent-conflict question
    to the owner through the question tool'
proof:
  plan: 'Recommended for the owner to decide: in a live session with a recorded reminder intent, the owner
    answers ''also by SMS'' and the status shows one reminder intent covering both; then the owner answers
    ''by SMS instead'' and gets one intent-conflict question.'
  kind: live-session
