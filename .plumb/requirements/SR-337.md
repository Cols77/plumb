id: SR-337
title: The band draws no cards, and Hide stays hidden
statement: plumb's band in Claude Code draws its one status line (with the count of questions waiting,
  read from the session state file), the intent loop's lines and the study lines; it draws no card, no
  card buttons and no list of cards, and the pane shows only the intent loop. Once the owner presses Hide,
  the band stays hidden until they press Show, whatever arrives.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: two questions wait for the owner
  when: the band draws
  then: its line says two await you and no card or button for them is drawn
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner pressed Hide
  when: a turn ends with a new or changed question waiting
  then: the band stays hidden
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner pressed Hide
  when: they press Show
  then: the band draws its line again
proof:
  plan: 'In a live Claude Code session on this repository, the owner sees: no card in plumb''s band and
    Hide staying hidden; every waiting question arriving through the question tool as soon as it is raised,
    grouped by principle, each offering to study it in the room when there is more to see; no question
    the owner already answered coming back; and no turn-end line counting questions (SR-361, SR-363).'
