---
id: SR-359
title: A judge that cannot settle shows red with its exact cause
statement: When a judge cannot settle a record, the band shows a red line naming the judge and the exact
  cause in plain words (not configured, no key, timed out, network down, the provider's error, a quota,
  an unusable answer), until it answers again. A judge that answered but whose question plumb set aside
  is shown apart, naming the question and why; it is never called unreachable.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the JEV key is missing
  when: a record needs judging
  then: 'the band shows red: JEV judge, no key (OPENROUTER_API_KEY not found)'
- id: EX-2
  given: the judge answered with a question plumb cannot use (one option only)
  when: plumb sets it aside
  then: the band says the judge's question was set aside and why, and no line says no judge can be reached
- id: EX-3
  given: the red judge line shows
  when: the judge answers again
  then: the line is gone
proof:
  plan: after landing, with the judge's key removed, the band shows red with the cause; restored, it clears
  kind: live-session
  post_merge: true
