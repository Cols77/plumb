---
id: SR-091
title: A bare "yes" or option letter remembers what it answered
feature: FEAT-007
statement: 'When the owner''s words behind an intent or an example are only a yes or an option letter
  ("yes", "A", "ok"), the record remembers what was answered: the question or the proposal, exactly as
  it was shown. plumb refuses to record a bare answer that does not say what it answered, or that points
  to something never shown. Cards show: you said "A" to: the question or proposal in plain words. Automatic
  confirmation counts a bare yes only when the owner accepted a single proposal whole; picking among options
  never makes the question count as the owner''s own words (ruling R18.1).'
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the reply proposed "reminders go out three days before the deadline" and the owner answered "yes"
  when: the agent records the intent with the owner's "yes" as the answer to that proposal
  then: 'the card shows: you said "yes" to: reminders go out three days before the deadline'
- id: EX-2
  given: any session
  when: the agent records an intent whose only words from the owner are "A", without saying what it answered
  then: plumb refuses it and writes nothing
- id: EX-3
  given: a decision asked "email or SMS?" with options A and B, and the owner answered "A"
  when: automatic confirmation is on and judges the intent
  then: it is not confirmed automatically and waits for the owner
- id: EX-4
  given: the owner said "yes" to one proposal shown whole, automatic confirmation is on, and JEV judges
    that the owner's words support the record
  when: automatic confirmation checks it
  then: the record may be confirmed by JEV
- id: EX-5
  given: a bare answer said to reply to a proposal plumb never showed
  when: the agent records it
  then: it is refused
- id: EX-6
  given: a reply that asks "Question (DEC-026)" with options A, B and C as plain text, no card
  when: the owner answers "ok for A"
  then: the stated set of that reply holds DEC-026, so the agent can relay the answer to DEC-026 without
    the owner naming it
