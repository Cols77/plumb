---
id: SR-292
title: plumb keeps a picture of what the owner understands
feature: FEAT-007
statement: The AI keeps, per project, a picture of what the owner understands of the system and what they
  need to understand, inferred from their prompts, their answers and the studies they open, corrected
  by the 'I know this' and 'Go deeper' buttons on each study; the owner reads and edits it in the browser
  room. No quizzes.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: the owner pressed 'I know this' on a study about the judge cascade
  when: the AI next picks studies
  then: it proposes nothing more on the judge cascade at that depth
- id: EX-2
  given: the owner pressed 'Go deeper' on a study about record links
  when: the AI next picks studies
  then: a deeper study on record links becomes a candidate
- id: EX-3
  given: the owner opens the understanding page in the room
  when: they remove a line saying they understand the hooks
  then: the picture no longer says so and hooks studies become candidates again
- id: EX-4
  given: a fresh project with no picture
  when: the first loop opens
  then: the picture starts from the owner's prompts and answers so far, nothing assumed known
proof:
  plan: After the landing, the owner opens a study with a diagram in the room and sees the diagram drawn,
    scrolling when wide and keeping its size
  kind: live-session
  post_merge: true
