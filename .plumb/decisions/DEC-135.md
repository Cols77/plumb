title: Which Windows creation flags do background plumb processes use?
question: Which Windows creation flags do background plumb processes use?
chosen: CREATE_NO_WINDOW with CREATE_NEW_PROCESS_GROUP, never DETACHED_PROCESS, through one launch helper
  shared by every background process.
alternatives:
- Keep DETACHED_PROCESS and add CREATE_NO_WINDOW to every one of the 50 subprocess calls plumb makes.
- Keep DETACHED_PROCESS and accept the windows.
matters: true
recommended: CREATE_NO_WINDOW with CREATE_NEW_PROCESS_GROUP, never DETACHED_PROCESS, through one launch
  helper shared by every background process.
