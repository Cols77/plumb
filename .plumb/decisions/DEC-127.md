title: What happens to git's merge when a record has an intent conflict?
question: What happens to git's merge when a record has an intent conflict?
chosen: the merge driver completes, leaving the record unconfirmed with the conflict inside it; the merge
  check (the quick merge-time hook and the pre-push hook) holds the merge until the conflict is settled
alternatives:
- the driver stops git's merge until the owner chooses
matters: true

  merge check (the quick merge-time hook and the pre-push hook) holds the merge until the conflict is
  settled
