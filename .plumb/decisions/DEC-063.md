title: How long may a hook run before the watchdog cuts it?
question: How long may a hook run before the watchdog cuts it?
chosen: A configurable hooks.hard_limit_s, defaulting to 6 s, with soft budgets about 4 s; the 2-second
  rule gives way.
alternatives:
- Keep the fixed 1.9 s limit.
- Remove the watchdog.
rationale: Under CPU load every hook ended at 1.9 s with its check skipped; the owner asked to raise the
  timeout.
matters: true
recommended: A configurable hooks.hard_limit_s, defaulting to 6 s, with soft budgets about 4 s; the 2-second
  rule gives way.
