id: DEC-021
title: An intent conflict does not stop git's merge; the boundary holds it
question: What happens to git's merge when a record has an intent conflict?
chosen: the merge completes with the record unconfirmed and carrying the conflict; plumb check --merge
  and the optional pre-merge-commit hook hold it until the owner chooses
alternatives:
- the driver stops git's merge until the owner chooses
rationale: R10.3 shows the conflict at the merge boundary; parallel work goes on, and nothing unconfirmed
  passes the boundary.
