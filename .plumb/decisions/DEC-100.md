question: How should calibration treat a judge-approved record the owner reverses?
chosen: Re-formalize always; the old version counts as a calibration 'no' only when the owner says it
  misread their words; an intent change gets no label.
alternatives:
- Re-formalize always and never label the old version
- Every reversal counts as a calibration 'no'
matters: true
recommended: Re-formalize always; the old version counts as a calibration 'no' only when the owner says
  it misread their words; an intent change gets no label.
