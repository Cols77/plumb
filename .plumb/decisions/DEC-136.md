title: How long may a hook run before it is cut?
question: How long may a hook run before it is cut?
chosen: Watchdog hard limit 30 s; soft budgets 20 s (session start, prompt, stop) and 5 s (post-edit);
  covering tests 60 s per turn; judge in a hook 5 s; git in the stop hook 5 s; host timeouts 60 s and
  120 s, kept above the hard limit plus the stop grace. All are settings the owner may raise by asking.
alternatives:
- 6 s hard limit with 4 s soft budgets, accepting skipped checks.
- Remove the watchdog and rely on the host timeouts alone.
matters: true
