title: 'Build order: a contracts phase alone, then five lanes in parallel'
question: How are sub-projects 2 to 5 and the importer completion built?
chosen: The coordinator first writes every cross-lane signature, data class, route, config key and stub
  in a contracts phase on main; then CAPTURE, MERGE, HOSTS, PRESENT and IMPORT run in parallel on disjoint
  files.
alternatives:
- The sub-projects one after another, in roadmap order.
- Five lanes with no contracts phase, each defining what it needs.
rationale: Disjoint files and frozen interfaces let five lanes work at once without waiting on each other,
  as the CORE-first order did for sub-project 1.
