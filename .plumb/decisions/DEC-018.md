title: Calibration passes only with enough items and negatives and no false accept
question: When does a calibration pass for auto-approval?
chosen: 'Per claim kind: at least 20 labelled items, at least 5 negatives (synthetic perturbations count
  up to half), a false-accept rate of 0 at the chosen threshold, and at least half the positives accepted.'
alternatives:
- A fixed 0.9 threshold without measurement.
- Precision of at least 0.95 with any number of items.
rationale: R12.1 asks for accuracy measured on the owner's own decisions; a project rarely has many revocations,
  so deterministic perturbations supply negatives, and no false accept keeps auto-approval narrow.
