id: DEC-077
title: Which Windows creation flags do background plumb processes use?
question: Which Windows creation flags do background plumb processes use?
chosen: CREATE_NO_WINDOW with CREATE_NEW_PROCESS_GROUP, never DETACHED_PROCESS.
alternatives:
- Keep DETACHED_PROCESS and add CREATE_NO_WINDOW to every one of the 50 subprocess calls plumb makes.
- Keep DETACHED_PROCESS and accept the windows.
rationale: A probe on 2026-10-16 showed DETACHED_PROCESS forces a visible console on the child and its
  grandchildren whatever else is set, while CREATE_NO_WINDOW alone or with a new process group hides both
  and the child still outlives its parent.
matters: true
recommended: CREATE_NO_WINDOW with CREATE_NEW_PROCESS_GROUP, never DETACHED_PROCESS.
