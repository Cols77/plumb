title: The grouped one-line confirmation names every record and pins exact hashes
question: How can one "ok" confirm a triage group whose records were not each shown in full?
chosen: The one-line offer names every record of the group by ID; the stated set holds the group with
  its hashes; plumb relay --group approves exactly that group, by agent-relayed, after the R2 quote checks.
alternatives:
- Show every record of the group in full before an ok counts.
- Only plumb triage --approve in the owner's terminal.
rationale: The owner chose the grouped one-line confirmation explicitly (R8 A); naming the IDs and pinning
  the hashes keeps the owner's words tied to an exact, inspectable set.
