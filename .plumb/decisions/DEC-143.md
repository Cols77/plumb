title: Does the fast track need a passing calibration?
question: Does the fast track need a passing calibration?
chosen: 'No: the owner''s switch and JEV strictly above 0.9 suffice. A passing calibration of the same
  JEV version and question form only raises the floor to its threshold; a calibration that is missing,
  not passing, of another version or of another form is ignored, and nothing is suspended.'
alternatives:
- Mixed-evidence calibration must pass first and a JEV version change suspends the fast track (DEC-120).
- Generated flips alone may count as the 'no' examples, so a live calibration can pass without real reversals.
rationale: 'The owner on 2026-10-17: ''I''m sure that''s fine to go with that. let''s progress'', answering
  ''can''t we just go with the threshold of 0.9?''. No real ''no'' labels exist (revoke is gone), so a
  mixed-evidence calibration could not pass.'
matters: true
recommended: Generated flips alone may count as the 'no' examples, so a live calibration can pass without
  real reversals.
