title: Logs are two JSONL streams
question: How are the append-only logs stored?
chosen: Two hash-chained JSONL streams, .plumb/log/events.jsonl and evidence.jsonl, with merge=union in
  git.
alternatives:
- One JSON file per log entry under .plumb/log/<kind>/ (concept 4.7).
rationale: One file read per hook instead of hundreds of small files on Windows; same per-entry digest,
  prev chain and append-only check.
